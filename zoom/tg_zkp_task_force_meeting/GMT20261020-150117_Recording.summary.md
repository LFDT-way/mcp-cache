## Details

### VTC Research Collaboration Plans

Mitchell and Scott held a brief meeting with only two participants due to Dennis's absence. They discussed Mitchell's recent travel to DC and his visit to the Capitol building. Mitchell thanked Scott for his contributions to a previous meeting and mentioned that Scott had been accepted as a fellow mage in Major City. Mitchell explained his plans to use the Verifiable Trust Community (VTC) as a gateway for revealing evidence in agentic auto-research, particularly for cryptography and math problems, and described his vision for Major City as a trusted environment for research collaboration.

### Linux Plumbers Conference Updates

Scott provided an update on Linux Plumbers conference insights, sharing that kernel-level validation was received for the approach, with ZKP layer confirmed as core and gradual adoption planned across Linux Foundation projects over the next 6 months. Mitchell discussed his work on joining a verifiable trust community without using keyrings, specifically working on connecting as "majors.city.vta" rather than through a wallet-based keyring system. Mitchell mentioned he was experiencing some technical issues with this approach and was working to resolve them.

### VTC Admin Portal Development Update

Mitchell demonstrated his work on the VTC admin portal, including a handmade browser extension for managing verifiable trust agents and a system for storing memories to help with document access. He discussed ongoing challenges with connecting the system in a self-sovereign way and mentioned recent updates including PR18 and PR29, as well as new security considerations for post-quantum cryptography that he added to the ZKP spec document. Mitchell also announced that the ZKP implementation guide has been deployed on pages as a user guide for requesting and implementing ZKP constructions.

### OpenVTC PRs and Nullifiers Discussion

Mitchell discussed recent PRs and contributions related to scoped nullifiers and proof of authorship systems using OpenVTC. He mentioned a PR from D. Condrey regarding a system called Writer's Logic, which was previously discussed in the ZKP task force. Mitchell indicated he would review the PRs asynchronously and potentially pull them, noting that the discussions could continue in the Trust Task repo.

### Cross-Spec Vetting and Trust Discussion

Mitchell discussed his work on cross-spec review of the vetting card in the VDS spec, explaining the concept of CAI attestation and its zero-knowledge proof mechanism. He mentioned some open items related to runtime evidence and trusted execution environments (TEEs), noting that TEEs are approximately $200 per month. Vikas joined the meeting to share insights from a recent BIGIN conference, where he observed various firms developing trust-related products and noted the growing focus on trust in the industry.
