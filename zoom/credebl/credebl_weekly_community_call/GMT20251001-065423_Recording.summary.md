Rinkal explained the implementation of OID4 VC, discussing different types of VC formats and the option to issue credentials using X509 certificates. She highlighted that for MDoc, it's mandatory to use X509 certificates, while for SDJORT, there are two options: X509 certificates or DID methods. Ajay emphasized the need to manage certificates at the organization level, allowing issuers to import or create self-signed certificates, and discussed the challenge of distributing intermediate and root CAs at the ecosystem level for validating certificates.
