They discussed changes to the Creddo Controller, including the removal of duplicate and conflicting routes. Tipu presented a rough sketch for a YD 4 GC discussion, but the transcript ended before he could provide further details.

### OID Pharmacy Issuer Configuration

Tipu and Rinkal discussed the process of creating an issuer for OID pharmacy issues, focusing on the configuration requirements. They explored two main flows: pre-authentication and authentication using IDPs like Keycloak. Tipu explained the necessary configurations for creating an issuer, including the issuer ID, display object, and credential configuration. Rinkal emphasized the importance of validating the IDP URL and client ID/secret, as well as gathering information about the desired format of VCs from organizations. They also discussed the need to specify scopes and claims for the issuer.

### Issuer Creation and Authentication Flows

Tipu explained the process of creating an issuer and offer in their platform, including the necessary steps and information required for each. He outlined the IDP and pre-authentication flows, detailing how users authenticate and request credentials. Tipu also mentioned the need to integrate these flows with their incredible platform, and he planned to review the APIs involved to determine how to implement this integration.

### API Prefixing for DID and OID Flows

Rinkal discussed the need to support both DID:com and OID:4 VC flows in the platform by distinguishing APIs based on their flow type. They agreed to prefix DID:com-related APIs with "/didcom" and OID:4 VC-related APIs with "/oid4vc" to facilitate organization onboarding. Rinkal emphasized that while the existing onboarding process would remain unchanged, additional flows for OID:4 VC would be implemented. Tipu and Sai confirmed their understanding, with Sai raising a question about the authorization flow configuration, which Rinkal noted would be addressed later.

### IDP User Registration Process Discussion

The team discussed the process of user registration and authentication for holders using IDPs. Rinkal explained that if organizations have their own IDPs, they can provide their own configurations, but currently, only Keycloak is supported. Sai suggested that holders should be registered in Credible, and Rinkal agreed, mentioning the need for a registration method for Keycloak users. They also discussed the possibility of creating offers that require registration via a specific URL. Amit clarified that once an IDP URL is configured for authentication, users will be redirected to the IDP's specified URL after authentication.

### IDP Integration and Authentication Flows

The team discussed IDP integration and authentication flows, particularly focusing on Keycloak and Google login options. Rinkal explained that custom mappers are currently required for Keycloak to intercept requests and modify claims in the token, and these mappers would likely be needed for other IDPs as well. Amit noted that other IDPs are also working on supporting similar flows, and these mappers might become default in future IDP implementations. The team agreed to seek input from the Credeo team about the IDP process and decided to continue discussions in the next meeting to make further progress.
