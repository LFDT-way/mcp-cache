### Kubernetes VTI Deployment System

Geoff and Vincent demonstrated a Kubernetes-based deployment system for the VTI stack that allows users to easily deploy VTAs at scale. The system offers two options: full stack deployment for complete control and VTA-only deployment using shared platform services. The infrastructure runs on a three-node Kubernetes cluster costing $30 monthly and can comfortably support 2-3 platform stacks plus 120 individual VTAs. The system uses HashiCorp Vault for secret management, with security levels ranging from development environments with plain text secrets to future TEE-based implementations. The team plans to make this open source and will demonstrate it at upcoming conferences including the Plumbers Conference and Open Source Summit.
