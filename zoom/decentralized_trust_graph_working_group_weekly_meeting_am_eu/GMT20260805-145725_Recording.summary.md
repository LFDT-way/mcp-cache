### Trustworthy AI and Privacy Focus

The group discussed their mission around securing confidential data and privacy-preserving AI in future work. ### Agentic AI Focus Group Launch

Grace announced the creation of a new ITU focus group on Agentic AI and identity, which will include multiple working groups and public discussions, with the next in-person meeting scheduled for December in Paris. Martina shared information about upcoming Internet Identity Workshop and Agentic Internet Workshop events in November, noting that early bird registration ends soon for the Internet Identity Workshop. The meeting followed a relaxed agenda due to holidays and travel, with plans to discuss capacity and incapacitation issues later in the session.

### Risk and Credential Specifications Progress

Nicky discussed progress on risk assessment and harms prevention specifications, targeting completion by October 3rd for the Linux Builders conference, with a focus on applying guardrails and assurance tests to ensure human-centered design. Geoff and Brendan highlighted ongoing work on credential specifications, including recent PRs submitted by Brendan based on Sankarshan's issues, with a particular focus on a ZKP-related fifth issue that Mitchell agreed to review. The team emphasized the importance of community input through issues and pull requests to refine the specifications, particularly given their heavy use in Linux kernel solutions.

### Specifications and ZKP Task Progress

The team discussed progress on three specifications, with Nicky sharing links to the Trust Task Task Force GitHub and spec document for review. Brendan reported that Drummond had provided comments on his submitted PR, particularly regarding the connection between the credential spec and trust task spec, with both specifications needing to be developed in parallel. Scott updated the group on the ZKP task force's second working call, where two new members joined and the team reached a working position on the issuer-verifier collusion question, while Mitchell stood up an experimental lab with real ZK circuits for testing.

### Interactive Trust Database Presentation

Daniel presented a new interactive database for collecting and sharing best practices in human trust experience, which is publicly accessible and community-built. The database is tagged to a defined customer journey and trust moments, allowing filtering and classification of solutions. Daniel explained that while the tool currently uses an initial algorithm for classification, future improvements will include a more sophisticated rating system and the ability to incorporate scientific findings and KPIs. Nicky congratulated Daniel on the quick delivery of this task force deliverable.

### Decentralized Network User Experience Discussion

Reza discussed a project involving a decentralized peer-to-peer network with command line interfaces and expressed interest in using Daniel's demonstrated tool to design a better user experience and journey. Daniel noted that the current content in the tool is limited but suggested that the audience could find valuable insights. Reza indicated he might follow up with Daniel offline to explore the tool further, and Nicky emphasized the importance of contributing content and considering various user metrics and feedback in the design process.

### Credential Specification Inclusivity Challenges

Nicky presented challenges in the current credential specification regarding people with limited capacity or legal guardians, using examples like Mia and Arthur to illustrate issues with the current system. She recommended addressing these gaps in the specification to ensure inclusivity and maintain the relational model of the Trust Graph. Brendan suggested exploring ways to model these structures using existing credentials and witness credentials from official organizations, while Mitchell noted that the Trust Graph technology could naturally solve these problems over time as users build relationships within the network. The group agreed to continue the discussion in their GitHub repository to explore potential solutions within the existing specification.
