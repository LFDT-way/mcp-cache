### Decentralized Trust Agent Architecture

The meeting focused on the architecture and functionality of verifiable trust agents (VTAs) in decentralized trust networks. Drummond explained that VTAs represent DTG nodes, whether they are individuals, communities, AI agents, or devices, and have associated DID documents for interaction. Brendan detailed the technical architecture, including personal network managers, community network managers, and multiple VTAs for segmentation, with core functionalities like DID and key management, backup, and consent management. Michael raised questions about trust anchor election and governance, which Drummond clarified is determined by each community. The group discussed the flexibility of VTCs, including self-organization and nesting, as well as the potential for AI agents and devices to participate in decentralized trust graphs. Adam inquired about the representation of multiple devices and personas, which Drummond explained is managed through a set of DIDs and can be extended to apps on devices. Jorge asked about offline communication between individuals, which Drummond confirmed is possible through direct mesh networking or QR codes. The conversation ended with Reza announcing Drummond's keynote speaking engagement at the Southern California Linux Expo on March 5th.
