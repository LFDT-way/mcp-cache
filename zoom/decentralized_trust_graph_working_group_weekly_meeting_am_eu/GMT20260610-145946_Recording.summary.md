## Details

### Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group

Drummond opened the June 10th Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group meeting and welcomed attendees, noting the joint nature of the group with TrustRIP and DIF. The agenda included checking in on seven task forces and discussing two special topics: Martina's interaction architecture work and DTG credential specs. The meeting began with announcements, including Carly's demonstration of Key ring at the Ecosystem Governance Founding Working Group meeting and Reza's mention of the AGI 26 conference in San Francisco from July 27-30.

### Task Force Updates and Progress

Drummond provided updates on several task forces within the working group, noting that there are currently seven task forces with potential for an eighth by month's end. He discussed the .agent domain initiative led by Balazs Lamenti, where TrustRIP was asked to endorse the community application, and reported on progress with the ZKP task force, including collaboration with Ciro's organization and the Berkeley team working on ZKP for decentralized trust graphs.

### UX Task Force and Trust Graph

Martina announced progress on creating a UX Task Force and invited interested participants to join or take leadership roles, though she will not serve as the main chair. Drummond clarified that task forces operate independently with flexibility in meeting structures and organization methods. Martina then provided an introduction to the Decentralized Trust Graph project, explaining its evolution from the Web of Trust concept and describing how different entities like people, communities, agents, and devices are mapped onto the graph through VTA (Verifiable Trust Agent) infrastructure.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Architecture Overview

Martina presented an architecture overview of the decentralized trust graph, defining seven layers that provide structure while allowing for flexibility in scaling from individual actors to communities and networks. She discussed the importance of custodial patterns for offline or disconnected citizens, though Nicky raised concerns about potentially confusing terminology with blockchain concepts. The presentation covered different types of credentials including relationship credentials, membership credentials, and annotation credentials, with Martina clarifying that these are not protocol layers but different architectural perspectives. The discussion concluded with Drummond requesting access to the shared GitHub page containing the architecture overview and acronyms.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Governance Layers

The group discussed the distinction between trust tasks, credentials, and artifacts in the decentralized trust graph system. Brendan proposed three layers of governance for trust tasks and credentials, suggesting that core specifications should remain small while allowing for community-blessed extensions and decentralized implementation through DIDs. Nicky introduced a third category of "signal data" that functions within the VTA ecosystem, while Drummond and others emphasized the need to clarify the relationship between trust tasks, ceremonies, and credentials. The team agreed to review trust tasks at trusttask.org and planned a follow-up meeting with Glenn to further discuss the layering of protocols and ceremonies.
