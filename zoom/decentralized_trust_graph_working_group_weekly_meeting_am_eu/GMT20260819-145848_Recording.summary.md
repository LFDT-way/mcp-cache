### GDC 26 FIDUS Use Case Promotion

Daniel requested votes for a GDC 26 FIDUS use case promotion focused on AI fairness and responsible AI, which includes verifiable credentials and explainable AI capabilities. Drummond announced that tickets are still available for the Global Digital Collaboration event and mentioned a First Person Meetup in Geneva hosted by First Person Project and partners. The meeting also noted a new paper from the Singapore AI Safety Hub on Designing Agent IDs, which will be discussed in a special topic later.

### Credential Task Force Progress Update

Brendan provided an update on the credential task force, noting increased activity with weekly calls and the implementation of semantic versioning for coordinating the credential spec and trust task spec. He highlighted recent progress on multi-trust task flows and verifiable delegation credentials, emphasizing the importance of distinguishing between delegation and authorization. The task force has also updated the credential task force repo to point to the official spec repo.

### Rust Crate Contribution Discussion

Reza proposed contributing a Rust crate for trust tasks in P2P infrastructure to the existing codebase rather than forking the code. Steve clarified that Reza's work might overlap with existing trust spanning protocol work by Wenjing and Sam at Cary. The group agreed this approach would be preferable to forking, and Reza committed to following up with Glenn and the team about specific details. Drummond announced that Tuesday mornings now have three contiguous time slots for task force meetings covering editors, ZKP, and HTX topics.

### Spec Repositories and ZKP Progress

Drummond announced the creation of dedicated repositories for all specs, with an index to be maintained on the main wiki and GitHub page. Scott reported progress on the ST ZKP task force, including welcoming David Condry from Writer's Logic as a new member, graduating requirements to V0.4, and successful verification registry testing by Glenn Gore. Drummond committed to introducing Scott and Mitchell to the Berkeley team, specifically Dr. Sanjim, Garg, and Hart.

### HTX Experience Analysis Approach

Daniel presented HTX's human-centered experience analysis approach, which uses a structured framework to examine scenarios through different lenses including personas, journeys, and interactions. Drummond shared insights from his recent trip to New Zealand and Melbourne, where he discussed verifiable trust infrastructure and the Decentralized Trust Graph with local contributors. Drummond mentioned he would share links to a podcast recorded with John Phillips and Joe Spencer, as well as his presentation about the "Sovereign Stack" at the Digital Identity New Zealand conference.

### Sovereign Stack and VTI Updates

Drummond presented updates on the Sovereign Stack and Verifiable Trust Infrastructure (VTI), sharing insights from recent discussions with Digital Identity New Zealand and potential opportunities in Australia and New Zealand, including interest from Maori leaders. Glenn discussed a proposed new credential type for verifiable delegation and authority, highlighting the complexity of the topic and the need for further discussion on implementation details. The group also discussed plans for GDC, including alignment between VTI and EU digital identity standards, and Kalia raised the need for a comprehensive paper mapping pragmatic solutions for agent-human connections, with Glenn, Steve, and Mitchell volunteering to contribute to this effort.
