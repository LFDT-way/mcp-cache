## Details

### Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group

The meeting began with introductions and welcoming new members to the Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group, which is a joint call of Trust Over IP and the Decentralized Identity Foundation. Art introduced himself as a maintainer of the Personal Data Portability Protocol (PDP) Working Group and expressed interest in the crossover between his work and the current group's efforts. Martina encouraged new participants to complete their bio information for easier identification, and noted there were no other official announcements at this time.

### VPK Workgroup Kickoff Meeting

Reza announced the kickoff of the Virtual Private Knowledge (VPK) Workgroup under Decentralized Trust, focusing on securing data sent to third-party AI systems through homomorphic encryption. The workgroup has launched two task forces: one for standardizing specifications and protocols, and another for exploring personalized embedders. Reza clarified that while homomorphic encryption has improved in speed and usability, the current scope is limited to confidential vector search, which addresses 99% of the problem. The group meets Mondays at 9 AM Pacific, and Reza agreed to provide a web link for the agenda.

### V2 Development and Credential Updates

The team discussed progress on two main initiatives. Sankarshan and Nicky reported on V2 development, explaining that while significant work remains, they have a plan to move forward with V3 that will create an operational tool for standards implementation and governance. Geoff updated the group on the credential spec, which was bumped to version 0.6 and includes a new TOIP registry, with plans to publish an implementer's draft by late October ahead of IIW in early November. The team confirmed their next meeting will be in two weeks, and Brendan mentioned alignment work is underway for the Prague demo.

### Linux Plumbers Conference Preparations

The meeting covered updates from several task forces and preparations for the upcoming Linux Plumbers Conference. Mitchell reported that the ZKP Working Group received endorsement from LEIF regarding their approach to trade-offs and timeline for implementing ZKP solutions. The team demonstrated progress on predicate credential systems and is preparing for demonstrations at Plumbers Conference next week, including a 3.5-hour workshop. Glenn provided an update on the VTA and VTC development, noting significant progress but expressing concerns about AI costs and integration challenges. The team discussed terminology challenges, with Drummond proposing to use "Trust Agent" as a core term, though Steve suggested keeping "V Trust Agent" to maintain brand identity. The group decided to leave next week's meeting on the calendar to potentially provide real-time updates on the Plumbers Conference presentations.
