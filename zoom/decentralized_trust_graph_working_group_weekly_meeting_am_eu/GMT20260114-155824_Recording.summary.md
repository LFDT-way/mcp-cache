### Decentralized Trust Graph Credentials Review

The meeting focused on reviewing the Decentralized Trust Graph (DTG) credentials specification and related concepts. Brendan presented an overview of six credential types and one verifiable data structure, explaining their purposes and relationships. The group discussed questions about membership credentials versus relationship credentials, and concerns were raised about potential misuse of invitation credentials. Drummond clarified the differences between identity verification credentials and DTG credentials. The team agreed to start a new discussion about VC3 in the credentials repo. Jonathan suggested creating a follow-up document defining what constitutes an organization in the network. Margaret raised concerns about the burden of proof for unwanted membership. The group set three main objectives for Q1: moving the glossary into a formal format, advancing the DTG credentials specification to an implementer's draft, and developing requirements for zero-knowledge proof requirements. Reza suggested creating diagrams to explain the concepts to a layperson audience. The team will continue discussions on the repo and meet again next week to address questions and move the specs forward.
