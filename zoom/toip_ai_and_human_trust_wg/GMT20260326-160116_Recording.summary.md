### Accountability and Authority Definitions

The team discussed the definition and distinction between accountability and authority, with Wenjing explaining that accountability refers to rules that are not automatically enforced but can be addressed after the fact, while authority involves strict enforcement. Drummond suggested classifying accountability into machine-enforceable and human-enforceable categories, which Wenjing agreed could be useful subclasses. The group also discussed the importance of auditability for accountability and noted that these definitions are intended for informational purposes within the specification rather than for normative use in other contexts.

### AI Agent Architecture Changes

Tim expressed concerns about the rapid changes in AI agent architecture over the past few months and questioned how relevant current work is to the new agent systems being developed. Wenjing acknowledged the rapid changes in agents and agreed to address both authority and delegation of authority in their coverage, while Haley suggested focusing on authority and delegation of authority due to related issues with authority hopping and deputy confusion. Drummond clarified the distinction between machine-readable policy and human-readable policy, noting that machine policy is designed for enforcement purposes.

### Policy Enforcement Framework Discussion

The group discussed the distinction between machine-enforceable and human-enforceable policies, with Wenjing suggesting that "machine enforceable policy and human enforceable policy" might be a better distinction than "machine readable and human readable." Tim explained that courts focus on control and ownership rather than the specific mechanisms, emphasizing the importance of establishing who was in control. The team reviewed a framework diagram showing various entities and their interactions, with Tim adding that the dotted line represents the control boundary. Steve proposed starting a discussion on fiduciary duty frameworks within the task force, and the group agreed to make this a standing agenda item for future meetings.
