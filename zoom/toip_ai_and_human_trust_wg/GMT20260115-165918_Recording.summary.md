### Trust Credentials and AI Security

The group discussed potential collaboration with Drummond's Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group, agreeing to focus on general-purpose trust credentials rather than first-person projects to avoid overlap. They explored the challenges of AI agent security, particularly the vulnerability to malicious prompts, and discussed the need to move beyond data management to behavior management. The team agreed to survey relevant papers on this topic and potentially schedule a dedicated section in future meetings to discuss it further. They also briefly touched on the need for people to indicate their interest in working groups by adding their names to a shared document.

### Personal AI Privacy Solutions

The group discussed data filtering and privacy concerns in personal AI systems, with Neil explaining the need for specialized agents to manage personal information and transactions. Wenjing shared insights from a Google paper on rephrasing prompts to remove personal identifiers, while Steve presented his vision for digital twins, which includes an externally-facing agent separated from an internally-facing digital twin to protect private data. The team agreed to review and update their plan at the next meeting, with an emphasis on surveying existing approaches to these challenges before developing more organized solutions.
