## Overview

Darrell and Sankarshan discussed the role and responsibilities of the Trust Registry Task Force (TRTF) regarding the development and promotion of the Trust Registry Query Protocol (TRQP). They agreed that while TRTF should provide guidance and documentation for extensions like discovery and lifecycle management, it is not the group's primary role to build business implementations or reference implementations. ## Details

### Trust Registry Task Force Challenges

Darrell and Sankarshan discussed the challenges with the Trust Registry Task Force, noting low attendance and capacity issues due to volunteers being spread thin. They agreed that while the task force's goal of demonstrating the specification's business value is important, it may not be the appropriate forum for achieving this, as the providers of trust registry capabilities should be leading the outreach efforts. Darrell mentioned he would have a one-on-one meeting with Scott to discuss the situation further.

### Trust Registry Task Force Discussion

Darrell and Sankarshan discussed the role of the Trust Registry Task Force and the Trust Over IP Foundation, with Sankarshan expressing concerns about the group's focus on developing specifications rather than championing initiatives. Darrell argued for the importance of having an organization like IRA serve as a "glue" among different market segments, including both commercial and non-commercial stakeholders, to help identify and address common needs. The conversation concluded with Darrell mentioning IRA's work on the Discovery Registry of Registries, though he noted this effort was not yet complete.

### TRQP Reference Implementation Strategy

Darrell and Sankarshan discussed the approach to implementing a reference implementation for the TRQP extension. Sankarshan emphasized that it should be a business initiative driven by a legal entity capable of handling contracts, rather than Trust Over IP or IRA building it themselves. They agreed that Trust4IP's role should be to provide guidance and lessons rather than building implementations in the field, with business entities contributing back to the standards. Darrell noted that while the technical work would be relatively straightforward, there are challenges with volunteer engagement and business development efforts needed to drive the implementation forward.

### TRQP Topics Practical Evaluation Proposal

Sankarshan proposed doing a practical evaluation of 6-7 TRQP topics, focusing particularly on Discovery which he identified as a mature area that could be extended without disturbing the core system. He suggested using a table to assess how the core would be impacted and what would become optional. Darrell agreed to review the materials and confirmed that the foundation would be willing to contribute the IRA profile extension back, as it serves specific partner business value.

### Trust Registry Query Protocol Extensions

Darrell and Sankarshan discussed the need for a Trust Registry Query Protocol (TRQP) version 3 or a separate trust registry discovery protocol. They agreed that discovery and registry of registries should be considered as extensions to TRQP rather than separate protocols, with the first implementation serving as a reference for future extensions. Sankarshan emphasized the importance of formally accepting this extension concept within TRQP to facilitate discussions about TRQP in future grid design work.

### TRQP Approval Process Delays

Sankarshan identified delays in the TRQP approval process and noted that similar trust registry work is being done by other organizations, including the Linux Foundation's AGENTCY project. He explained that initial decisions to defer governance questions to a working group never materialized as planned, creating issues that are now resurfacing. Darrell acknowledged these concerns and mentioned he would create a wiki page to document and expand on the discussion about where similar trust registry work is being conducted.

### Metadata Guidance for Trust Registries

Darrell and Sankarshan discussed approaches to handling metadata work for trust registries, agreeing to focus on creating lightweight guidance documents rather than complex standards-track pieces. They aligned on tackling specific areas including lifecycle management and discovery mechanisms, with Sankarshan planning to work on lifecycle semantics after returning from a high-altitude driving trip in mid-June, while Darrell will handle the discovery aspect and governance metadata components. The discussion emphasized the importance of providing practical solutions that can be easily adopted by implementers rather than pursuing extensive formal standardization processes.
