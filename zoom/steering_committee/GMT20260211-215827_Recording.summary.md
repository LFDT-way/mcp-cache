### DOIs for Carey Suite Specifications

The group discussed using DOIs to provide persistent identifiers for the three specifications being finalized by the Carey Suite working group. Carly explained that the specifications would be pre-registered to receive DOIs from CERN's Zenodo repository, which would provide permanent URLs and metadata backup. The group agreed this was a suitable solution for addressing broken URLs in existing specifications. Scott mentioned that DOI registration agents are involved in the Creator Assertions Working Group, highlighting the importance of persistent identifiers in content authenticity. Carly and Nico agreed to collaborate on a blog post to accompany the release of the specifications.

Karla mentioned her experience with ISO and offered to provide guidance. The team also discussed creating a template for specifications using SpecupT, learning from Carrie's work. They considered the benefits of getting specifications recognized as ISO standards for greater credibility and reach. Makki suggested aligning internal messaging and positioning Carry for growth to support these efforts. The conversation ended with a brief mention of an invitation-only Human-to-Human Summit on Human Agency.
