### Verona Trust Registry Governance Structure

Fabrice presented Verona, a registry of trust registries that allows ecosystems to create their own governance frameworks and credential schemas. He outlined the proposed governance structure for the Verona Council, which would be a non-profit Swiss association with a small board operating on a one-member-one-vote basis, emphasizing neutrality, equality, and transparency. The council's role would focus on governing and securing the network infrastructure while maintaining separation from individual ecosystem governance. Fabrice noted that the council selection process is just beginning with some interested candidates, and the governance framework is still pending final definition, seeking feedback from the audience.

### Verona Token System Discussion

Fabrice explained Verona's business model, which relies on a token system where 5% of transaction fees fund trust deposits that pay network validators. He clarified that ecosystems can set their own pricing in fiat currency, with the token system primarily used for trust deposits rather than direct transaction settlement. Scott raised concerns about adoption challenges, noting that trust registry concepts take significant time to implement and questioning the lack of major champions supporting Verona's approach. The group discussed potential collaboration opportunities, with Drummond confirming Verona's compatibility with the Trust Registry of Registries (TRQP) protocol and offering to share the presentation on the LFDT blog for wider visibility.
