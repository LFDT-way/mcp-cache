## Details

### Indy Contributors Working Group Update

The Indy Contributors Working Group meeting discussed several key topics. Char presented a draft of the Indy Quarterly Report and requested feedback. The group also addressed the Indy upgrade and the Github organization. Wade mentioned that Sylvan had inquired about updates on the Indy upgrade channel, and there was no official update on the Quebec team's return. Char announced that the Indy test net would be reset in one week. The meeting was recorded and would be posted on the meeting page later that day.

### Sovereign Mainnet Historical Preservation

Wade explained that Riley has taken on the role of caretaker for the Sovereign Mainnet, which continues to exist in a read-only format as a historical artifact. The Mainnet is currently running on Digital Ocean with four nodes and Indy scan, and is being monitored 24/7. Char confirmed that the testnet and staging networks are completely gone, and Renata asked about the reasons behind maintaining the Mainnet, to which Wade clarified that it's being kept alive due to its historical significance to Riley and the origins of self-sovereign identity.

### Indy Quarterly Report Status Update

The team discussed the Indy Quarterly report, which is due Thursday, and confirmed the project remains stable in production with an ongoing upgrade as the main focus. Wade raised concerns about maintaining long-term support for Indy, suggesting it's currently in final beta stages with good MVP quality but no final releases yet. Renata shared positive news about Brazil's government potentially moving towards production use of Indy for identity features, while also noting that Indy is being used by various organizations worldwide, though its long-term future remains uncertain.

### Indy 2.22.0 Upgrade Status Review

The team discussed the status of the Indy 2.22.0 upgrade, noting that new contributors were abruptly removed from the project, slowing momentum. Wade expressed hope to allocate additional time to complete the upgrade, while Char mentioned internal discussions at Indyco about contributing to move the project forward. The team also reviewed the Github organization change, which will be implemented at a favorable time after removing obsolete dependencies. They noted that the project remains stable with no new features needed, and discussed increasing the OpenSSF scorecard rating, pending the outcome of the upgrade and organization change. Renata inquired about Bhutan's use of Indy projects, but the team could not confirm if they still use Anoncreds or have switched to other systems.

### Indy Project Report Finalization

The team discussed finalizing a report, emphasizing the stability and continued use of the Indy project despite slower contributions. They agreed to highlight specific use cases and include a paragraph about Indy's ongoing activity. Wade suggested linking recent work on the Indy DCO side to a PR to engage more team members and facilitate review. Char agreed to follow up with Kim about this.

### Ubuntu Upgrade and Indy Testnet

Kim reported that tests are passing except for an issue with Ubuntu 22.04 in production, which broke when upgrading to the latest version. Wade suggested posting the code in Discord or submitting a PR for further review. Kim explained that reverting to older dependencies was a temporary measure while the upgrade process continues, and Wade agreed to create a new branch for dependency updates. Lynn announced that the Indy Testnet will be reset next Tuesday, but old transactions will remain accessible through Indy Scan for at least a year. Char will finalize the meeting notes and submit them the following afternoon.
