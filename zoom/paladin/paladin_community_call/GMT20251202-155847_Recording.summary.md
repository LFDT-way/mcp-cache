### Paladin V1 Release Planning Update

The meeting focused on the V1 project board and the structure of the Paladin organization, which includes four repositories: Paladin, Zeto, and two GitHub repositories for governance and LFDT artifacts. Matthew Whitehead provided an update on Paladin's development, highlighting regular releases and the upcoming V1 major release, which will include significant improvements to the distributed sequencer. Suzanne suggested including an overview of Paladin's functionality in future community calls to help non-technical users understand the product's maturity and potential. The group discussed sharing existing educational materials, including a YouTube playlist and the Paladinprivacy.org website, to support community understanding of the project.

### Paladin Documentation and Resources Overview

The meeting focused on introducing Paladin's documentation and resources, including a comprehensive main doc site, YouTube playlist, and Discord channels. Matthew Whitehead highlighted Paladin's capabilities, such as zero-knowledge base tokens (Zeto) and notarized tokens (NOTO), and discussed the project's support for various base ledgers like Ethereum and Polygon. He emphasized the importance of making Paladin user-friendly, mentioning the getting started guide, tutorials, and a contributing page for those interested in contributing to the project. The group considered running demos in future calls to provide a better understanding of Paladin's functionality.

### Paladin Development Progress Overview

Matthew Whitehead provided an overview of Paladin's development progress, noting that the focus is currently on stability, performance, and reliability for version 1. He mentioned that the next meeting might be rescheduled due to the Christmas holidays, and he and Anna will prepare more material for the following call. The team aims to move closer to discussing V1 properly in the next meeting.
