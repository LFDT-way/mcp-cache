### Struct Field Name Printing Solution

Alston demonstrates a solution for printing nested structs with field names rather than just field numbers by modifying the toString method in the DynamicStruct class. He shows how his implementation uses reflection to get field names and handles nested structs recursively through an unwrap method. Angelina explains her original approach involved creating a new struct class extending DynamicStruct, but Alston suggests this isn't necessary since the toString method in DynamicStruct is sufficient. George agrees with Alston's approach as it avoids adding too much code to the wrapper while still making the toString method available when called on structs.
