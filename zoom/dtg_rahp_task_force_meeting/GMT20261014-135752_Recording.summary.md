### Threat Assurance Matrix Framework

Steve presented a Threat Assurance Matrix and Warranted Lifecycle Alignment framework that combines his taxonomy for agent systems with the RAP toolkit and Yoav's work on safety coverage mapping. The framework aims to provide granular assurance testing across different stages of an AI system's lifecycle, focusing on identifying marginal failure states where systems appear credible but have hidden flaws. Steve explained that while fiduciary agents are needed for full implementation, subsets of the tests would be useful for broader AI safety applications.
