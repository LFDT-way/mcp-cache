### Digital Identity Trust Framework

Daniel presented research on developing a trust and agency layer for digital identity wallets to help users make informed data sharing decisions, based on 15 years of research and recent findings from GDC colleagues. He explained that the current VTA system needs additional capabilities to support users beyond simple consent buttons, including reasoning about situations, observing requests, verifying parties, and implementing strategic decision-making. Nicky acknowledged the trailblazing nature of the work but questioned what deliverable the Human Trust Experience Task Force might produce, expressing interest in understanding the expected outcomes from the group's involvement.

### VTA Implementation Strategy Discussion

Daniel discussed the potential of the VTA to enable practical implementations, noting previous theoretical limitations due to bounded rationality and privacy concerns. Margeigh suggested creating a storyboard to illustrate real-world scenarios and recommended pursuing a mechanistic rather than AI-driven approach to avoid introducing new risks. The group agreed to dedicate more time to discussing this topic in future Task Force meetings.

### Wallet Issues and Trust Guidelines

Daniel explained that the wallet issues being addressed by EU and Switzerland are not limited to just EU but are general challenges across the industry, with EU countries feeling pressure due to their end-of-year timeline. He provided an update on trust alerts and the best practice repository, noting progress in training the pipeline and producing additional videos, though alignment with trust bites still needs to be addressed. Daniel emphasized the importance of developing clear graphical design guidelines for communication before determining whether to continue with the current pipeline approach or switch to a curated method.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Development Updates

Carly announced that Bite Size Trust will wait for the 1.0 release of Decentralized Trust Graph before starting video production to avoid working with an ever-changing specification. Nicky discussed persona management screens created by Glenn, clarifying that contexts are applications rather than subsets of worlds, and that there is no hierarchy between worlds, faces, and contexts. Margeigh expressed confusion about the relationship between worlds and contexts but agreed the simplified approach without hierarchy was preferable.

### Persona Management UI/UX Design Discussion

The team discussed UI/UX design approaches for a persona management system, with Nicky presenting a simplified user journey starting with an invitation to join a VTC rather than requiring users to build one from scratch. Margeigh suggested using more familiar metaphors like business cards or baseball cards to make the concept more understandable, while Daniel noted that the context derivation aspect would be challenging to implement. The group agreed to continue iterating on the design exploration, with Margeigh potentially leading the UI expertise, though they need more specific requirements from Glenn regarding timeline and deliverables for the Linux Plumbers Conference in early October.
