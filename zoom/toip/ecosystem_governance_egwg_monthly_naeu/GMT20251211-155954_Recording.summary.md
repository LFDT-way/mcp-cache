### OCME: Creator-Controlled Content Platform

Andy Woodruff, Executive Director of the Open Commercial Media Ecosystem (OCME), presented a comprehensive overview of their creator-governed content platform. He explained how OCME enables creators to maintain control over their identity, content policies, and revenue through a decentralized system using Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI), blockchain payments, and AI. The platform has seen significant growth since its launch in 2024, with viewership increasing from 12,000 monthly views to over 5 million in just one year. Andy detailed the ecosystem's structure, which includes four key roles: creators (60% revenue), curators (17.5%), technology providers (17.5%), and OCME (5%), operating within a unified framework that supports multiple content verticals. The platform is built on a hierarchical approach rather than a separate ecosystems model, with governance coordinated through Industry Advisory Councils at both ecosystem and colony levels.
