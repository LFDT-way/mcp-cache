## Overview

The ecosystem governance working group held their final scheduled meeting to review a new strategy proposed by Makki, which outlined five key pillars including governance tech stack alignment, use case deployment, emerging concepts development, knowledge artifacts advancement, and speaker series rebalancing. The strategy focused on creating templates and processes for working groups to develop and test use cases, with plans to align governance frameworks with technical protocols like TRQP and TSP. However, several participants including Eric, Drummond, and Karla raised concerns about the scope of work, lack of clear authorship, and the need for broader working group support before proceeding further. ## Details

The meeting began with introductions and casual conversation before transitioning to business matters. Eric announced that the next two meetings would have no speakers lined up and would be followed by a summer break in July and August. ### Ecosystem Governance Strategy Discussion

Makki led a discussion on resuming ecosystem and governance working group strategy discussions, focusing on five core areas including governance alignment with the tech stack, road mapping and use case deployment, developing emerging concepts like orchid, advancing knowledge artifacts, and rebalancing the guest speaker series. The team reviewed their progress on principles and metadata documentation, with plans to continue collaboration with technical working groups including teams developing TSP, MCP, and AI-enabled trust mechanisms. The discussion emphasized the need for lightweight governance criteria that would allow for self-assessment while maintaining flexibility for teams working on agentic and AI concepts.

### TRQP Governance Framework Implementation Discussion

Makki and Eric discussed governance frameworks and implementation strategies for TRQP and other protocols. Makki proposed enhancing implementation through community outreach, developer engagement, and gathering feedback from users, particularly executives deploying the specifications. They agreed to identify the top three use cases for the remainder of the year and discussed introducing use case templates to facilitate suggestions and prioritization. The conversation concluded with Eric outlining action items, including someone needing to attend the AI working group meeting to discuss TSP MCP evolution.

### Use Case Development Process Discussion

Makki and Eric discussed the process for developing use cases, emphasizing the need for more team participation and clearer role definitions. They agreed that after identifying a use case, the next steps involve assessing its feasibility, checking for overlap with other working groups, and clarifying authorship and responsibilities for implementation and testing. Makki highlighted the importance of testing and deployment to ensure use cases are not just documented but also implemented, and Eric raised concerns about the potential challenges if no one is willing to take on development work.

### Third Party Implementation Assessment

The group discussed involving third parties in assessing implementations and deployments, with Makki suggesting community outreach and announcing use cases as projects. Eric expressed concerns about the ecosystem's role in developing and implementing use cases, preferring to focus on promoting the technology and standard instead. Drummond emphasized the importance of ensuring volunteer availability and suggested using task forces for self-organized work on specific initiatives.

### Ecosystem Governance Strategy Review

The meeting focused on discussing a new ecosystem governance strategy proposed by Makki, which includes developing use cases, templates, and a self-assessment tool based on 23 governance principles. Several concerns were raised about the lack of clear authorship, limited involvement from working group members, and uncertainty about the document's alignment with Trust Over IP standards. The working group decided to review the proposal further before making a decision about moving forward, with Eric and others expressing the need for broader support and clearer benefits before committing to the initiative.
