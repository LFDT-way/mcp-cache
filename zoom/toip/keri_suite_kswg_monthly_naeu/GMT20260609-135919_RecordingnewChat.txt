00:04:36	Carly Huitema (University of Guelph):	This session introduces the principles behind SAIDs, explains how they complement traditional identifiers like DOIs, and highlights their advantages for workflows requiring immediate, verifiable integrity checks. Participants will learn how to generate SAIDs, integrate them into infrastructures, and apply them to enhance trustworthy, standards based metadata practices.
