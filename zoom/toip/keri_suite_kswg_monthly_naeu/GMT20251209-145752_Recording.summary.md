### NIST Key Management and Cryptography

Phil emphasized the importance of understanding key management practices, which are crucial for cryptographic protocols and are outlined in NIST Special Publication 800-57. Samuel introduced the NIST publication, noting its revision in 2020 and anticipating another update in 2026, while highlighting KERI's innovation in addressing key management issues not covered by NIST 857. The discussion focused on the concept of a "crypto period," the lifespan of cryptographic keys, and the vulnerabilities associated with key exposure, emphasizing the need for secure key infrastructures.

### Secure Signing Infrastructure Best Practices

Samuel explained the importance of secure signing infrastructure, emphasizing that it extends beyond secure hardware to include access control APIs and authentication mechanisms. He highlighted that side-channel attacks can compromise the entire infrastructure, not just the cryptographic algorithms. Samuel discussed NIST recommendations for crypto periods, noting that for source authentication keys, the recommended period is one to two years. He also explained the challenges of long-term persistent issuances and the need for mechanisms to anchor issuances to key state, such as secure timestamps or blockchains. Samuel emphasized that without proper key management and logging mechanisms, long-term issuances can become fraud magnets. He also introduced the concept of adaptive crypto periods in KERI, which allows for dynamic adjustments in response to changing threat environments.
