### Self-Addressing Identifiers Presentation

Carly presented her upcoming talk at the Pitfest conference about self-addressing identifiers (SEDs) and their implementation at Agri-Food Data Canada. She explained how SEDs work as content-addressable identifiers that are cryptographically bound to their content, and discussed their practical application in metadata schemas and research infrastructure. The presentation included examples of using the Setify library for generating SEDs and demonstrated how SEDs can be integrated into existing systems as URN:SED identifiers through IANA registration.
