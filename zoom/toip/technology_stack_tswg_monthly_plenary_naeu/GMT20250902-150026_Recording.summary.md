### Trust Challenges in Hospitality Industry

Neil discussed the importance of trust work in the travel and hospitality industry, highlighting the complexity and lack of trust in the sector. He shared an example of how a single hotel property could have up to six different legal identifier owner companies, each responsible for different aspects of the property. Drummond expressed surprise at the complexity of the situation, acknowledging the significant trust issues in the industry.

### Task Force Progress and Updates

The meeting covered updates on various task forces and working groups. Drummond reported that the technology architecture task force has a nearly complete draft with two pending action items and one section requiring changes from Wenjing. The Cross Treasury Task Force, led by Darrell, is preparing for a second public review by the end of the month. The Transpathy protocol is in public review and receiving implementation feedback. ### AI Trust Working Groups Update

Drummond explained that two separate working groups will address AI and human trust: the AI and Human Trust Working Group at Trust Over IP Stack (TSP) and the Decentralized Trust Agent Working Group. He noted that while there will be overlap and synergy between the groups, they will remain distinct. Drummond also mentioned that a blog post announcing these groups and the new Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group would be published soon, with the kickoff meeting for the latter scheduled for the 24th. He added that a white paper on the First Person project, which references the Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group, would be released for community review on Thursday. Steven inquired about an invitation for the Trustcraft group, to which Drummond clarified that joining the working group only requires attending the meeting, and the calendar invite would be added once an LF person returned from vacation. Neil briefly mentioned that the Governance Architecture team is focusing on the UNC FACT project, which examines trade flows with treasuries.
