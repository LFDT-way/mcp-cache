### Decentralized Personal AI System

Reza explained their project to create a decentralized but personal AI system, inspired by Doc Searles' concept of "My Terms" which aims to replace cookies with user-defined data usage terms. The project, which Reza's organization is developing, involves creating a decentralized digital infrastructure for AI that would allow individuals to participate in the AI economy rather than being controlled by a few large companies. Reza invited Drummond to visit their website (www.quai.ai) to learn more about their approach to building this decentralized AI infrastructure.

### Trust and AI Working Group Updates

The meeting covered updates on various working groups and projects related to trust, AI, and decentralized identifiers. Drummond explained the differences between TrustRIP's Trust Spanning Protocol working group and DIFF's Trusted AI Agents group, noting their overlapping yet distinct focuses. Reza highlighted the formation of the Loyal Agents group by Consumer Reports and others, emphasizing the need to align efforts. The High Assurance Vids Task Force was noted as being in stasis, with Alex Tweeddale still aiming to release a public review draft by the symposium. Drummond and Reza discussed the concept of self-certifying identifiers (SCIDs) and its relevance to decentralized AI and trust fabrics, with Reza expressing interest in integrating this into the Kwai project. The group also discussed potential workshop and presentation opportunities at an upcoming symposium and explored the possibility of presenting Kwai's decentralized AI work in the Ecosystem and Governance Working Group.
