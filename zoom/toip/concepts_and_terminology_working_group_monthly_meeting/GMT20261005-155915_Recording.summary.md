### Governance Funding and Framework Updates

Drummond discussed funding opportunities from Humanity and the Pro-Human Coalition for upcoming work, including SpecUp T and glossary development. Carly reported on setting up a glossary spec and working on transforming information into SpecUp T for the governance metamodel refresh. Drummond provided context about the Greater Assertion Working Group's plans to graduate to a Joint Development Foundation (JDF) project, emphasizing the growing demand for governance frameworks in industries like news and entertainment.

### Net Privity and Verifiable Relationships

Steve discussed Richard's concept of net privity, which involves a mutual fiduciary relationship beyond contractual arrangements, with potential applications in employment and entertainment industries. He then explained his focus on verifiable relationship records, including a new proposal for community verifiable relationship records that would allow communities to have agreements with cryptographic verification. Steve mentioned that Sankarshan had already reviewed this work.

### Working Group Rebranding and Standards

Steve suggested focusing on making terms "verifiable" and shortening "Community V Relationship Record" to "C V V R R" or "Sivir" for better comprehension and pronunciation. Drummond discussed the Creative Assertion Working Group's rebranding efforts and shared that the Decentralized Trust Gap Working Group has standardized on three specs using SPACHMPT-enabled repos. Drummond noted that while specs are being agent-generated, human curation will be needed to ensure glossaries remain both agent-readable and human-readable, as different working groups may use inconsistent terms.

### Glossary Management and Standardization

The team discussed glossary management and standardization efforts, with Drummond explaining plans to advance DTWG specifications and potentially secure funding for glossary curation work. Kor demonstrated a new IETF export feature for Spec Up T, which Carly noted would be approximately six months away from completion. Steve provided clarification on digital twin terminology, distinguishing between personal sovereign digital twins and virtual assistants, explaining that the VTA handles trust tasks while the trust-enabled agent (TEA) uses those tasks for various activities.
