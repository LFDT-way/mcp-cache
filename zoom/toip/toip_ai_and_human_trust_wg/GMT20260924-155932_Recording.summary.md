### Classifier Models for AI Agents

The group discussed classifier models as an alternative to traditional LLMs for AI agents. Steve explained that classifier models can evaluate and rank multiple action options generated by an LLM, making them safer and more transparent for fiduciary agents. Wenjing clarified that classifier models are non-generative, functioning as grading algorithms rather than text-generating systems, which makes them safer for compliance purposes. Nicky noted that these models could be beneficial for transparency and compliance with regulations like the EU AI Act.

### AI Care Protocol Discussion

Fingerprint presented a protocol of care designed to encourage helpful behavior between AI agents beyond just speed and task completion. The protocol includes two mechanisms: a "care signal" to flag concerns and a "deliberative hold" to pause actions when issues arise. The group discussed how to implement and test these concepts, with Steve suggesting integration with fiduciary duty frameworks and others noting the need to first establish basic verifiable relationships between agents before advancing to more complex care behaviors. The discussion concluded with agreement to continue exploring these ideas through email and GitHub, with Fingerprint offering to share her presentation and GitHub repository for further feedback.
