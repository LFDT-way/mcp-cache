### Meeting Realignment and Task Force

The team discussed the need to define the differences between various meetings and working groups, with Eric agreeing to start a task force for realignment. Wenjing emphasized the importance of understanding overlaps and current active work within the working groups, particularly regarding antitrust policy and IPR policy. They also introduced new members and discussed a technical paper on confidential systems, which involves secure enclaves in hardware for running computations remotely.

### Secure AI Supply Chain Challenges

Nicky and Wenjing discussed the digital supply chain for AI, focusing on trustworthy supply chains and the governance of intellectual property. They explored the use of secure enclaves for confidential computing, both in the cloud and on mobile devices. Neil shared examples of how agent control and data privacy are crucial in certain scenarios, such as medical history analysis and food allergy considerations. The group acknowledged that while confidential computing provides a foundation, there are still many challenges to address in implementing secure and privacy-preserving systems.

### TEE Integration and Security Challenges

The group discussed trusted execution environments (TEEs) in the context of blockchain and data security. Mitchell presented examples of TEE integrations, highlighting the challenges and costs associated with running computations within these environments. Wenjing suggested that TEEs could be useful for protecting specific sensitive computations, though they remain difficult to implement at scale. The group agreed that creating a comparison table of different protocols, including alternatives to MCP, would be valuable for understanding the landscape of options available.

### AI Agent Framework Development Meeting

The group discussed two main work items: Eric's Trustworthy AI Agent Framework Task Force and Wenjing's AI Agent Protocols over TSP. Eric presented plans for a weekly task force meeting on Tuesdays at 4pm to develop a trust framework for AI agents, while Wenjing shared progress on implementing TSP protocols to enhance security and trust for agent-based applications. The team agreed to organize their work into complementary task forces - one technical and one focused on human experience - with Eric, Neil, and Nicky volunteering to lead the next two meetings while Wenjing is in Greece. They also discussed the need to better define terms like "personal agent" and agreed that use cases and personas should serve as enablers for the broader framework development.
