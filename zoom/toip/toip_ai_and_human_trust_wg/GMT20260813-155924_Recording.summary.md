### Trust Tasks and Agent Architecture

Steve and Wenjing discussed trust tasks, explaining them as reusable, granular cryptographic operations that create verifiable trust between parties. They described how trust tasks can be built on top of a TSP layer to simplify implementation, with the concept of ceremonies representing complete job processes composed of these tasks. The discussion also covered the agent role, which lives inside an ACDC container and manages context for agents, preventing context rot through proper memory management. They explained the deterministic workflow and policy gate system that provides redundancy and ensures system safety through triple failure mechanisms before an agent can fail.

### System Relationship Types Discussion

Steve and Wenjing discussed the different types of relationships in their system, starting with the TSP relationship as a basic handle, followed by verifiable credentials, agreements, and substantive relationships. Steve explained that the most substantial type of relationship is the emergent social bond between agents, which goes beyond verifiable information and involves subjective judgment and reputation. They also touched on relationship views and the relationship surface as a meta view for managing relationship views.
