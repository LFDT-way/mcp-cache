### AI Trustworthiness Assessment Framework

Eric presented a framework for assessing trustworthiness in AI agents, consisting of three pillars: verifiable identity, purpose, and accountability. The framework includes design, implementation, and evaluation patterns, along with practices for users to understand and evaluate AI agents. The framework aims to be accessible to a broad audience, including non-technologists, and provides a structured approach for builders, deployers, and users of AI agents to signal and assess trustworthiness.
