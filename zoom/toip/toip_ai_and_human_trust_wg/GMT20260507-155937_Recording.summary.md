## Details

### Fiduciary Duties for AI Agents

Steve and David discussed the concept of fiduciary duties for AI agents. Steve explained that fiduciary duties involve a more powerful entity having obligations to act in the best interest of a less powerful entity, similar to the relationship between doctors and patients. He proposed that AI agents, particularly those with autonomy, should have specific loyalties and responsibilities, including duties of care and loyalty. Steve mentioned that defining these fiduciary relationships could help determine the necessary architectures for AI systems and support the development of self-sovereign AI.

### AI Security and Cryptography Discussion

David shared his work on a cryptographic primitive called Proof of Sequential Work Function. Steve then described Fractalic.AI, an open-source project that creates self-evolving runtime environments by saving context and interactions as a single hashed file. The conversation ended with an agreement to explore each other's work further.
