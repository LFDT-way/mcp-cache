### Protocol Acceptance vs. Action Clarification

Steve and Wenjing discussed the difference between "accept" and "act" in a protocol. Wenjing explained that "accept" is a provisional acceptance that creates a binding condition, while "act" finalizes the agreement and makes it effective. They clarified that in their model, one party accepts and the other party acknowledges to create binding. Wenjing also introduced ACDC (Authentic Chained Data Containers) as a data format that enables delegation and verification through content-based addressability.

### Delegation Protocol Design Discussion

The team discussed a proposed protocol for delegation and authorization that doesn't require a universal clock and aims to balance risks symmetrically between parties. Wenjing presented the protocol design, which includes message requirements with bounding invariants and an act that closes negotiations. Neil raised concerns about personal data privacy in delegation chains and proposed a micro-sharing approach where agents request specific data on demand rather than receiving full profiles. The group agreed to add sections covering patterns where agents request additional authorization and mechanisms for minimizing private data sharing, with Wenjing planning to draft these sections and incorporate feedback from the discussion.
