Eric presented a proposal for a task force to develop a framework for trustworthy AI agents, focusing on identity, purpose, and accountability as key pillars. The framework aims to provide builders, service providers, and users with a clear understanding of AI agent risks and benefits, incorporating both governance and technical aspects. The task force will evaluate whether the proposed framework encompasses all necessary elements and discuss potential expansions or combinations.
