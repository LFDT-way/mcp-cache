### Fiduciary Agent Architecture Discussion

Steve presented his work on fiduciary agents, explaining that these are agents that implement duties derived from the legal concepts of duty of care and duty of loyalty. He discussed his 100+ page fiduciary preferences document that details workflows for fiduciary behavior and agent interactions, and shared a proposed layered architecture for an integrated fiduciary agent stack. The discussion was cut off mid-explanation as Steve was about to show how the architecture implements fiduciary relationships, with Drummond requesting clarification on what specifically defines a fiduciary agent.

### Agent Training and Trust Architecture

Steve explained to Drummond how agents can be trained using duty cases in the training data, with system prompts guiding agents on their roles and duties. He described implementing redundant duty checks and verification processes to ensure agents follow appropriate behaviors and prevent unauthorized actions. Steve also outlined the Trust over IP stack architecture, including the use of R cards, verifiable relationship credentials, and trust tasks, noting that implementing this system would be a significant challenge for the task force.

### ODRL for Fiduciary Duties Discussion

The team discussed using ODRL (Open Digital Rights Language) to specify and verify fiduciary duties, with Steve noting that while ODRL seems suitable, it may not handle all aspects of complex fiduciary duties effectively. Carly explained that ODRL requires additional vocabulary specifications beyond the core language to define duty concepts, similar to how JSON-LD works. The group acknowledged that while ODRL can express specific duties like data deletion requirements, broader fiduciary duties like acting in someone's best interest may require additional policy expression mechanisms beyond what ODRL provides.

### Digital Agent Fiduciary Taxonomy Framework

Steve presented his work on creating a taxonomy of fiduciary duties for digital agents, which took three weeks to develop and contains approximately 200 interwoven duties derived from loyalty and care. The framework includes concepts like epistemic humility, rational verification, and the creation of an epistemic core similar to Wikipedia for verified information sharing among agents. Steve explained his motivation came from reading Richard's book and wanting to implement fiduciary duties to constrain digital twin behavior, with the system designed to create a collective database of anonymized decision-making examples that other fiduciary agents can reference.

### Fiduciary Agent Architecture Development

The group discussed developing a fiduciary agent architecture focused on travel-related duties as a practical example. Steve confirmed that Richard and Nell Clasby had reviewed the document positively, and he offered to create a travel-specific appendix to illustrate the duty generation process. Steve announced plans to release a reference implementation by year-end, starting with ChatGPT-based functionality within the next month or two, primarily using Fractalic.ai models for versioning and execution tracking.
