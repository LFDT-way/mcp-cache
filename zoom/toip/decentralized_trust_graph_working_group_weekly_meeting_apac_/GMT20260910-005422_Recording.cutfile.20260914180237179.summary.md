## Details

### Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group

Drummond hosted a meeting of the Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group's Asia Pacific session, welcoming participants from Digital Identity New Zealand. He introduced working group members including Joe Spencer and Sankarshan, who discussed their contributions to the Trust Over IP protocol development. Drummond shared that there is significant interest in New Zealand's potential leadership role in decentralized trust graph adoption, which he mentioned at the Global Digital Collaboration Conference in Geneva. The meeting was focused on providing updates to Digital Identity New Zealand participants about the working group's progress and exploring how the technology could be applied in the region.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Overview

Drummond provided an overview of the Decentralized Trust Graph work and the structure of the joint Trust Over IP and Decentralized Identity Foundation working group. He highlighted recent developments, including an upcoming presentation to the Linux kernel team and the simplification of persona management to three types: one-time synonym, private persona, and public persona. The group also discussed three new proposed credentials closely associated with Agentic: verifiable delegation credentials, verifiable authorization credentials, and statement credentials.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Updates

Drummond provided an update on the Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group, highlighting seven active task forces and two major open source implementations: OpenVTC and P2P Trust Stack. He also discussed recent developments at the Global Digital Collaboration conference in Geneva, where there was significant interest in fiduciary AI and decentralized trust solutions. The Open Wallet Foundation has joined the Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust, and several mobile apps, including Keyring, are being developed to implement verifiable credentials.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Project Updates

Drummond presented updates on the Decentralized Trust Graph project, highlighting potential applications including Union (a social contact management app), VTA Data Rooms, priority messaging, and crowdfunding features. The team discussed upcoming milestones for the next 60-90 days, focusing on developing MVP product plans for verifiable trust networks (VTNs) in collaboration with the First Person Cooperative. Andy and Chris expressed strong interest in implementing these trust graph technologies in New Zealand, particularly for agricultural applications and integrating with existing systems like Affinity's Agent Gateway.

### Decentralized Identity Systems Progress

Souradeep presented a paper on decentralized identity, highlighting New Zealand's progress in moving from centralized to decentralized identity systems and the need to scale reference architecture. He emphasized the importance of considering community aspects and devolved decision-making in identity systems, using a health system hack as an example of how decentralized approaches could address legacy system problems. The presentation generated positive feedback from participants, with Karaitiana noting some terminology issues that needed correction and Drummond expressing interest in sharing the presentation with the Decentralized Identity Working Group.

### TrustRP in Education and Employment

The meeting focused on discussing the application of TrustRP (Trust Relationship Protocol) in educational and employment contexts, with participants exploring how the decentralized trust graph could support lifelong learning journeys and credential verification. Drummond shared insights from a recent GDC conference track and mentioned an upcoming ProHuman Assembly conference in DC, while noting his upcoming absence from next week's calls due to the conference. The group discussed progress on use cases, with four out of six use cases (including owning a bank account, lost funds/credentials, qualifications, and proof of age) currently under review, and plans to address remaining use cases including Know Your Customer and digital equity policy considerations.
