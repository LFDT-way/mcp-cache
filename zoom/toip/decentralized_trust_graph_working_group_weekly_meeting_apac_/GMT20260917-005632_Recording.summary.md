## Details

### DTV Project Linux Plumbers Update

Sankarshan provided an update on the DTV project, focusing on preparations for the upcoming Linux Plumbers Conference in 2-3 weeks. The main progress included stabilization of credential specifications, publication of 386 Trust Tasks with support for multiple programming languages, and discussions with Berkeley regarding ZKP-related code contributions. Andy shared that he presented the New Zealand team's work at an APAC summit and mentioned that someone from Seattle was interested in connecting with the team.

### ZKP Implementation Progress Update

Sankarshan provided an update on ZKP (Zero Knowledge Proof) work, noting that while there has been progress and outreach, the team has not yet reached the stage of moving from discussions to actual code implementation and consumer-facing use cases. Adam inquired about the European digital wallets initiative and its alignment with the 2027 timeframe, to which Sankarshan responded that while there is reasonable confidence about lining up, the main challenge is coordinating stakeholders to move from intent to actual implementation. Sankarshan shared relevant links and resources, including the Trust Over IP repository and a GDC presentation, to help Adam catch up on the ZKP specifications and initiatives.

### Task Force Activities Update

Sankarshan outlined upcoming task force activities over the next few weeks, focusing on internal conversations and delivering requirements. Adam confirmed his affiliation with Mastercard and discussed expanding his remit to include APAC, while noting the importance of aligning work with EU developments. Andy mentioned Westpac's recent accreditation as a MasterCard issuer in New Zealand and discussed the importance of cross-party trust in addressing fraud issues. Tom inquired about MTLS implementation but was informed by Adam that no current solution exists. Sankarshan also shared a link providing more detailed information about project components and tests.

### Project Signal Group Updates

Sankarshan discussed adding Adam to a signal group for project updates and offered to broker contact with Drummond. The group reviewed a VTC repository on GitHub, which Tom confirmed he would examine, particularly regarding its deployment status. Adam expressed enthusiasm about the project's potential to replace PGP, to which Tom responded with cautious optimism.

### Mobile License Trust Interoperability Challenges

Andy shared insights about Australia's interoperability challenges with mobile driver's licenses across different states and their approach to solving this through a multi-state trust service. He expressed confidence in the Decentralized Trust Graph approach and discussed progress on setting up verifiable trust networks in Auckland and Queenstown. Adam noted that Australia is following the EU's trust list approach using OpenID Federation, though with privacy concerns, while both agreed on the importance of addressing global trust interoperability.

### Vehicle Registry Standards Collaboration

The group discussed challenges with vehicle registry interoperability standards in Australia and New Zealand. Adam explained that while Victoria has its own standards and New South Wales has adopted full ISO specifications, there's no central governance in Australia, with each state maintaining separate DMVs. The discussion explored potential collaboration with AMVA (the Association of American Motor Vehicle Administrators) and the Global Registry Information Directory working group, with Adam offering to connect Andy with relevant contacts including John Phillips from Sezu who chairs the working group. Sankarshan agreed to provide Andy with the meeting link for the upcoming working group session, and Adam committed to following up with Drummond about adding Andy to the signal group.
