## Details

### APAC Trust Working Group Meeting

The APAC meeting of the Decentralized Trust Trask Working Group was held, with Drummond chairing the session. The group confirmed their regular Wednesday meeting time and discussed the Risk Assessment Harms Prevention Task Force proposal, which Scott will present in Nikki's absence. They reviewed proposed deliverables and discussed recent announcements, including Trevor's appointment as chair of the Blockchain Governance Working Group and Lisa Rassoon's leadership of the Hawaii open source AI project. ### Deliverables and Task Force Planning

Drummond presented a table of proposed deliverables for the working group, including risk assessment, harm prevention, and credentials specifications. He explained the structure of task forces and their relationship to deliverables. Sankarshan suggested adding indicative timelines to help potential contributors plan their participation. Scott was set to discuss the risk assessment proposal, which would inform the development of credentials specifications. The group agreed to form the risk assessment task force, with Jeff Turk expressing interest in joining.

### Trust Over IP Risk Assessment Framework

Scott presented a risk assessment framework for the Trust Over IP project, explaining how to use a downloadable Excel/Google Sheets template that follows ISO 27000 and NIST best practices. He outlined the risk assessment process, including establishing scope, identifying risks, analyzing likelihood and severity, and determining mitigation strategies. Drummond emphasized the unique challenges of assessing risks in socio-technical systems, particularly for decentralized trust graphs, and highlighted the importance of considering both technical and human factors in risk management.

### Digital Identity Risks and Framework

The meeting focused on risks and harms in digital identity systems, with Scott highlighting three key risks: control of private keys, identity verification, and cryptographic assertions. Sankar Shan discussed a 2022 document on human harms in digital identity, noting that coercion was a significant concern then, and emphasized the need for a framework that can be easily adopted and understood by designers. Drummond recommended reading the Risk Assessment Companion Guide and the harms document, which provide valuable insights for the working group. Kevin suggested applying concepts from the process industry, such as layers of protection, to address risks in digital identity systems.

### Task Force Planning and Scheduling

The group discussed plans for a new task force, agreeing to hold its first meeting next week to define scope and risks, with Drummond noting that regular meetings would be suspended during the upcoming Internet Identity Workshop. Scott proposed holding two sessions for the task force, while Drummond suggested using next week's meeting as the first session, particularly since some members will be attending the Internet Identity Workshop. Russ shared his thoughts on the first-person project as a foundation for a new intention economy, and mentioned plans to discuss aspirational identity with Drummond on Monday.
