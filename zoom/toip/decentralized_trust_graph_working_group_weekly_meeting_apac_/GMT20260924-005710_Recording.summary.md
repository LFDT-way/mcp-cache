## Overview

The meeting focused on updates and discussions around digital identity initiatives, particularly the development and implementation of Verifiable Trust Agents (VTAs) and related technologies. Key updates included progress from the ZKP team, ongoing work on open-source projects like OpenVTA, Trust Stackers, and Keyring, and plans to demonstrate these solutions at the Linux Plumber Summit in Prague. Participants shared insights on challenges and opportunities in different regions, including Australia, New Zealand, India, and the US, with a particular emphasis on indigenous communities and their unique needs, such as simplifying identity verification for Maori land trusts. Technical discussions covered integration challenges, hardware-based authentication options, and the development of standards for confidential computing and P2P networks. The group also explored potential partnerships and funding opportunities, including a possible proposal to Humanity AI focused on supporting indigenous groups in the US.

### Digital Identity Systems Comparison

The discussion focused on differences between India and the US regarding digital identity systems, particularly driver's licenses. Sankarshan explained that India currently doesn't have a national digital license system (MDL) and licenses are issued at the state level by transport authorities, though they have recently started issuing digitally verifiable licenses through DigiLocker. The conversation revealed that while India has been early in digital ID programs for passports, their approach to driver's licenses differs from the US system, with India having a national ID program while the US lacks a federal approach to digital licenses.

Justin explained that AuthSignal has already signed major customers including MoneyGram, Air New Zealand, TradeMe, and British Telecom, and they are working on connecting digital identity networks with blockchain technologies like Solana. The discussion was cut off mid-sentence but appeared to be building on updates from a previous meeting about Maori VTN.
