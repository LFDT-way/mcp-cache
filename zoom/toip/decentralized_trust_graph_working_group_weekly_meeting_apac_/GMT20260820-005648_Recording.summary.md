## Details

### Digital Identity Review Meeting

Drummond introduced Andy Higgs, the Executive Director of Digital Identity New Zealand, to the meeting participants. Drummond mentioned he would complete reviewing the PR after the call, noting it contained detailed work on witness credentials and their complexity. ### Digital Identity and Trust Governance

Sankarshan provided background on his seven-year involvement with TrustRP, focusing on governance and the Decentralized Trust Graph, while Tom shared his experience starting with PGP implementation and his current focus on personal AI rather than enterprise AI. Andrew explained his role with Digital Identity New Zealand, describing it as part of Tech New Zealand with approximately 2,000 members including corporates and government entities. The discussion included a brief personal connection as Tom mentioned his daughter's connection to University of Otago.

### New Zealand Sovereign Identity Progress

Andrew discussed New Zealand's progress on sovereign identity infrastructure, highlighting recent events including presentations by SSI experts and Dr. Pramod Varma from India. He explained that while New Zealanders understand the identity problems, they haven't built their own sovereign infrastructure and are currently dependent on foreign tech companies and Australian banks. Andrew expressed interest in New Zealand serving as a test lab for identity solutions and connecting with the broader community working on verifiable trust credentials.

### Trusted Credentials Adoption Updates

Andrew explained that the trusted credentials adoption group is currently focusing on low complexity, high volume use cases including workplace check-ins, proof of age, payments, and education qualifications. He mentioned a gap in their program regarding Maori communities and discussed potential solutions including crowdfunding through first-person cooperatives. The group is also exploring agentic use cases, specifically working with IRD on allowing tax agents to submit returns digitally through VTCs or VTNs. Drummond offered to help Andrew introduce key people from his network to future meetings, suggesting they could attend the next weekly plenary meeting scheduled for the same time slot the following week.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Planning Meeting

Drummond and Andrew discussed planning a meeting for September 9th-10th to introduce the Decentralized Trust Graph work to a broader team, with Andrew noting he would need to get Executive Council commitment first. Drummond provided an overview of the Linux Foundation's meeting structure and processes, explaining how task forces operate and the use of collaboration tools like Discord, Confluence, and GitHub. Sankarshan shared insights about Aadhaar's evolution toward more VC-oriented specifications and discussed potential opportunities for collaboration between India and New Zealand markets. The group agreed to work together on preparing a structured agenda for the upcoming APAC meeting.
