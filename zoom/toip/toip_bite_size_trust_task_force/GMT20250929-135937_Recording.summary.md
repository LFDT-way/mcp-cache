### DID Verification Methods Diverge

Carly explained the different decentralized identifier (DID) methods and their approaches to updating and verifying DID documents. She highlighted that while all DID methods produce standardized documents, their methods for verifying updates vary significantly, with some providing tamper-evident logs and cryptographic evidence while others rely on trust between parties. Scott raised concerns about the lack of consensus on DID methods, noting that this variability hinders interoperability and progress. Carly acknowledged this issue and suggested adding a statement to emphasize that while the DID document is standardized, the external verification methods differ across implementations.

Carly presented on password security and alternatives, explaining the limitations of traditional passwords and introducing the concept of public and private keys, as well as decentralized identifiers (DIDs).
