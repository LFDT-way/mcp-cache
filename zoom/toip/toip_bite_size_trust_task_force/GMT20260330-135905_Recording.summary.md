### Third-Party Trust Anchor Risks

Carly and Scott discussed the risks of third-party trust anchors in digital systems, particularly the issue of duplicity where controllers of identifiers present conflicting versions of their history to different relying parties. Drummond joined the conversation to clarify that both conflicting histories would appear valid, raising questions about how such duplicity could be detected and understood. The discussion highlighted the complexity of digital trust systems and the challenges in ensuring the integrity of identifier histories.

### Digital Identity System Duplicity Discussion

Drummond discussed the concept of duplicity in digital identity systems, explaining how using different key histories with different parties doesn't constitute duplicity as long as each party has a verifiable history with the same identifier. Carly raised concerns about security risks, particularly key compromise and impersonation, leading to a discussion about the importance of witnesses and watchers in self-certifying identifiers.
