### Identity Verification Witness and Watcher

Carly and Scott discussed the concepts of witnesses and watchers in identity verification systems. Carly explained that witnesses are selected by the identity owner and act independently, while watchers are selected by the verifier to verify identities and detect inconsistencies. They clarified that these are programmatic processes rather than people, with witnesses signing receipts and watchers performing verification tasks. The discussion also touched on similarities between current identity systems and X.509 certificate systems, with Carly noting that while the systems look different, they perform similar functional roles.

### Certificate Validation Processes Discussion

Scott and Carly discussed certificate validation processes, focusing on the differences between X509 certificate status methods and how they compare to verifiable credentials. They also discussed timestamping authorities and trust lists in the context of digital asset validation, with Scott questioning how verifiable credentials handle timestamping standards.
