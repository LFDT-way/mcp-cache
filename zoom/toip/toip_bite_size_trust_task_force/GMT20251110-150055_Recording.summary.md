### Key Pre-rotation in DID Systems

Carly explained the concept of key pre-rotation in DID systems, where a cryptographic digest of the next public key is published instead of the full key, making key rotation predictable and tamper-resistant. She noted that while some DID methods like DidWebS and DIDWebVH support key pre-rotation, others do not. The practice of pre-rotation helps secure DIDs against takeover by ensuring that even if the current private key is compromised, the attacker cannot hijack the DID since they do not know the next key that has already been committed to.

### Service Endpoints in Decentralized Identifiers

Carly explained the concept of service endpoints in decentralized identifiers (DIDs), describing them as address book entries that tell others how to reach a DID holder and which services they support. She emphasized that DIDs themselves do not handle routing, but service endpoints connect DIDs to existing infrastructure, enabling communication between parties. Carly's explanation was praised by Drummond for being clearer than previous attempts, and they discussed the possibility of creating a separate explainer video for semantic engines.
