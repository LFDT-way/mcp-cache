### DIDCOM: Secure Digital Identity Protocol

Carly and Neil discussed DIDCOM (Decentralized Identifier Communication), a protocol enabling secure and private messaging between digital identities. Carly explained how DIDCOM ensures trust by cryptographically signing messages, verifying control over DIDs, and being used in digital identity wallets and government pilots. Neil highlighted OpenID Connect's adoption of DIDCOM and its limitations, suggesting a government-issued identifier bootstrap mechanism. They also touched on key rotation challenges in FIDO authentication and the potential of passkeys for identity management.
