Min and Drummond discussed the membership and participation requirements for the Trust Over IP working group, clarifying that existing LF Decentralized Trust members need to sign a Trust over IP membership agreement to contribute, while non-members can participate as observers. Drummond highlighted the group's joint participation with DIF and outlined the IPR terms, including Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 for output and Apache 2.0 for source code. He emphasized the importance of governance and user experience in the TrustRIP stack, mentioning a proposed task force on UI and UX to prevent harm and promote benefits. The conversation ended with a brief overview of the TrustRep Wiki, which contains background information and resources related to the working group's charter.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group

The meeting focused on organizing a working group to develop decentralized trust graph technology. Drummond outlined the group's structure, including plans for task forces, a wiki for documentation, and a Discord channel for communication. He emphasized the importance of privacy preservation and explained key concepts like personal credentials, verifiable relationship credentials, and persona DIDs. The group discussed the need for a risk assessment task force, which Scott agreed to participate in. Drummond suggested an asynchronous work approach with one weekly coordinating meeting. The conversation ended with a discussion about funding and pilot use cases, with Jonathan offering to help with funding efforts.
