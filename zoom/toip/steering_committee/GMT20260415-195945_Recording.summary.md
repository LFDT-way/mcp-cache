### Decentralized Identity and AI Initiatives

Grace discussed her experience as Executive Director at DIF, noting that the organization is in good shape with proper systems and budgets in place. She highlighted AI-related initiatives as a key focus area, including work on content assertions, creator assertions, and Agendic AI working groups. ### DIF Collaboration and Standards Strategy

Grace discussed the importance of collaborations in DIF, emphasizing their role in avoiding duplicated efforts and creating standards that can be accepted by larger entities. She highlighted the focus on bringing these efforts into organizations like standards bodies or large companies to develop more comprehensive specifications. Grace also explained the difference between DIF and Trust Over IP, noting that DIF operates on a consensus-based approach, while Trust Over IP is more opinionated about the stack. Drummond raised a question about potential collaboration on GDC sessions, mentioning an update about LF-related organizations being encouraged to coordinate under LF and propose sessions by September 1st or 3rd.

### Linux Foundation Organization Changes

Drummond discussed changes to session organization, noting that the Linux Foundation and related groups are being consolidating under a single umbrella organization to reduce the number of small players involved. Grace expressed enthusiasm about potential collaboration opportunities and mentioned that DIF has received two session proposals from members, though they're taking a measured approach due to unpredictable outcomes from last year's process. The group agreed to support and co-sponsor each other's session proposals, with Grace confirming DIF's willingness to collaborate on relevant initiatives.

### European Submission Timeline Discussion

Grace discussed the timeline for submissions to a European organization, noting that while the deadline is in June, meetings will only occur every other week in June and July. She expressed a preference for graduating current working groups like crypto and DIDCOM to more mature standards bodies, emphasizing that Diff's role should be as a proto-standards organization focused on implementation rather than maintaining ongoing working groups. Grace mentioned that quantum computing may become more relevant next year, and she includes updates about ongoing discussions in her newsletter to keep members informed.
