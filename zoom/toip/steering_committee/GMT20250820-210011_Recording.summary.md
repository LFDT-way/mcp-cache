### Steering Committee Report Planning Discussion

The steering committee discussed the need to submit annual and quarterly reports to TAC, with Wenjing suggesting they could use the operational plan as a starting point for the annual report. ### Trust Over IP Participation Challenges

Scott and Drummond discussed the participation levels within Trust over IP, noting that only about 1% of members are actively leading tasks, while 9% are active participants, and 90% are passive. They emphasized the need for a more active steering committee, composed of those in the 1-9% categories, to ensure the organization's viability. Scott acknowledged the voluntary nature of the work and the challenges faced by members balancing billable work with Trust over IP commitments, while Drummond highlighted the importance of personal interest in driving the organization forward.

### New Working Group Formation Discussion

Drummond discussed the formation of a new working group to better represent and act on various projects and interests within the organization. Makki suggested reviewing the organizational plan and setting clear objectives and key results to align efforts. David offered support in engaging the broader community and suggested resources for increasing participation. Nicholas introduced an idea about making governance frameworks programmatically enforceable, which Scott related to an upcoming presentation on proof of governance applications. The group discussed the process for proposing new working groups and the importance of aligning efforts to achieve common goals.

### Symposium Scheduling and Attendance Challenges

The group discussed scheduling challenges for an upcoming symposium, with Wenjing expressing concerns about October's busy conference schedule making it difficult to attend in person. While the symposium was originally planned for August 21-23, the team considered moving it to November or December due to potential remote participation issues, though Scott emphasized that 90% of success would come from attendance rather than just planning.

### Conference Planning and Presentation Format

The team discussed plans for an upcoming conference, focusing on the timing and format of presentations and workshops. Drummond suggested a format with recorded 30-60 minute talks on the first day, followed by interactive workshops on the second day, aiming for a 6-week lead time for announcements. Wenjing expressed a need for specific time slots to block in her calendar, and confirmed she would present on TSP, including its new revision and various applications beyond AI.

### Symposium Topics and Trust Initiatives

The steering committee discussed several hot topics for an upcoming symposium, including TSP adoption, AI trust issues, and a new decentralized trust graph working group launching in September. Drummond highlighted that they have secured presentations for at least six topics, with champions identified for each, though timing remains a key consideration. The group agreed to add trust over IP and a Valei Hackathon presentation to the agenda, with Nicholas and Keren expressing interest in collaborating on these topics.

### Hong Kong Event Planning Discussion

The team discussed plans for an upcoming two-day event in Hong Kong on November 7th, with Keren confirming the location and seeking proposals for judges. Eric raised questions about differentiating between day one presentations and day two workshops, suggesting a potential Glyph Hackathon workshop. Makki offered to create a strategy document for day one, focusing on an overview of Trust over IP. The group agreed to validate workshop proposals and consider timing, with Drummond suggesting that some workshops might be better suited for early December due to resource constraints. Wenjing raised concerns about the feasibility of hackathon-style workshops and suggested potentially spreading the event over non-consecutive days.

### Symposium Scheduling and Telescope Group

The group discussed scheduling for an upcoming symposium, settling on November 19-20 as the preferred dates after considering various calendar constraints. They noted that this timing would be a week before Thanksgiving, allowing for better attendance since people tend to travel less that week. The team also discussed the launch of a decentralized telescope working group and agreed to present their proposed dates at the ecosystem governance working group meeting the following day.

### Trust Graph Working Group Launch

Drummond discussed the launch of the Decentralized Trust Graph working group, which is planned for September after a long delay since its May approval. He shared a review copy of the Trust P white paper with Kim Duffy to explore potential joint participation from Diff, and presented the concept to the W3C Credentials community group. Drummond and Daniela agreed to schedule the first meeting for the second week of September, after the white paper's release, and Daniela offered to create a meeting stub and blog post to promote the group's launch.

### Trust Over IP Working Group

The group discussed forming a working group to engage new members for the Trust over IP initiative, aiming to attract individuals who are currently 9% or 1% participants. Drummond expressed enthusiasm for the project, highlighting its potential to bring in new corporate members and clarify the organization's goals, such as the trust stack and governance. Eric suggested targeting the graph community, but Drummond clarified that while two graph-based projects are involved, the decentralized Trust Graph focuses more on trust than graph complexity.

### Decentralized Trust Working Group Collaboration

The meeting focused on the decentralized trust working group and its potential collaboration with Diff. Drummond presented a draft white paper and charter for the group, which includes four initial task forces. The team discussed the need for formal agreements with Diff and other partners, with Daniela confirming she had completed her part of the paperwork. They also touched on the potential for collaboration with the Carey Foundation, with Daniela reporting positive progress in that area. The conversation ended with a goal to make the decentralized trust project the most active and productive in LFDT.
