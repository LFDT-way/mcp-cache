### Trust IP Specification Approval Process

The Trustees Steering Committee meeting focused on finalizing the process for approving technical specifications, particularly for the Kery Suite Working Group's three specs. Drummond explained that the process was developed because these were the first technical specifications ready for Trust IP approval. Alan Davies, the CTO of Digital Trust Foundry, introduced himself and shared his background in the SSI space, including his work on Credential Master. The committee reviewed two wiki pages detailing the JDF requirements and the process for approving specifications. Due to the absence of Carla and Nico, some planned discussions were postponed for future meetings.

### Technical Specifications Review Process

Drummond explained the process for technical specifications to be reviewed and approved by the Trust over AP Steering Committee, emphasizing the importance of the working group's involvement and the necessity of a public review before final approval. He outlined the stages of the process, including pre-draft, working draft, working group approval, and final Steering Committee approval, as well as the option for an expedited review in urgent cases. Drummond also detailed the requirements for a review package, including a link to the deliverable, summary of changes, and a request for either standard or expedited review, highlighting the importance of the working group's input in recommending review committee members.

### Documentation Review Process Improvements

The group discussed the use of "must" versus "should" in documentation, with Scott advocating for using "must" to indicate requirements. They reviewed a document's review process, emphasizing the need for invited experts from both problem/requirements and solution spaces. Maki suggested delegating the activity of sourcing independent experts to the working group early on, as delays in securing experts could impact the review process. Scott proposed making Maki a Steering Committee member due to his active participation in meetings.

### Steering Committee and Review Process Updates

The group discussed adding Maki to the Steering Committee for 2026, with Drummond noting they need to hold a formal election in January. Drummond assigned an action item to update the process documentation, and the group discussed how to handle intellectual property rights for external contributors, confirming that GitHub contributions would be covered under the standard license.
