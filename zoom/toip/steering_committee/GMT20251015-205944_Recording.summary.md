### Utah's State-Endorsed Digital Identity

Steve explained that the term "state-endorsed digital identity" refers to the state of Utah's approach of endorsing rather than establishing identity, similar to how it endorses a birth certificate. He emphasized that this endorsement allows individuals to maintain their identity across different states and contexts, unlike previous systems that anchored identity to specific credentials like driver's licenses. Scott agreed with this approach, comparing it to the role of certification authorities in endorsing trust and defining clear roles, which aligns with the concept of governance authorities asserting only what they have the right to assert.
