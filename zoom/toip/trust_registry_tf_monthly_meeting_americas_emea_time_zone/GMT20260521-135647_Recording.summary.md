## Overview

The Trust Registry Task Force meeting focused on discussing next steps for the Trust Registry Query Protocol (TRQP) v2.0 specification, which has been approved by the steering committee. The main discussion centered around how to demonstrate real-world adoption and create traction for the protocol, with Scott emphasizing the need for a proof of concept and white paper showing how TRQP could be used within the COG specification to query trust registries. Darrell and Sankarshan discussed the balance between driving adoption through practical implementations while also addressing technical gaps in the specification, particularly around discovery capabilities that were removed from TRQP v2.0. The group acknowledged that downstream implementers like IRA and COG could take the lead on creating reference implementations, while the task force could focus on promoting the specification and identifying additional work areas.

Darrell and Sankarshan discussed categorizing work items for TRQP, with Darrell proposing a "heavy and light" approach to organize the tasks. They reviewed a landscape diagram created by Sankarshan that outlines various areas of work including lifecycle, revocation, discovery, transport, and credential ecosystems. Scott emphasized the need for a proof of concept and a paper describing how to use the TRQP within the COG specification to gain adoption. Scott and Darrell discussed the need to demonstrate and document the implementation of TRQP protocol, particularly for COG. ### Proof of Concept Discussion

Scott emphasized the need to promote the specification and attract more people to the task force before proceeding with work products. The group agreed on a dual-pronged approach where the specification-focused task force would continue technical work while a traction group would focus on outreach and demonstrations. They also briefly discussed an upcoming APAC edition meeting, with Sankarshan expressing concern about the early time for him.
