### Digital Identity Trust Registry Framework

Darrell explained the Global Digital Collaboration's Trust Registry Task Force, which he co-leads with Apple and France, focusing on defining the minimal requirements for consuming government-issued digital identity documents. The task force is working to scope the problem by identifying what is needed for verifying digital passports, driver's licenses, and ID cards, with particular attention to both technical trust and authority verification. Darrell noted that while there are ongoing taxonomy debates about what constitutes a credential, the group is working to establish clear definitions and authority frameworks for trust registries.

### Service Discovery and Trust Framework

Fabrice explained Verana's approach to service discovery, which involves identifying controllers and actors of services using verifiable credentials and building a trust graph for querying services. Darrell and Scott discussed the complexity of certification processes, particularly for ISO standards, emphasizing the need for accreditation bodies to validate certification entities. The group agreed that trust verification is context-dependent and requires a governance framework to ensure adoption and security of ecosystems.

### Building Trust in Credential Ecosystems

Fabrice discussed the challenges of building trust in credential ecosystems, emphasizing the need for a "proof of trust" system that uses trust deposits and slashes to rate ecosystem participants. He highlighted the importance of search and discovery functionality to help relying parties find and verify trustworthy ecosystems, noting that current systems lack effective searching capabilities beyond basic credential crawling. The discussion concluded with agreement on the need for better trust graph indexing and verification methods, with Fabrice suggesting that credentials could serve as a more meaningful form of visibility than traditional advertising.
