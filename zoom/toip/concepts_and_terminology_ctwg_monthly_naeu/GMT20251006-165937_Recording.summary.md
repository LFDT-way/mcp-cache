### Glossary Reference Types Explained

The meeting focused on explaining different types of references in glossaries, including DEF (internal definition), REF (internal reference), TREF (transclusion reference to external glossary), and XREF (external reference). Drummond and Kor clarified that TREF and XREF are used for referencing terms from external glossaries, with TREF copying the definition into the local document while XREF only points to the external definition. Martina raised a question about the direction of referencing, which was clarified by Drummond and Kor as being from the local document to an external source. They also discussed the ability to customize how terms appear in local documents while maintaining consistency with the original definitions.

### TREF Implementation and SpecUpT Discussion

Kor and Drummond discussed the implementation of TREF (External Reference) in documents, explaining how tags are used to map to URLs of referenced documents. They clarified that tags are set up once for a document and used to point to other documents efficiently. Kor mentioned that SpecUpT, a terminology feature developed from SpecUp by DIF, is not actively maintained and will not be merged into SpecUp, making it easier to proceed with Speck of Tea.

### TREF Feature and Blockchain Migration

Kor demonstrated a new feature that allows users to control the appearance of terms in TREFs and automatically redirect internal links to local definitions if available. They also showed how users can add their own annotations to external definitions, making it possible to customize and extend TREF content. Henk mentioned that they have moved from blockchain Bird to Trust over IP and are working on creating keys for term definitions in glossaries and specs, ensuring compatibility across different ecosystems.

### Glossary Tool Functionality Demonstration

He highlighted the tool's ability to handle external links and its focus on providing context-specific information.
