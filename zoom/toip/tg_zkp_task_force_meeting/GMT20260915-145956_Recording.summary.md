## Details

### ADR001 Offline Voucher Verification Progress

The team discussed progress from the Berkeley session where ADR001 was validated as the first proof to build for offline voucher verification using blind signatures. Mitchell explained that the signature scheme selection impacts ZKP choices, with lattice-based schemes having different proof families than hash-based schemes, and noted that timing considerations affect user experience. The group reviewed Mitchell's work on creating construction records in the ZKP spec draft, which now includes 12 records with ADR001 being the first composed, and discussed the need to clarify where Berkeley's work fits within the Trust Over IP framework.

### Zero-Knowledge Proof Specification Overview

Mitchell explained the key components of the zero-knowledge proof (ZKP) specification, including statements, witnesses, gadgets, and disclosure sets, using Zcash's SAPLING vulnerability as an example to illustrate the importance of gadgets in ZKP systems. He outlined the construction process from request to publication, including draft (carded), construction, runtime, vetting, and final publication stages. Mitchell also discussed the relationship between different credential disclosure models (pairwise, directed, and public) and how they relate to the ZKP specification, emphasizing that pairwise connections require stronger edge security due to limited visibility.

### ZKP Specifications and Implementations

The team discussed ZKP (Zero-Knowledge Proof) specifications and constructions, with Mitchell presenting details about gadgets, constructions, and proofs including key binding, membership verification, and range proofs. The group reviewed how to implement and review the specifications, with Mitchell explaining the process for building and testing the ZKBook branch locally. They confirmed that the ZKP task force would own construction 007 related to cross-spec linkage in CredSpec Issue 9, and agreed to handle asynchronous reviews of adjacent work rather than blocking it. Scott announced that Leif from Ciro Foundation and WW Wallet would join the next meeting on the 29th to discuss performance tests across major ZKP families.
