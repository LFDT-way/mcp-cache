### Dossier Specification and Evidence Structure

Daniel discussed his work on dossier specifications and shared his plans to expand the spec during the holidays. He explained his approach to structuring dossiers, proposing to use attributes blocks for metadata and edges for evidence. Daniel outlined two contrasting use cases: a simple Verifiable Voice Protocol dossier and a complex NTSB crash scene investigation. He also introduced the concept of "joint issuance" or endorsement, where multiple parties can sign off on evidence without a traditional multi-sig arrangement.

### Multi-sig vs. Joint Issuance Models

Daniel and Samuel discussed the differences between multi-sig and joint issuance models, emphasizing the need for loose coordination in the latter. They explored the concept of a "loosely coordinated signature" and the idea of introducing phases or degrees of endorsement to data. The conversation also touched on the importance of modeling relationships between pieces of evidence, such as siblings in a dossier, and the need for a system that can handle partial revocation of evidence while maintaining the overall validity of a dossier based on the preponderance of evidence.

### Multi-Objective Decision-Making Techniques

The group discussed the use of multi-attribute, multi-constraint, and multi-objective decision-making techniques in uncertain environments, including the use of labeled property graphs for reasoning in real-world scenarios. Samuel clarified the distinction between property graphs and labeled property graphs, explaining that they are often used interchangeably. Daniel shared various use cases for ACDCs, including NTSB crash investigations, mortgage applications, investigative journalism, scientific research, and law enforcement, emphasizing the need for a schema to address chain of custody issues across different types of dossiers.

### Chain of Custody Schema Development

Daniel discussed the development of a chain of custody schema and the potential to model it in a generic way applicable to various scenarios. He highlighted the need to consider privacy constraints, particularly in cases involving confidential sources, and suggested adding a statement in the dossier specification about selective disclosures. Samuel emphasized the importance of graduated disclosure mechanisms in the context of ACDCs and suggested introducing a new edge operator for endorsement thresholds. Daniel expressed interest in exploring new edge operators and mentioned plans to write new sections on these topics over the holidays, inviting others to collaborate or take ownership of specific areas.

### Dossier System Development Framework

The group discussed developing a dossier system, with Daniel proposing a framework for engagement where participants could contribute to a document with placeholders. Kent shared his interest in the operator section, specifically focusing on political reputation management for law enforcement officers moving between precincts, and mentioned his work with the Utah County Republican Party. Samuel suggested viewing dossiers as reputation for evidence, and recommended reading articles by Daniel and Randy Warshaw on evidence.
