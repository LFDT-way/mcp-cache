### Decentralized Trust Graph Simplification Proposal

The meeting focused on discussing a proposal to simplify the design of decentralized trust graph credentials, including personhood credentials (PHCs) and verifiable relationship credentials (VRCs). Drummond presented a new proposal that restricts credentials to two DIDs (source and target) and uses RDIDs for privacy purposes. Michael raised concerns about the proposal's implementation and suggested using proof sets as a universal mechanism. The group discussed the differences between VRCs and VPCs (verifiable persona credentials), with Drummond explaining that VPCs can be used to assert personas that are shared across relationships. The conversation ended with an agreement to schedule more time for Michael to present his proof set proposal in the next meeting, and Drummond committing to add documentation to the existing proposal.
