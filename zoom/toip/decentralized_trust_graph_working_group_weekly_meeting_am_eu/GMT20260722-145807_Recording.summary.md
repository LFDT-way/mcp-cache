The team discussed the VTA (Verifiable Trust Agent) system and its capabilities for delegating control of digital identities without giving up custodial ownership. The group explored potential use cases including enterprise AI applications, healthcare data management, and government e-citizen systems, with Nicky suggesting opportunities for digital kinship and intergenerational support through the VTA framework. The next special topic session will feature Jeff and Glenn discussing VTA forms.
