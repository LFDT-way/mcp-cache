### Decentralized Trust Graph Ecosystem

The group discussed the scope and structure of a decentralized trust graph ecosystem, focusing on two main types of credentials: personhood credentials (issued to individuals) and verifiable relationship credentials (which can be between any two parties, including people, devices, and AI agents). Drummond clarified that the trust graph is built around personhood credentials, while verifiable relationship credentials provide a second trust signal. The discussion also touched on risk assessment in the ecosystem, with Kevin raising a question about analyzing multiple risks simultaneously, which Scott acknowledged as something to consider while maintaining a broad initial perspective.

### Risk Assessment for Verifiable Credentials

The meeting focused on risk assessment for verifiable credentials and relationship credentials. Scott and Drummond discussed various categories of risks, including credential storage, privacy, trust communities, and infrastructure risks. Brendan presented initial findings from recent sessions on verifiable relationship credentials (VRCs) and witnessed VRCs, distinguishing between relationship credentials and endorsements. The group agreed to continue discussions on risk assessment and to collaborate on developing specifications for VRCs. Drummond mentioned plans for a workshop in Berlin next week and suggested setting up a repository for sharing work on VRCs. The conversation ended with a brief overview of witnessed VRCs, which adds a layer of trust by involving witnesses or notaries in the credential exchange process.
