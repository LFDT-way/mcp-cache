### PSC OpenAC Presentation Overview

Zoey led a team presentation introducing PSC (Privacy Stewards of Ethereum), an R&D lab for applied zero-knowledge proofs that has been working on ZK technologies for 7 years. The team discussed their work on OpenAC, an open and lightweight anonymous credential system designed to address cryptographer feedback about SD jots and salted hashes being non-unlinkable. They highlighted that OpenAC provides unlinkability to credentials and is compatible with existing systems, with the team currently working on generalized predicates and post-quantum upgrades.

### OpenAC Cryptography Credential System

Vivian presented OpenAC, a cryptography-based credential system that uses standard ECDSA P256 and includes features like device binding and modular support for different document types. She demonstrated the system's two main phases - prepare and show - and explained its privacy features including blinding processes. The presentation included benchmarks showing proof generation times of 99 milliseconds for blind processing and 30 milliseconds for verification, and discussed the system's developer-friendly approach with integration taking less than 2 weeks. The meeting experienced technical difficulties with screen sharing that prevented Vikas from presenting his portion.

### OpenAC Zero-Knowledge Proof Solution

The meeting focused on a comprehensive presentation of OpenAC, a zero-knowledge proof (ZKP) solution for verifiable credentials. The team discussed OpenAC's key features, including its secure-by-construction design without trusted setup, support for multiple signing algorithms, and optimization for mobile devices. They outlined plans to add support for multi-VC linking, post-quantum solutions, and revocation features over the next 3-6 months. The team also addressed questions about interoperability with different blockchain methods, key types, and complex data types. Drummond announced plans to establish ZKP and UX task forces with dedicated repositories for ongoing collaboration.
