## Details

### TrustGraph Working Group Meeting Update

The Decentralized TrustGraph Working Group meeting began with Drummond introducing the agenda and noting that some members were attending or speaking at the concurrent Patchwork event. Martina was scheduled to present a credential relationship map, followed by a discussion of a new proposal from Brandon and Alberto on DTD credentials version 0.02, and Nikki was set to provide an update on risk assessment and harms. The meeting also included a reminder about the antitrust policy notice and an invitation for new attendees to introduce themselves.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Overview

Martina presented an overview of a community credentialing system, explaining how personal credentials (PHCs), verifiable relationship credentials (VRCs), and verifiable persona credentials work together to build a decentralized trust graph. She demonstrated how community members can establish private channels and exchange credentials, with the system allowing for both direct relationships and shared personas. The presentation aimed to provide a shared understanding of the credentialing process and identify potential questions for further discussion.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Discussion

The meeting focused on the Decentralized Trust Graph, presented by Martina, which visualizes community relationships and trust connections. Roman and Drummond discussed the graph's features, including the ability to form verifiable relationship credentials (VRCs) independent of community managers. Reza inquired about the role of community managers and the persistence of connections, which Drummond explained is based on peer-to-peer relationships rather than platform-dependent connections. The group also touched on the concept of personas and how trust relationships persist even if a community or persona changes. The conversation ended with Brendan and Alberto preparing to present their topic, with Drummond allocating 25 minutes for their presentation and Q&A.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Specification Update

Brendan presented a draft specification for version 0.2 of the Decentralized Trust Graph (DTG), which includes new credential types like personhood credentials and community credentials. The specification was developed based on previous discussions and proposals, particularly from Drummond. Key features include a simplified credential structure, pairwise private and unique DIDs, and various credential types such as relationship credentials, persona credentials, and endorsements. The group discussed questions about liveness detection, personhood credentials, and the relationship between community DIDs and personhood DIDs. Nicky provided a brief update on the risk assessment work, which is progressing well. The conversation ended with a reminder about the upcoming holiday break and an invitation to join the next meeting next week.
