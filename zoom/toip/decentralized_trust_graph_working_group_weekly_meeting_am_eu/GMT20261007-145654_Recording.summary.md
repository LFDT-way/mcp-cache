## Overview

The October 7th meeting of the Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group focused on updates from the recent workshop and presentation in Prague. Drummond shared a report on the successful demonstration of OpenVTC and its integration with GitHub to the Linux kernel team, which was well-received. ## Details

### Prague Site Overview Meeting

Drummond shared his screen to show the view of Prague, including Vishrad and Prague Castle, as well as the Prague Congress Center. He mentioned the good weather, allowing the team to eat lunch outside. The meeting began at 4 PM with Nicky taking notes and helping with screen sharing.

### Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group

Drummond led the October 7th meeting of the Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group, welcoming participants including guest David Vogel. The team reviewed task force progress, with Sankeshan reporting on efforts to upstream capabilities from his repository into the main task force repository, and Glenn highlighting positive feedback about the infrastructure's maturity and safety. The meeting included plans to share updates from recent presentations to the Linux kernel team and a workshop, with additional reports from the HTX Human Trust Experience Task Force pending.

### VTA Setup and Progress Updates

Daniel provided an update on the VTA setup, noting improvements made based on previous feedback and discussing the need for user testing with both technical and non-technical users. The team also explored potential collaborations with a Swiss digital identity working group and emphasized the importance of working asynchronously to improve meeting efficiency. Mitchell reported progress on the implementation guide for the Spec Up T guide, which is nearly ready for review and integration with ZKP. Drummond shared details about a recent presentation at the Plumber Summit, highlighting the successful demo of OpenVTC and positive feedback from key stakeholders, including Greg Crow-Hartman from the kernel team.

### OpenVTC Onboarding Milestone Achievement

Glenn announced the successful completion of a full OpenVTC onboarding process with post-quantum cryptography and GitHub integration, attributing significant contributions to Jeff, Brendan Alberto, and the Keyring team. He highlighted the acceleration in development velocity and emphasized the importance of community effort in reaching this milestone. Glenn proposed shifting focus to driving utility of VTCs and suggested holding workshops to address complex issues like partitioning, trust context, and user experience. He also recommended moving away from discussing VTA as a tool and instead focusing on higher-level capabilities offered by VTC and VTN.

### Trust Agents Project Completion Update

The team discussed the successful completion of a project involving trust-enabled agents and the VTA (Verification Trust Agent). Brendan highlighted the need to move forward with use cases and human experience improvements, while Alberto mentioned new features like QR codes and a "My Agent" tab on the OpenBTC website. The team expressed gratitude for the collaboration and looked forward to demonstrating the progress at an upcoming meeting next week.

### OpenVTC and Community Projects Update

Grace shared her excitement about the progress made with OpenVTC, particularly its integration with GitHub, and expressed interest in onboarding communities for a proof of concept. Drummond and the team discussed the significance of the Linux kernel adoption and the potential impact on broader community adoption. David Bovo introduced his project, Hitchhiker's Guide to Earth, which aims to create a community-based guide publishing platform using decentralized trust graphs and Raspberry Pis, with plans to launch on November 5th and present at Transmediale in Berlin. The team expressed interest in David's proposal to present at the Chaos Computer Club conference and agreed to follow up with volunteers to coordinate the submission.
