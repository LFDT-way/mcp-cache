## Details

### Decentralized Trans Graph Working Group

Drummond welcomed attendees to the September 23rd meeting of the Decentralized Trans Graph Working Group, a joint working group of Trust over IP and DIF. Stephanie introduced herself as head of operations and global partnerships at GLIANET Alliance, noting she was invited by Alberto and was attending as an observer.

### JEV Classifier Model Announcement

Steve announced a new classifier model called JEV for fiduciary agents, describing it as "manna from heaven" due to its ease of implementation with the Iron Curtain Policy Gate. Drummond mentioned upcoming announcements around fiduciary AI and welcomed Stephanie to the meeting. The group discussed upcoming events including a demo of zero-knowledge proofs (ZKP) and participation in the Linux Plumbers Conference and LF Open Source EU in Prague, where they will present on barefoot trust infrastructure and verifiable trust communities.

### Healthcare Privacy Panel and Updates

Vikas announced his attendance at the United Nations Digital Cooperation Day and the Convo TEX event in Boston, where he will lead a panel on privacy in decentralized healthcare. The Verifiable Private Knowledge Working Group has resumed meetings on Mondays. Sankarshan released an updated toolkit aligned with VTI spec work and plans to review and refresh the documentation, while Nikki reported that risk assessment and harm prevention meetings will resume fortnightly next week.

### Credentials Specification Progress Update

The team discussed progress on the credentials specification, with Geoff reporting that they reached working draft version 0.5 and merged changes including verifiable statement credentials (VSC) which now serves as a parent type for witness and endorsement credentials. Drummond expressed enthusiasm about the VSC concept and its potential for enabling communities to define their own assertion vocabularies. The discussion touched on governance framework implementation, with Glenn explaining that while policies could potentially have DIDs, using trust tasks with different service endpoints would be more practical to avoid discovery issues. The team confirmed they are using SpecUp T for all specifications but harmonization of glossaries will be addressed as specifications mature.

### Documentation Acronym Usage Discussion

Steve raised concerns about the overuse of three-letter acronyms in their documentation, suggesting they should use full terms like "V-mem" instead of "VMC" to reduce confusion. Drummond acknowledged Steve's feedback and noted it for future consideration. Glenn reported progress on Trust House, with the type being loosened to accept any URI instead of just HTTPS entries. Drummond highlighted a key demand from the bioregional conference for dynamic data sharing into data trusts, which their system could potentially support.

### ZKP Specification Progress Update

Mitchell reported significant progress on the ZKP spec, including splitting it into cryptographic background and implementation guide components, and creating 63 formal Lean proofs for different constructions and gadgets. The team discussed plans for a demo and ongoing work on vetting flows, with some team members working to set up their VTCs and VTAs to improve UX and flows. Nicky mentioned upcoming work on the Plumbers' Conference and discussion about TADA, including Glenn's interest in mental models and Narrative Agency concepts for identity assertion.

### Predicate Credential System Demonstration

Glenn demonstrated a Predicate Credential System (PCS) for Linux kernel onboarding that uses zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs) to protect vetter identities and prevent correlation between vetting requests and acceptance decisions. The system allows vetters to issue credentials through a ticket system and enables applicants to prove they've been vetted by multiple different people without revealing vetter identities. Drummond announced that the next meeting will focus on planning the half-day workshop at the Plumbers Conference, which will include interactive demonstrations of the Decentralized Trust Graph system and hands-on community joining exercises.
