### Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group

New members, including Kevin and Brian, introduced themselves, with Kevin highlighting his background in tech and PR, and Brian mentioning his involvement in AI and interest in the group's work. ### Linux Foundation Task Force Updates

Drummond discussed the formation of two new task forces: the Credentials Task Force and the RCards Task Force. Jeff Turk explained that the Credentials Task Force would focus on defining personal and verifiable relationship credentials for the Linux Foundation project, aiming to replace the current PGP web of trust with a more scalable system. Drummond outlined the process for forming task forces, including creating a wiki page and signing up for an account. The RCards Task Force was introduced as a way to establish interoperability standards for relationship cards, which are shared signed data objects between parties. Drummond agreed to help create the wiki pages for both task forces.

### Risk Assessment Guide Discussion

Scott explained the purpose of the Risk Assessment Companion Guide and the task force's role in addressing threats and risks associated with their current project. He emphasized the importance of establishing context and understanding the ecosystem to determine what can be controlled and mitigated. Scott invited Drummond to take notes and participate actively in the discussion, highlighting the need for a clear understanding of the ecosystem's boundaries to communicate effectively with relying parties.

### Charter Review and Risk Assessment

Nicky proposed reviewing the charter and suggested a timeline with milestones, including a landscaping exercise in October to identify key risks and deliver insights monthly. Scott emphasized separating the risk assessment for the ecosystem from developing requirements to address identified risks, which would be an input for the next task force phase.

### Risk Assessment Process Timeline

Scott proposed a two-step process for addressing risks, with the first step involving task forces defining their requirements within six weeks. Drummond suggested a timeframe for a public review draft, but emphasized that the final decision should be made by the task force. Scott highlighted the importance of collaboration and compromise in the risk assessment process, aiming to complete it before the holiday break, ideally by mid-December. Russ agreed with the December 15th target date.

### Project Funding Exploration Discussion

The group discussed potential funding mechanisms for their project, with Drummond explaining that while the Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust currently doesn't provide grants, the First Person Project is actively seeking funding for a digital network cooperative model that could eventually help provide small grants to advance the work. Reza shared that Kwai has historically offered grant funding through hackathons, including a recent event with $10,000 in prizes, and suggested exploring Web3 funding opportunities in the decentralized world.

### Integrating Harm Prevention in Risk Assessment

Drummond and Nicky discussed integrating harm prevention work into Scott's risk assessment process. Nicky proposed using a social design process, which includes participatory events and storytelling techniques, to identify and understand both harms and benefits. She suggested developing a toolkit for stakeholders to use in collaborative sessions, with the goal of feeding insights back into the risk assessment process. Scott expressed interest in this approach and mentioned plans to develop a risk assessment profile that includes human harm risks.

### Risk Assessment Process Separation Strategy

Scott emphasized the need to separate risk assessment from threats and harms, as risk assessment affects various requirement categories and should be conducted as a distinct task. He suggested completing the risk assessment process first and then validating the results annually, rather than combining it with threats and harms analysis. Scott also highlighted the importance of keeping the risk assessment process clean and efficient to allow for focused discussions within specific task forces.

### Risk Assessment Strategy Planning Meeting

The group discussed the scope and purpose of risk assessments for a project, with Jonathan emphasizing the need to consider both potential harms and benefits, particularly in communicating with the public. Scott explained that the primary audience for risk assessments is both internal and external stakeholders, aiming to establish a governance framework and build trust in the ecosystem. Drummond suggested that Scott and Nikki offline determine whether one or two task forces should be formed, with plans to finalize the charter and work plans for review at the next meeting in two weeks.
