## Overview

The meeting focused on developing a confidence metric system for personal data profiles in travel and hospitality contexts, exploring how to handle self-attested information and assess its reliability. The participants also addressed the importance of understanding individual preferences and limitations in various activities, as well as the need for machine-readable content standardization to facilitate better service experiences.
