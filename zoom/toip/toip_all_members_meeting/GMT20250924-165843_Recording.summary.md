The symposium was confirmed for November 19th and 20th, with registration opening soon and the first round of selection happening at the October 2nd Ecosystem and Governance Working Group Meeting. The Ecosystem and Governance Working Group highlighted recent speakers, including Kyle Robinson discussing BC Gov's governance frameworks and Christine Sani addressing policy enforcement, and called for more submissions from the audience.

Carly discussed the release of five new vertical format videos for Bite Size Trust on YouTube, which have received 200-700 views each. Carly also mentioned working with the Linux Foundation to add the content to their educational materials and website, though she needed help with WordPress. The Trust Revenue Task Force was moving forward with their second public review draft of the TRQP spec and implementation work was ongoing at the IRA Association.

### Trust Manning Protocol Progress Update

The Trust Manning Protocol Task Force is making progress, with no major issues remaining in the specification, which is expected to be finalized in Q4 alongside the implementation. Wenjing mentioned that meetings may become less frequent, potentially moving to monthly or online-only, and a comprehensive presentation on TSP will be given at the upcoming symposium. Drummond noted that the high assurance vids work is advancing, with a public review draft expected in Q4, and the DidSkin Method Task Force is preparing to submit its specification to the DID Methods Working Group for evaluation in October.
