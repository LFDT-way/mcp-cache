## Details

### Trust Registry Action Framework

Carly and Daniel discussed the implementation of a trust registry, focusing on the need for a list of actions allowed within an ecosystem. Daniel explained that while technical standards exist for actions like signing verifiable credentials, governance questions arise regarding who can add entities to trust lists. They noted that different ecosystems might use varying terminology for the same actions, but PKI standards provide a framework for consistency. Carly suggested the possibility of creating a library of potential actions for others to adopt in their governance frameworks, which she plans to discuss further with Drummond.

### Best Practices and Tech Governance

Darrell and Carly discussed the importance of developing best practices and guidance for technology and governance, emphasizing the need for a vocabulary to bridge these areas. Darrell raised technical issues with accessing LFX Zoom calls on multiple devices, which Drummond addressed by suggesting solutions related to device cookies and calendar links. Drummond also mentioned reviewing PRs for updating the SPAC with park revisions, noting some editorial suggestions he had made.

### Specification Changes and Clarifications

Drummond reviewed changes to the specification, noting simplifications made to authorization recognition queries and discussing the repositioning of the park model section. He proposed moving the park model explanation back to the start of its section to better inform subsequent discussions. Drummond also clarified the context object requirements, emphasizing that while a context object is optional, it must include an authority ID, and explained the relationship between time values and time-based conditions.

### Authorization Schema Clarification Discussion

Darrell and Drummond discussed the schema definition for authorization requests, clarifying that the context parameter is optional and must be a JSON object if included. They agreed to update the documentation to reflect this and to change the term "API" to "authorization query" for clarity. Drummond also suggested adjusting the terminology to avoid confusion, and Darrell agreed with the changes.

### Specification Document Editorial Updates

Drummond and Darrell discussed editorial changes to a specification document, including reordering sections to prioritize authorization over recognition and addressing formatting inconsistencies. They agreed that SpecUpT updates were not necessary for public review, as the changes were cosmetic. Drummond offered to help with the SpecUpT update and references section, suggesting three separate pull requests to complete the work.

### TrustRP Symposium Timeline Discussion

Drummond and Darrell discussed the timeline for completing the TrustRP symposium materials, with a focus on having the editorial work done by the end of the weekend. They agreed to have Darrell's portion of the presentation ready to point out the progress on Public Review 02, and Drummond proposed working with Hank to update the spec codebase. The goal was to release Public Review 2.0 before the end of the month, hold it during December, and incorporate any feedback in January. Darrell noted that while Drummond and he had been leading the conversation, they should consider other perspectives on the timeline.

### Trust Registry Release Strategy

The team discussed the urgency of releasing their trust registry work, with Scott emphasizing the need to get it reviewed and published quickly to prevent others from developing similar solutions. Drummond shared insights from a meeting with Fabrice about the growing number of registries expected by 2026, particularly in the IRA and adjacent spaces. Scott mentioned plans to present their work to COG for potential integration into conformance mandates, particularly regarding external trust registries in the music industry. The team also discussed a new Conformance Explorer tool created by a C2PA colleague, which provides a human-readable way to review trust registries.

### Trust Registry Protocol Development Review

The group discussed the Trust Registry Career Protocol (TRQP) and its capabilities, including its ability to enable tools for browsing and exploring trust relationships, as demonstrated by examples from Google and FIDEs in the Netherlands. Darrell emphasized the importance of making the protocol accessible and usable by humans, while Drummond highlighted the need for editorial recommendations and cosmetic changes to the specification. The team agreed on the urgency of pushing the protocol forward, with Darrell suggesting a faster review process through Discord, and Drummond stressing that the current draft represents a significant evolutionary step by separating assertion statements into action and resource.

### Trust Registry Simplification Update

The team discussed the simplification of their approach to trust registries, which has made explanations easier and is being implemented by Kevin before year-end. They agreed to skip next week's meeting due to scheduling conflicts, including a symposium and AGM, and will instead provide updates via Discord and push for vote recommendations to TSWG during the following week.
