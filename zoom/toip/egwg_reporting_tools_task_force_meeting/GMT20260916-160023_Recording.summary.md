## Agenda

📣 New Reporting Tools Task Force
The EGWG has approved a new Task Force to help streamline reporting from ToIP Working Groups and Task Forces to the Technical Advisory Committee (TAC), while also supporting preparation for our next ToIP AGM on October 28, 2026.
The objective is to develop optional templates, guidance, and AI-assisted tools (prompts, skills, workflows) that make it easier for WGs and TFs to provide structured updates that can be reused for both TAC reports and AGM materials.
Rather than relying on a few final authors who search and compile information, we'd like to create a process where WG and TF leads can contribute updates in a consistent format that can be efficiently collated into reports and presentations.

### AI Tool for Review Reports

Carly and Sankarshan discussed implementing an AI-powered tool to help working group chairs generate annual review reports. Sankarshan clarified that creating these reports should be part of the chair's regular responsibilities rather than additional work. They agreed that chairs could either manually input the required information or use an AI tool to automatically synthesize data from Confluence and GitHub, with the process expected to take about one to two hours.

### AI Reporting System Discussion

Carly and Sankarshan discussed reporting processes and a potential LFDT-wide AI system for generating reports. Sankarshan suggested focusing on near-term solutions to avoid last-minute scrambles while considering the AI system as a mid-term goal. Carly agreed to come up with a proposal and mentioned discussing it with Thomas, noting that the AI system would not be required for information submission.

### AI Skills Offerings Discussion

Carly and Sankarshan discussed the sufficiency of current AI skills offerings, with Sankarshan noting that Netflix provides adequate skills to address initial questions. Carly mentioned she would bring up this topic at the steering committee meeting later that evening. Sankarshan also observed that messy information reporting appears to be a broader issue across LF, not just specific to their work.
