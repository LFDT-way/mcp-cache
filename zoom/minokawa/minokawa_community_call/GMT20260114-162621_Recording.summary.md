The team discussed documentation approaches for language specifications, with Parisa proposing LaTeX as a long-term solution but agreeing it wasn't a current priority due to documentation being behind schedule. The team also discussed the need for a Compact Improvement Process (COIP) similar to MIPs, which Kevin explained would be a fork of the MIP process with different stream numbers and editors.
