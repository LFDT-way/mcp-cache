### Logging Function Naming Decision

The team discussed the naming for a logging function, comparing different options including "log", "event", "emit", and "publish". After considering factors like semantic clarity and existing terminology in Solidity, they decided to use "emit" as the keyword, aligning with Solidity's convention and addressing concerns about potential confusion with other terms. Dominik noted that the original rejection of "emit" was a spontaneous decision made by the team present at the time, and since there was no strong objection to using "emit", they agreed to proceed with that choice.

### Provable Circuits and Impact Code

The team discussed the definition of provable circuits and impact code, clarifying that log instructions now generate impact code and should be included in provable circuits. They determined that log operations are considered side effects, making pure circuits impossible to define as circuits without side effects. The discussion concluded with updates on Compact runtime releases, where different downstream components have varying needs - some requiring internal releases while others prefer public releases of the Compact compiler.
