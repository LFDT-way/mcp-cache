The discussion highlighted the need for a decoupling point between compiler output and on-chain runtime to allow compiler updates without requiring contract redeployments.

The team discussed challenges around compiler upgrades and their impact on verifier keys for blockchain contracts. Andrzej raised concerns about the tight coupling between compilers and on-chain runtimes, seeking to identify where a boundary could be established to allow recompilation with different runtimes while maintaining compatibility with existing verifier keys.
