### Ledger Release Strategy Discussion

The team discussed whether another Ledger 8 release of Compact is needed, with Kent confirming that Ledger 9 networks won't be available until the end of Q3 rather than Q2. Parisa proposed releasing Compact 0.32 as the final Ledger 8-only version, followed by a new release supporting events and composable contracts on Ledger 9. The group agreed they would likely need separate development branches for Ledger 8 and Ledger 9, with Kent suggesting they should wait until the end of Q2 before creating a development branch for Ledger 8.

### Q2 Release Planning Discussion

The team discussed release planning for Q2, deciding to branch for a Ledger 8 release by the end of the week to include SECP256K1 curve and ECDSA verification features. They agreed to release version 0.33 after incorporating Ledger 9 and composable contracts, with a potential Ledger 8 release planned for early Q3. Kent expressed concerns about workload and timeline constraints, emphasizing the need to avoid rushing multiple releases between now and the quarter's end on the 26th.

### Version Numbering and Release Planning

The team discussed version numbering and release planning, with Kevin explaining they would bump the version number on main and tag it, only creating another Ledger 8 developer branch if needed. Kent and Kevin clarified that they are using the pre-1.0 period to make breaking changes without deprecation processes, as they want to get it right before expanding user base. The team confirmed that public releases are planned for Q3, with current releases being internal alpha/beta versions, and Joseph raised questions about QA processes for events which Matthew explained is handled by Giuseppe.

### COIP Document Updates Discussion

The team discussed updates to the COIP (Contract Interface Proposal) document, focusing on purity declarations, subtyping rules, and disclosure requirements for cross-contract calls. They agreed that pure circuits don't require argument disclosure, while impure circuits do, and clarified that asserts don't automatically make circuits impure. The group decided to number this as COIP2 and move it to draft status, with Kevin volunteering to handle the administrative task of merging the PR. They also settled on using "contract interface" as the syntax, though some members expressed mixed opinions about this choice.
