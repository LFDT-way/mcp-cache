### Credit Union Project Status Metrics

The TAC discussed how to move forward with graduated credit union projects beyond current OpenSSF scorecards. Hendrik and Jessica explained that the community is looking for additional metrics to work toward, such as bronze, silver, and gold status levels, with diversity and community engagement being key factors beyond technical metrics. Marcus suggested considering external validation systems like OpenSSF scorecards rather than creating new internal classification systems, while Matthew supported having more granularity in project lifecycle stages but warned about potential misinterpretation of status levels.

### Project Quality Badge System Discussion

The team discussed implementing a quality mark or badge system for projects, with Hendrik suggesting a tiered approach like "incubation, graduation, and diamond" to recognize projects' strengths in specific directions. Arun and Ramakrishna explained that previous discussions had determined badges were not needed due to existing metrics in the scorecard and LFX insights, as the current system was deemed adequate without adding burden to TAC and maintainers. The group agreed that any additional recognition should be additive rather than a graduated ranking system, focusing on positive indicators like uptake and multi-organizational contributions beyond meeting basic success criteria.

### TAC Project Funding and Compliance

Rafael presented two requests to the TAC: assistance with project marketing to increase maintainer diversity, and funding for independent maintainers. The group discussed scheduling conflicts between TAC calls and Ethereum Core Dev calls, with Matthew proposing to create a governance issue for voting on potentially moving the TAC meeting time. Hart provided an overview of new requirements for European compliance with the CRA regulations, explaining that open source projects with commercial use must implement security vulnerability disclosure processes and report vulnerabilities to EU platforms.
