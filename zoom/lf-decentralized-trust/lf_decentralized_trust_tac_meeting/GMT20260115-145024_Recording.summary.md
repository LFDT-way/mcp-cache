### Streamlining Annual Review Process

The TAC discussed overdue reports, noting that the Trust Over IP team will not submit their Q3 report but will continue submitting future reports in the new format. They agreed to allow the Calipers project to skip their Q4 report due to a lab proposal. The team reviewed upcoming reports for the Fabric and Cacti projects, due next week, and reminded members to fill out conflict sheets if they have any. Enrique suggested reflecting on how to streamline the annual review process, which has been taking over 4-5 months in the past. Arun noted that annual reviews were previously done quarterly but were changed to annual reviews to provide deeper insights, though this has led to long review durations.

### Mentorship Program Implementation Discussion

The TAC discussed implementing a mentorship program for new members, with Kevin advocating for formal assigned mentors while Matthew suggested informal check-ins. Marcus proposed regular Discord sessions or coffee meetups to facilitate peer-to-peer learning. Arun noted that mentorship could help new members navigate the annual review process and provide guidance on project-related questions. The group agreed to explore setting up mentorship opportunities and recognition programs for contributors, with a focus on making the onboarding process more welcoming for new projects and community members.

### Mentorship and Project Growth Strategies

The TAC discussed two main themes: mentorship programs and project growth/health. They explored ways to improve mentorship for new maintainers and discussed challenges faced by new projects, including Kevin's experience with Minokawa. The group agreed to consider adding guidelines for open source collaboration to project requirements. They also discussed the need to better showcase cross-project collaboration and diversity in project participation. The TAC decided to consider an alternating meeting schedule to accommodate different time zones, with Ry agreeing to investigate the logistics of this change.
