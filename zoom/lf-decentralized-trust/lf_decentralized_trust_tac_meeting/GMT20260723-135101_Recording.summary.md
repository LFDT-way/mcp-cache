### Sub-Project Maturity Levels Discussion

The meeting discussed sub-project maturity levels, specifically focusing on the distinction between Fabric and Fabric X, and the evolution of the Iroha project. Arun explained that while the Fabric team requested treating Fabric and Fabric X as different lifecycle stages, the current charter treats them as one project. The discussion also addressed the pattern of parallel developments in the Iroha project, with Iroha 2 and Iroha 3 showing minimal activity while new developments occur in parallel. The meeting raised questions about how to handle such parallel developments within the community and whether to enforce guidelines prohibiting continued graduation status while working on new parallel stacks.

### Umbrella Project Maturity Evaluation Challenges

Marcus and Diane discussed challenges in evaluating the lifecycle of umbrella projects and their sub-projects. Marcus raised questions about how to determine the maturity status of umbrella projects with overlapping sub-projects, while Diane shared her perspective from a HIRO project context, noting that release cycles and production readiness are key factors in determining whether a sub-project should be considered graduated. Both participants acknowledged the complexity of these decisions and the need to consider project history and adoption when making determinations about maturity status.

### Umbrella Project Sub-Project Management

Yoav proposed a solution for handling multiple sub-projects under an umbrella project like Fabric, suggesting that each sub-project should have its own maturity level judgment while sharing common APIs and repositories. He recommended that the umbrella project could list its sub-projects and indicate their individual maturity levels, with the overall status of the umbrella project being determined by the union of its sub-projects' status. Yoav also noted that reporting could remain unified for convenience, with individual TSCs making maintenance decisions about maturity levels.

### Project Status Evaluation Criteria

The group discussed how developers evaluate project status when considering adoption, with Diane and Marcus agreeing that specific project details like release status and documentation are more important than general community labels. Marcus noted that the technical team hasn't established clear production readiness criteria beyond general health metrics for projects. Arun raised a concern about competing governance structures within umbrella projects, suggesting that unified TSCs would help provide clearer project status information.

### Fabric Governance Structure Discussion

The discussion focused on governance challenges within the Fabric umbrella project, which includes both Fabric and FabricX sub-projects at different development stages. Yoav explained that while the projects currently coexist collaboratively with unified community calls, there are concerns about decision-making mechanisms and transparency for users. Marcus clarified that the Fabric umbrella project charter includes an Ecosystem Committee (EC) that represents all sub-project TSCs, providing a unified governance structure above individual TSCs. Arun proposed potential solutions including having a single TSC for the umbrella project with internal differentiation, or treating Fabric as a mature project with different naming conventions for technical maturity levels.

### Ecosystem Sub-Project Evaluation Framework

The TAC discussed how to evaluate and handle sub-projects within ecosystem projects, particularly in the context of Fabric and similar umbrella projects. Arun raised concerns about messaging clarity and security auditing for sub-projects, while Yoav suggested creating a clear mechanism to signal sub-project status and decisions. Diane proposed adding sub-project health as a section in annual reports to help the TAC monitor new projects and provide guidance on when splits might be necessary. The group agreed to continue this discussion in next week's meeting.
