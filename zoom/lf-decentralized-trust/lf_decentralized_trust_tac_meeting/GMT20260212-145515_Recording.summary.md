### Trust Council Meeting: Policies and Reviews

The LF Decentralized Trust Technical Advisory Council meeting covered several key topics. Arun reminded participants about the mandate trust policy and encouraged feedback and collaboration. He announced the developer newsletter and the ongoing mentorship program, with a deadline of March 13 for project submissions. The meeting also addressed quarterly reports, with Diane inquiring about the number of reviewers needed for acceptance. Arun mentioned that at least 6 reviews are required. The group discussed the need for timely reviews of Trust Over IP reports to allow for approval at the steering committee meeting. Finally, Arun encouraged TAC members to review and present on annual reports in the next meeting, aiming to cover at least 3-4 reports in detail.

### CACTI Project Status Update

Hendrik provided an overview of the CACTI project, noting its stability and continued development with maintainers from Fujitsu, IBM, and Procdemon. He highlighted the project's real-world usage and mentioned that previous issues related to the move from Hyper-get to Hyper-getCTI have been resolved. The goal for the coming year is to create a new major release, version 3, which seems achievable due to the removal of blockers and good scores on the OpenSSF scorecard. Hendrik concluded that the project is in a good state and no specific requests or help are needed from the tech team at this point.

### Cacti Project Streamlining Initiative

The Cacti Project team discussed streamlining efforts, focusing on reducing the number of connectors to improve efficiency and address security vulnerabilities. Rama explained that the project is shifting from breadth to depth, prioritizing actively maintained connectors and aligning with emerging standards. The team agreed to hold off on approving the project report until the next meeting and requested additional approvals via GitHub pull requests. Arun encouraged participants to review January's discussion summary and prioritize goals for the year, suggesting the formation of task forces to address specific objectives.

### LFX Insights Tool Enhancement Discussion

The team discussed the need to improve the LFX Insights tool, with Diane and Hendrik agreeing to wait for a session with the LFX team before deciding whether to form a task force. Arun suggested bringing up LFX Insights progress in regular staff check-ins, and the group discussed the importance of fixing existing issues before adding new features. The team also touched on the need to measure progress for trust over IP reporting, with Arun proposing to explore this further through brainstorming sessions.

### Annual Reports and Mentorship Plans

The group discussed feedback on quarterly reports and the need for more detailed annual reports, with Diane offering to help develop a template and potentially join a task force on trust over IP standards. Arun raised the topic of a mentorship program for TAC members and maintainers, which Sophie questioned, leading to a consensus to prioritize it without opposition. Hendrik suggested a Q&A or office hours format for mentorship, indicating a preference for a less formal approach than a task force.

### Translation, Community, and AI Trust

The TAC discussed several topics including documentation translation activities, regional community engagement, and AI trust infrastructure. They agreed that translation efforts should be primarily handled at the project level, with Hendrik suggesting focusing on static documentation that doesn't change frequently. The group explored ways to better leverage regional communities, with Sophie sharing her success in attracting developers from diverse regions. Regarding AI trust infrastructure, Hart explained that the LF is currently letting projects do what they want with AI, but encourages documentation of AI usage. The TAC agreed to prioritize a few key items and leave comments in the GitHub discussion, with Arun promising to share a follow-up document. They also mentioned upcoming Lab Steward program openings and set the next meeting for 11am Pacific time the following week.
