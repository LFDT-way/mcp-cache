## Overview

The meeting focused on discussing issues and improvements for LFX Insights, an analytics platform for Linux Foundation projects. Joanna, the engineering manager for Insights, addressed concerns about data accuracy, including wrong affiliations, missing maintainers, and broken links to historical reports. She explained ongoing efforts to improve data quality and promised to investigate specific issues raised by participants. Sophie offered to help debug data extraction processes, and Diane requested a feature to track individual contributors across multiple projects. The team discussed the need for better visualization of maintainer migrations and potential collaboration with the Chaos project for community health metrics. Joanna encouraged participants to submit feature requests and promised to share the public roadmap for future releases.

## Details

### Project Updates and Mentorship Focus

The meeting focused on several key announcements and discussions. Arun welcomed attendees and reminded everyone about antitrust policies and the importance of respectful interactions. He encouraged project teams to use the weekly developer community updates to share their work and seek feedback. Arun also highlighted the ongoing mentorship program, urging TAC members to review project proposals and follow up with any questions. Additionally, he mentioned the need to review annual reports and requested Hendrik's assistance in reminding project teams to submit their reports. The conversation ended with a request for TAC members to review a recently submitted lab proposal and provide feedback.

### Proof of Process Protocol Review

David Condrey presented his proposal for "Proof of Process," a protocol that uses keystroke dynamics and behavioral biometrics to verify human authorship while maintaining privacy. He explained that the protocol generates cryptographic proofs without revealing content, addressing the lack of attestation methods for writers accused of using AI. Arun requested TAC members to review the proposal and provide comments, and David agreed to address any questions through Discord or the proposal's comments section. Joanna from the LFX Insights team provided an update on the onboarding and cleanup of projects, highlighting the need for feedback on data quality and dashboard improvements.

### LFX Insights Data Redirect Issues

Joana discussed the issues raised by Diane regarding broken anchors in LFX Insights and affiliation data. She acknowledged the problem with anchors redirecting to the main page instead of maintaining specific filters and date ranges, and promised to examine examples provided by Diane to improve redirect logic. Joana also explained that while affiliation data is a challenge due to the large number of projects and limited historical data, they are working on allowing users to fix affiliations themselves and incorporating date ranges for affiliation assignments. Arun clarified that the cleanup process applies only to legacy Hyperledger projects, which were moved to their own GitHub orgs as part of a transition, and explained that some projects are still in the process of re-onboarding.

### LFX Insights Data Accuracy Review

The meeting focused on addressing data accuracy issues in LFX Insights, a tool used for tracking vendor neutrality, diversity, and project activity. Hendrik and Diane highlighted several problems with incorrect data, including mislabeled maintainers, missing profiles, and duplicate company listings. Joana acknowledged these issues and explained that while some problems stem from automated processes, the team is actively working to improve data quality. Sophie offered to help investigate specific data extraction issues, and Arun inquired about the possibility of categorizing sub-repositories as sub-projects. The team also discussed potential new features, such as tracking individual contributor activity across multiple projects, and Joana encouraged participants to submit feature requests through GitHub or other channels.
