## Overview

The Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust Technical Advisory Council (TAC) meeting on October 22, 2026, could not be formally called to order due to the absence of both the chair and vice chair, preventing any official votes from taking place. The meeting focused on discussing several project proposals, with the IBC (Inter-Blockchain Communication) proposal being the main item up for consideration. Matthew from Interchain and Barry from Cosmos Labs presented the IBC proposal, which received positive feedback from some TAC members, though Diane indicated she needed more time to review it. Yoav from the Fabric team discussed plans to separate Fabric X into an independent incubating project under a new name to improve clarity for users and align better with LFDT processes. The meeting also covered updates on mid-year reports, upcoming project proposals including Digital Credential Framework and Multipass, and the status of Open Wallet Foundation (OWF) projects transitioning to LFDT. Due to the lack of quorum, the TAC decided to conduct votes on the IBC proposal and other items asynchronously through GitVote rather than during the meeting.

## Details

### Informal Meeting Discussion Session

The meeting could not proceed as a formal session because neither Arun nor Hendrick were present to call the meeting to order, which is required for voting. While participants discussed potential agenda items including VC Profile Validator, Digital Council Framework, and Fabric X, they determined they could only have an informal discussion rather than conduct official business or voting. ### TAC Updates and Nominations

The TAC meeting proceeded without a chair or vice chair present, so formal business could not be conducted. Sean provided updates on nominations for the 2027 Technical Advisory Council, which close on October 30th, and announced new project proposals including Digital Credential Frameworks and Multipass for TAC review in November. Several mid-year reports were discussed, with most projects either submitted or overdue, and a new lab proposal for VC Profile Validator was introduced.

### IBC Proposal TAC Discussion

The TAC meeting focused on discussing the IBC (Inter-Blockchain Communication) proposal, with representatives from Interchain Foundation and Cosmos Labs present to answer questions. Several TAC members expressed interest in the proposal, with Marcus and Matthew Whitehead indicating they were ready to vote in favor, while Diane needed more time to review the proposal due to other commitments. Barry from Cosmos Labs mentioned that an announcement campaign was scheduled to begin on November 2nd, contingent on the proposal's acceptance, and noted that sponsors from both Web3 and traditional finance sectors were participating in the campaign. The group agreed to conduct the vote asynchronously through GitVote rather than during the meeting.

### FabricX Project Separation Proposal

Yoav presented a proposal to separate FabricX from the Fabric umbrella project and establish it as an independent incubating project under a different name to improve clarity for users and align with LFDT processes. The team agreed to add a dated comment to the mid-year report reflecting this proposal without making immediate changes to the current report. The discussion on the EU Cyber Resilience Act proposal and the clarification on project experimentation space were postponed to the next TAC meeting.

### OWF Project Transfer Status Update

The team discussed the status of transferring Open Wallet Foundation (OWF) projects to the Linux Foundation, including domain ownership and trademark issues. Sean and David explained that while most domain names are owned by the LF, some projects like Multipass and Identity Common TS are awaiting trademark and logo approvals before moving forward. The group agreed to focus on reviewing the Digital Credential Framework proposal scheduled for November 5th, with Sean committing to create a slide showing the status of OWF project transfers for the next TAC meeting.
