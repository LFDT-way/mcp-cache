## Overview

### TAC Approves Minnicower Report

The Technical Advisory Council (TAC) reviewed and approved the Minnicower report with a voice vote. Marcus explained that the report recommended keeping the project in incubation status rather than graduated status, and Matthew noted a minor issue about the report serving dual purposes as both an annual and media report. The team agreed to fix typos in the document, and Matthew made the motion to approve the report, which was seconded by Diane and approved by all TAC members present.

### UniFlow Wallet Connector Discussion

Mirko presented UniFlow, an open-source wallet connector developed over a year ago in the OpenWallet Foundation, which helps issuers and verifiers interact with PODI wallets. He explained that the main challenge is building a developer community, as current contributions are limited to small bug fixes due to developers' time constraints. Hendrik highlighted UniFlow's importance in implementing European WAGAT standards for enterprise applications, noting it offers a free, open-source alternative to more expensive solutions. Matthew raised a question about the project's sponsorship structure, specifically questioning whether having the lead maintainer as the sole sponsor was acceptable.

### Project Sponsorship Requirements Discussion

The team discussed project sponsorship requirements and clarified that external sponsorship is already in place through the Open Wallet Foundation, which includes the Entesi Group, German National Project, and Raiffeisenbank International. Mirko explained that the UDPLO project and the Identity Common TypeScript project share the same sponsors and are designed to work together, with reusable components going to the Common TypeScript project while product-specific business logic goes to UDPLO. Arun confirmed that LFDT requires sponsorship from organizations expressing interest in projects, and the discussion concluded with questions about UDPLO's position in the technology stack, though this part of the conversation was cut off in the transcript.

### UDPLO Project Incubation Approval

The TAC discussed and approved the UDPLO project for incubation within the LFDT. Mirko explained that UDPLO is a standalone project that can run without licenses but offers pluggable interfaces for various wallet systems and cloud services, designed to support European EUDI wallet standards. The discussion covered maintainership diversity challenges, with Mirko noting that while over 30 people have contributed code, developer time commitment remains limited due to lack of business models supporting wallet connector development. The TAC voted unanimously to accept UDPLO as an incubating project, with Hendrik and Diane supporting the motion.
