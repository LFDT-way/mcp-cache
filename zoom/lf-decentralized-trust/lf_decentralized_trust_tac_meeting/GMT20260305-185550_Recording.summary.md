## Overview

The TAC LFTT tech meeting focused on reviewing annual reports, with particular attention to Anon Creds and Indy projects. Steven Curran from the Anon Creds community joined to discuss their status, explaining that while Anon Creds V1 is stable and widely used, V2 development is currently stalled due to uncertainty in the zero-knowledge proof landscape. The group reviewed Matthew's assessment of the Indy annual report, which highlighted concerns about OpenSSF security scores, particularly low vulnerability and well-maintained ratings across multiple repositories. The discussion revealed that while Indy Besu shows active development, other core repositories have limited recent activity, though this aligns with the project being largely stable and deployed in production environments. The conversation ended with a brief mention that once Indy Besu completes its final repository migration, attention would turn to planning the migration of the Firefly project to a new organization.
