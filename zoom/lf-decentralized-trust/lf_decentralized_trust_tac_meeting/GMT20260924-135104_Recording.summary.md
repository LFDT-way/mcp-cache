The TAC meeting focused on two main project decisions. The group voted to reclassify the Smoot project as a lab project, with six members voting in favor and one abstaining. Arun announced they would share this decision with the Smoot team and ask them to build the project in labs before incubation. ### Report Review and Extensions

The team discussed the Paladin report, which was submitted late but has now been completed. Enrique emphasized the need for TAC members to review outstanding reports that have been open since August. The group agreed to grant an extension to Loch Ness team's OD report, moving the deadline to the first week of October. The conversation ended with a discussion about project representation to TAC, referencing previous resolutions about unified representation and subgroups within projects.

### Fabric Project Governance Clarification

The TAC discussed concerns about Fabric's mid-year report which claimed different lifecycle statuses for Fabric and FabricX, with Matthew expressing worry that this undermines TAC governance processes. The group agreed that while FabricX appears to be experimental work under the Fabric umbrella, it doesn't necessarily need to be treated as a separate project with its own lifecycle classification. The TAC decided to clarify project definitions in governance documents and requested that future project reports clearly distinguish between the main project's status and any experimental components. Hendrik committed to providing a proposal for requiring SBOM publishing from graduated projects within two weeks, which would be implemented as a "should have" requirement before potentially becoming mandatory.
