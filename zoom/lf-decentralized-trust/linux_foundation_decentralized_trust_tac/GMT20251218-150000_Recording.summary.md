Tracy explained the responsibilities of TAC members, which are outlined in the charter and accessible through the Member Info section of the menu. Tracy also mentioned a retrospective link for the 2026 TAC to review past efforts and provide recommendations for the upcoming year. She encouraged new TAC members to review past meeting minutes and consider the pre-work done by the 2025 TAC members before setting goals for 2026.

### Standing Committees and Working Groups

The meeting focused on discussing the structure and effectiveness of standing committees and working groups within the LFDT. Arun raised a question about permanent standing committees, which led to a discussion about the benefits of long-term working groups versus temporary task forces. Diane suggested creating a permanent working group focused on contributor health, similar to successful models in other organizations. Tracy shared historical context about previous working groups in Hyperledger, noting that they often evolved into special interest groups focused more on conversation than specific outputs. The conversation ended with Yoav confirming that he had been briefed by Marcus on his role and expressing readiness to ask further questions as needed.

### Maintainer Days and Member Summit

David presented the draft agenda for Maintainer Days (January 26-27) and Member Summit, which will be held in Brooklyn and Jersey City respectively. The agenda aims to share information and facilitate collaboration among project maintainers, featuring presentations, lightning talks, and breakouts on various topics. While the event is primarily in-person, David suggested organizing a virtual event after this one to better accommodate remote participants. The group discussed potential improvements, such as incorporating project elevator pitches and visualizing project interconnections. Tracy announced her retirement and expressed gratitude for the community's support over the years.
