## Overview

The Technical Advisory Council meeting focused on reviewing quarterly reports and upcoming nominations, with discussions about improving the reporting process and addressing late submissions. The team explored changes to their reporting format and evaluation criteria, including modifications to annual and quarterly review processes while considering feedback from project maintainers. They also discussed removing LFX Insights Best Practices Score from project lifecycle evaluation criteria due to data reliability concerns and agreed to review other governance documents for potential updates.

## Details

### TAC Meeting: Reports and Nominations

The Technical Advisory Council meeting discussed quarterly reports and upcoming nominations. Tracy reminded attendees to review the Caliper report and mentioned that nominations for the TAC would begin on October 13th. Diane requested an additional week to complete the Trust Over IP review, and Ramakrishna indicated he would be ready with his assessment two weeks from now. Arun was tasked with reaching out to team members about overdue reports for Bevel and Baesu, while Tracy reminded those working on Trust Over IP, Cacti, and Fabric Q4 reports that they were due soon.

### Report Review Process Improvements

Tracy initiated a discussion on improving the process for annual and quarterly reports, focusing on closing them more efficiently and addressing late submissions. Enrique suggested examining the voting process for approvals, particularly distinguishing between annual and quarterly reviews. Tracy explained the current differences between the two types of reviews, including the use of primary and secondary reviewers for annual reviews and the expectation of individual reviews for quarterly reviews. The team considered whether these processes should be modified, with no clear decisions made.

### Annual Report Review Process Changes

The TAC discussed changes to their reporting process, deciding to bring back short presentations for annual reports while maintaining the two-reviewer approach, though keeping quarterly reports as self-reviewed documents. Marcus suggested limiting annual report presentations to 10 minutes with 5 slides, and expressed concern about the repetitive nature of quarterly report comments focusing on minor issues like typos. Tracy acknowledged the need to improve the process for new TAC members reviewing annual reports, noting some success in pairing them with experienced members but recognizing room for improvement in the matching process.

### Quarterly Reporting Process Enhancement

The team discussed improving the quarterly reporting process, with Enrique proposing the need for a checklist for reviewers to ensure comprehensive evaluation beyond just technical aspects. Tracy and Diane agreed, suggesting a SWOT analysis approach to focus on project progress, strengths, and areas to watch, rather than just typo checks. Arun highlighted the gap between annual and quarterly reports and emphasized the importance of feedback and continuous improvement in project health assessment.

### Enhancing TAC Project Review Process

The TAC team discussed improving their project review process, with Arun suggesting the need to track actions and critical areas identified during project reviews for better progressive tracking. Tracy agreed and proposed having primary and secondary reviewers follow projects throughout the year to ensure progress on annual review recommendations. Enrique raised the question of seeking feedback from project maintainers on the review process, suggesting that their input could help improve the effectiveness of TAC's reviews.

### Project Report Feedback Process Improvements

The group discussed challenges with project report completion and TAC meetings. Tracy and Enrique noted that certain experienced project maintainers might find value in consolidating feedback in one meeting, while Wenjing raised concerns about why busy maintainers might not prioritize these reports. They explored ideas to make the process more beneficial, including turning annual reviews into focused goal-setting sessions and having projects present during Q3, though Tracy expressed concern about this potentially consuming all TAC meeting time.

### Quarterly Reports Format Discussion

The TAC discussed the timing and format of quarterly reports, with Enrique suggesting a potential shift to semi-annual reports to allow more time for project progress. Tracy and Diane expressed concerns about the impact of delayed reports and agreed to gather feedback from maintainers on the current quarterly report system. Marcus suggested asking maintainers for their opinions on the report format and timing, and Tracy agreed to create a thread in the maintainers' Discord channel to collect feedback. The group also mentioned ongoing work on creating templates for standards and initiatives, with Diane and Rama set to review the Trust Over IP report as a starting point.

### LFX Insights Data Reliability Concerns

The team discussed removing LFX Insights Best Practices Score from the project lifecycle evaluation criteria due to unreliable data. Jessica explained that she had submitted multiple issues regarding data inconsistencies and inaccuracies in LFX Insights, and suggested focusing on more reliable tools like OpenSSF Scorecard and GitHub Insights. The group agreed to revisit the idea of creating custom GitHub Actions for eligibility projects, as previously considered during the Lifecycle Task Force. Jessica was tasked with reviewing other instances where LFX Insights is mentioned in governance documents and updating the relevant PR.
