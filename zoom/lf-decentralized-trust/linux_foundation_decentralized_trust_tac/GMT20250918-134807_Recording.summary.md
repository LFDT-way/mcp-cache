### Project Health Reporting Template Discussion

The group discussed creating a template for reporting project health, particularly for standards projects. Hart and Wenjing agreed to use Trust Over IP's current reporting format as a starting point rather than creating a prescriptive template. Tracy emphasized the need for consistency in project health tracking to enable the TAC to make decisions about graduation, archiving, or continuation of projects, noting that while initial decisions may be incorrect, they can be updated over time. The group acknowledged the need to make progress on this within the next 3 months to avoid similar discussions next year.

Wenjing expressed challenges with the current template for project reviews, suggesting it may be too rigid and not fully applicable to all projects. Tracy provided context on the purpose of project reports, explaining they are used for governing board updates, project health assessment, and helping new participants evaluate involvement opportunities. Ry proposed creating separate templates specifically for standards projects, including quarterly and annual versions, to better accommodate different project types and allow for iterative improvements based on feedback from projects like Trust Over IP.

The team decided to postpone discussion of other quarterly reports to the following week, and Diane reminded everyone about upcoming meetings for the Contribution Ladder Task Force and Project Consistency Task Force.
