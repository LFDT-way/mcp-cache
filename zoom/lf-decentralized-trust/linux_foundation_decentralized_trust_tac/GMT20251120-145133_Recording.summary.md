### PR Guidelines Update Discussion

The team discussed updates to a PR focused on consistency guidelines, incorporating feedback from Arun and others. They agreed to address Arun's suggestion about mandating public decision-making processes for features and bugs, with Matthew planning to review existing guidelines to avoid duplication. The group also considered adding content about the pathway for new contributors to become maintainers, though they noted this might overlap with existing project charter documentation. Tracy and Matthew emphasized the importance of not duplicating content across different documents to avoid confusion for project maintainers.

### Encouraging Contributor-to-Maintainer Pathways

Arun suggested adding a section to the consistency document that would encourage projects to clearly outline pathways for contributors to become maintainers, potentially serving as a landing page for such information. Matthew agreed that this encouragement aligns with the softer aims of the Task Force PR and suggested including it in the document, while Tracy highlighted the existence of a maintainer's guide that already specifies such paths. The group discussed whether to recommend a standard template for new projects, which might extend beyond the Task Force's scope but could be beneficial. Matthew proposed re-reading the document to find a natural place for this encouragement, balancing the need to complement existing guidelines without overburdening projects with too many requirements.

### Project Documentation Guidelines PR Review

The team discussed a PR related to project documentation guidelines, focusing particularly on SLAs and roadmaps. Tracy and others clarified that the task force aims for consistency across projects rather than within individual projects, leading to a decision to encourage projects to document their response handling practices without prescribing specific timeframes. The group agreed that Matthew would review the PR offline and incorporate feedback before the next meeting, where the team aims to vote on whether to merge the PR. Additionally, Tracy introduced a tool for collecting feedback on 2025 goals and encouraged all members to contribute their thoughts, as this will inform discussions in upcoming December meetings with the new TAC members.
