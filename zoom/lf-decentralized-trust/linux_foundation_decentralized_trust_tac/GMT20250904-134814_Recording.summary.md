The Technical Advisory Council (TAC) meeting discussed quarterly reports, with Tracy emphasizing the importance of timely reviews and noting that only the Cacti report was close to merging. ### Maintainer Summit Planning Discussion

The team discussed plans for the upcoming Maintainer Summit scheduled for January 26-27 in New York City, which will precede the Member Summit. David confirmed that maintainers attending the Maintainer Summit will also be invited to the Member Summit, as was done last year. Diane suggested workshops focused on connecting maintainers with end users, while Matthew proposed hands-on workshops to encourage cross-project collaboration and better understanding of available tools and projects. The exact locations for both events are still to be determined, but details will be shared soon.

### Hiro Maintainer Workshop Planning Discussion

The team discussed plans for an upcoming Maintainer workshop, which will be a 2-day event focused on Hiro project development. David explained that the agenda would be flexible and maintained-driven, with previous events having included various activities like general maintainer work and project-specific tasks. Hart and Tracy shared their experiences attending past events, noting the value of in-person collaboration and the diverse range of activities covered. The team agreed to reach out to maintainers from other projects for additional input on the agenda.

### Linux Foundation Event Format Evolution

Tracy and David discussed the format and purpose of Linux Foundation events, particularly focusing on the shift from hackfest to more structured formats like maintainer days. They noted that while the events maintain an open schedule, recent changes have included a more defined agenda to help attendees secure internal approval for travel. Tracy highlighted a successful past collaboration between Sawtooth and Burrow projects, emphasizing the value of in-person interactions for future project integrations like Firefly and Hiro. David added that the structured agenda helps attendees justify travel by providing a clear speaker slot, addressing feedback from previous unconference formats.

### Maintainers' Event Planning Discussion

The team discussed plans for an upcoming maintainers' event, agreeing to include a mix of planned talks and workshops, along with unstructured collaboration time. They decided to gather participants' priorities and objectives through a pre-event registration form and to share the event details widely through Discord channels, project lists, and the Dev Weekly Newsletter. The group also touched on upcoming TAC election planning, with Tracy suggesting they start the process in October and review last year's guidelines, which David shared in the chat.

### TAC Election Planning Discussion

The meeting focused on planning the upcoming election for the Technical Advisory Council (TAC). David announced that nominations would open soon, and Ry explained the election process, which involves both maintainer and board votes. Matthew suggested making the election process more transparent, but Ry clarified the current approach to maintain fairness. Tracy mentioned that one seat would be open next year, and the group discussed the possibility of changing the TAC's composition or election process. The conversation ended with a reminder that the nomination period typically starts in mid-October.

### 2026 Page and Project Guidelines

The meeting focused on two main topics: the creation of a 2026 page and guidelines for maintaining project status. Tracy suggested that now is a good time to reach out with ideas for the 2026 page, as it will likely be posted in the next week. The group also discussed the need for clearer criteria and guidelines for project lifecycle stages, including the development of a checklist for TAC members and potential updates to the OpenSSF scorecards. Diane mentioned ongoing challenges with quarterly reports for specifications like Trust over IP. The conversation ended with a decision to further develop these ideas and bring them back to the TAC for discussion.
