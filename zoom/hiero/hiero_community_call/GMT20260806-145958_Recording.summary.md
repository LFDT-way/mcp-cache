## Agenda

Hiero's Community Call welcomes new or existing contributors, collaborators and maintainers to join an open floor discussion regarding any issues, questions, how to's, lessons learnt and anything Hiero related. 
Current and future agendas can be found here: https://github.com/hiero-ledger/governance/wiki.

## Overview

The meeting focused on boosting community engagement and promoting tools like CodeRabbit and Claude to support maintainers and attract new contributors. Jessica and Angelina discussed plans for a CodeRabbit workshop to reach a broader audience and shared updates on sponsorship efforts. Sophie presented a strategy for automating the creation of "good first issues" using Claude to address the slow pace of new contributor onboarding. The group discussed challenges with maintainer engagement, the need for more accurate HIP tracking, and the potential for Angelina and Sophie to be nominated for TSE roles. They also addressed technical issues with GitHub Actions and the need for better communication and automation within the community. The role of Hiero Hackers in expediting work and the criteria for projects to graduate to Hiero Ledger were reviewed. Concerns about inactive maintainers in some repositories and the impact on progress were raised for future TSE discussion.

## Details

### CodeRabbit Presentation and Workshop Planning

Jessica and Angelina discussed promoting a CodeRabbit presentation and planning a workshop to increase engagement outside their community. ### CodeRabbit Demo Planning Meeting

Angelina discussed plans to present a full demo combining CodeRabbit and potentially Claude, depending on funding availability. She mentioned ongoing efforts to secure sponsorships and gather feedback through a survey about useful automations. Jessica suggested holding the workshop on September 22nd and offered to help promote it through Dev Weekly and Discord channels to reach a broader audience outside the Hiero community.

### Contributor Attraction Strategy Discussion

Jessica and Sophie discussed strategies for attracting new contributors, with Sophie reporting that while good first issue creation has slowed down, she developed an automated approach using templates in markdown format that Claude can generate. Sophie noted that manual good first issues creation takes significant time and patience, and she observed a general decline in such issues across repositories. Jessica mentioned that Diane and she plan to include issue progression metrics in the next TAC report, and Jessica expressed interest in having more maintainers learn from their approach to attracting contributors.

### TSC Elections and HIP Tracking

The team discussed several key topics including TSC elections, HIP tracking improvements, and community engagement challenges. Sophie and Angelina expressed interest in running for TSC positions, with Jessica offering to help promote their candidacies. The group reviewed a new HIP tracking dashboard initiative that Angie and Sophie developed, which aims to provide more accurate tracking of HIP implementation status across repositories. They also addressed concerns about missing actions tabs on the website, which were later restored. The team agreed to discuss several items in the next TSC meeting including SDK repositories with inactive maintainers and the Hiero Hackers project guidelines. Jessica noted that TSC elections will begin in mid-August with nominations starting August 25th.
