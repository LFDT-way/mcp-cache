The team discussed how to handle website development discussions within their current meeting structure. ### Modular Website Design Approach

Hendrik and Sophie discussed the approach to website design, agreeing that creating a comprehensive master design all at once would be too slow and impractical. They aligned on developing a more modular approach, starting with core branding elements and templates for key pages (landing page, product listing page, blog, and generic page) that would serve as a foundation for future content. Hendrik emphasized the need for a consistent base template in Figma to ensure all new pages would fit visually with the established design system, rather than having inconsistent elements across the site.
