## Overview

The meeting focused on discussing Hiero's project goals and challenges with community engagement. Hendrik, Jessica, Sophie, and others discussed the need for better TSC involvement in non-technical areas and the importance of having TSC members specialize in specific topics. They agreed to schedule a meeting with the Linux Foundation to explore possibilities for additional resources and support. The group also touched on the need for more diverse representation on the TSC and the potential for creating subcommittees to handle different aspects of the project.

## Details

### Project Goals and TSC Concerns

Jessica reported on a meeting with 60 maintainers where 9-10 people attended, including herself and Diane who helped gather missing information. The group discussed project goals, with Sophie raising concerns about the TSC's limited involvement in project steering beyond technical reviews. The attendees included Benil, Michael, Niharika, Oleg, Pernali, Rob, and Sophie, though some participants' names were unclear in the transcript.

### Enhancing Team Communication and Goals

Jessica and Hendrik discussed the need for better communication and support regarding team goals. Jessica mentioned receiving feedback from Rob that the goals were not surprising, as they align with team expectations. They plan to refine the goals and open them for discussion until the next TSC meeting, where Jessica will present them briefly. Hendrik expressed concern about information gaps among team members who were not present at the goal-setting meetings. He plans to conduct internal training to help teams transition to working more openly.

### Project Goals and Community Roadmap

Hendrik explained his plan to create guidelines and overviews about the project's internal history to help move it forward and increase meeting attendance. Sophie raised concerns about the lack of clarity regarding goal ownership and the overwhelming nature of the proposed plan, which Hendrik acknowledged and clarified that the goals were intended as a community roadmap rather than a binding plan with specific action items and deadlines.

### Hiero Project Goals Discussion

The team discussed goals for the Hiero project, with Sophie expressing concerns about making contributions sustainable and welcoming. Jessica clarified that the goals are meant to establish a baseline and are not strict requirements. Hendrik and Jessica agreed that TSC members should be more active in community meetings, and Hendrik suggested that TSC members could raise concerns about attendance at future TSC meetings.

### TSC Structure and Leadership Enhancement

Sophie and Hendrik discussed the challenges and future needs of the Technical Steering Committee (TSC) in Hiero, a growing open-source project. Sophie highlighted the TSC's strong technical capabilities but noted the need for broader leadership to address diverse project needs, such as marketing and community support. Hendrik suggested having dedicated individuals within the TSC or the company to handle specific areas and proposed regular project updates to the TSC. Both agreed that the TSC should become more structured and set clear guidelines or constraints to improve efficiency and address issues like community management.

### Enhancing Mentorship and TSC Engagement

The team discussed the goals meeting and Robert's perspective on community engagement and mentorship. Robert expressed interest in learning more about mentorship and integrating other LFTT projects. He also noted the need for more TSC involvement and accessibility. Jessica raised concerns about the TSC's familiarity with the goals and their focus on both technical and project-related aspects.

### Technical Steering Committee Diversity Concerns

Hendrik and Sophie discussed the diversity of the Technical Steering Committee (TSC). Hendrik explained that while the charter aims for a diverse TSC, including representation from different organizations, all current members are male. He suggested that during the next voting in September, someone should propose more diverse candidates. Sophie raised the possibility of suggesting changes to the charter to address this issue, but Hendrik noted that such changes would require approval from the entire TSC and involve legal considerations.

### Community Engagement and Support Strategies

Sophie and Hendrik discussed the need for the TST to engage more effectively with the community, particularly in diverse areas. They explored the possibility of assigning TST members to specific areas of interest and specializing in certain technical aspects. Sophie expressed concerns about the current 5-minute show-and-tell format and emphasized the importance of a strong support network. Hendrik suggested that the TSC could define specific topics for which members would be responsible, similar to other projects, and proposed a list of topics where the TSC's help is needed. They agreed that better decision-making processes are necessary for community management and outreach.

### Resource Scaling and Community Management

The group discussed scaling challenges with over 100-200 contributors and the need for better resource allocation. Hendrik suggested organizing a meeting with the Linux Foundation to explore possibilities for budgeting resources, including hiring additional community management staff. Jessica agreed this was a good idea and proposed inviting David to the upcoming community management meeting, with Daniela as an optional attendee. The conversation ended with a brief discussion about Hendrik's recent birthday celebration.
