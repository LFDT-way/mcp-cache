## Details

### AI Agentic Identity Discussion

Keith and Ty discussed the topic of AI and agentic identity, with Ty mentioning an interesting development about GoDaddy AMS and Cloudflare's partnership. They agreed to start by defining what agentic identity means, particularly focusing on identity for agents versus humans.

### Trust in Identity Systems Foundations

Ty and Keith discussed the foundations of trust in identity systems, particularly focusing on how trust is established through control of domains like PBS.org or hedera.com. Ty explained that in the ANS world, trust is based on controlling a DNS record that verifies agent identity and signatures, while Keith noted that this approach works well for B2B contexts but has limitations for human identities. The conversation touched on how identity differs from trust or reputation, with Ty emphasizing that identity is simply a unique identifier, and they briefly mentioned HCS14 and various registry systems like ERC 8004.

### Digital Identity and Verification Concepts

Keith and Ty discussed the concept of identity in digital systems, focusing on the distinction between identity and verification/certification. Ty argued that identity should be a simple unique string, separate from trust and reputation, which can be verified through registries and certifications. Keith countered by referencing real-world examples like bank accounts, where identity documents are directly used without separation from verification processes. The discussion highlighted the complexity of implementing universal identifiers across different systems and the importance of separating identity from certification and verification processes.

### AI Identity Definition Discussion

Ty and Keith discussed the concept of human identity in AI use cases, using online commerce as an example. They debated whether email and credit card details constitute identity, with Ty arguing that they serve as unique identifiers while Keith preferred a stricter definition. Baris contributed by sharing a relevant example about online retailers potentially colluding to identify users through anonymized activities. The discussion highlighted the complexity of defining identity in digital contexts and the potential for data correlation to reveal personal information.

### AI Identity and Trust Systems

Ty and Keith discussed challenges around AI and identity, focusing on privacy concerns and potential security risks. Ty explained the concept of an identity system that would allow for trust assessment based on reputation and historical actions, using unique identifiers and cryptographic verification. They acknowledged the need for a standardized approach to evaluating trust for anonymous identities in the AI ecosystem.

### AI Trust Scores Implementation Challenges

The group discussed the challenges of implementing trust scores in AI and identity systems. Ty emphasized that trust is inherently personal and subjective, suggesting instead to focus on algorithmic variables that can help users assess trust based on their own priorities. Keith noted that trust scores have been frequently proposed but rarely implemented successfully due to disagreements on scoring mechanisms. Baris proposed a different approach, focusing on trust thresholds based on established benchmarks, such as the IATA Operational Safety Audit for airlines, rather than scores.

### AI Identity Verification Challenges

The group discussed challenges around verifying human identity for AI agents, particularly in open source projects like GitHub. Keith and Ty explored various approaches, including KYC processes and zero-knowledge proofs, while acknowledging that any solution could potentially be gamed by malicious actors. The conversation highlighted that while complete verification might be difficult, requiring some form of human identity validation could help prevent AI-based fraud in specific contexts like open source contributions. The discussion concluded with consideration of how these concepts might apply to Hedera's AI agent studio and identity requirements for AI on the platform.
