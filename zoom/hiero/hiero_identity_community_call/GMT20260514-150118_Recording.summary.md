## Details

### GoDaddy's HCS14 Identity Standard Adoption

Ty shared significant news about Hyro Identity, including GoDaddy's adoption of HCS14 as ANS's identity standard and its inclusion in an IETF specification under the IRO project. GoDaddy submitted their own standards, HCS27 for attestation of state on Hedera and HCS28 for identity updates, both using Hedera Mainnet. Ty noted that HCS14's inclusion in multiple open-source projects represents a significant development for the technology.

### Press Release and Partner Updates

Keith and Ty discussed the status of a press release, which Ty confirmed was live on GoDaddy and had received positive feedback from Scott Courtney. Keith noted a decrease in activity from a previous partner who provided HECA and DSR services due to contract issues, though Ty mentioned the partner had joined the HOL's partner program. The conversation concluded with Keith raising the topic of identity rails, suggesting they may be required for stablecoin and real-world tokenization use cases on the Hedera platform.

### Blockchain Identity Verification Requirements

The discussion focused on identity verification and compliance requirements for blockchain assets and platforms. Keith shared insights from a newsletter about common gaps in blockchain implementations, particularly around trust, compliance, authorization, and reconciliation. The conversation explored whether Aceto's current KYC process effectively addresses these requirements, especially as they expand beyond Spanish banks to other jurisdictions. Keith also mentioned ongoing work in AI identity, noting that while there are multiple groups working on this topic, including Fido's AI identity work group, much of the current activity seems focused on process and structure rather than substantive progress.

### HOL Partner Program Launch Discussion

Ty discussed HOL's launch of a partner program with 30 partners, including DSR, and their creation of four subcommittees focusing on agent registries, payments, privacy and security, and interagency communication. The group explored the role of HCS 14 in identity standards, with Ty noting its unique approach to generalizing connection formats across ecosystems. Keith mentioned a new FIDO working group addressing identity verification challenges and expressed interest in attending their meetings to explore potential standards development.

### Hedera Identity Service Development Updates

Ty and Keith discussed recent developments in the identity and trust service space, particularly highlighting a co-press release between GoDaddy and HOL regarding the HIRO consensus specification standard. Ty explained that this validates Hedera as a trust service for external systems, as it's being used to prove Merkle hashes on the DLT. Keith agreed to look into the story further and potentially reach out to Devin about promoting it more broadly.
