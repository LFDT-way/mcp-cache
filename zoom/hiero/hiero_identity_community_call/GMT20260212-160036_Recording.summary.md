### Hedera Identity Platform Overview

The meeting introduced the new Hedera Identity Platform, previously known as Hekka, which is an open-source contribution based on proprietary products developed in 2022-2023. The platform supports various decentralized identity standards and protocols, including UDI RF Architecture, SD JOT verifiable credentials, and OpenID for verifiable credentials. It includes two main applications: mobile wallets built on React Native and an Identity Service consisting of a backend service and web UI. The platform is integrated with Hedera's HCS and Hiero Slashkit, offering competitive advantages in terms of low costs and high performance for storing Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs) and associated data documents.

### Verifiable Credentials Protocol Overview

Alexander presented on two protocol stacks for verifiable credentials: OpenID for referral credentials and DIDCOM/Aries. He explained that OpenID consists of two protocols for issuance and presentation, while DIDCOM is a transport-agnostic protocol with its own security features. Alexander highlighted the modular design of their identity platform, built on Creator and Nest.js, which allows easy addition of new ledger/credentials type support. He also mentioned backup and recovery approaches, support for credential revocation, and white label app capabilities.

### Decentralized Trust Registry Implementation Discussion

The team discussed the implementation of trust registries, with Alexander explaining there are two types: one stored on HCS/Hedera using DID and grades methods, and another used in trust frameworks to verify credential presenters. Keith shared that Europe was planning a centralized API-based trust registry to support identity initiatives, while Alexander mentioned their team proposed an HCS2 specification extension for decentralized identity trust registries, which is currently under review. The discussion concluded with Keith emphasizing that HECA provides a polished open source version of Credo and Bifold frameworks for the Hedera ecosystem, making it easier for companies to implement decentralized identity solutions.
