### Hedera Identity Studio Proposal

The group discussed creating an Identity Studio on Hedera, which would be a collection of open-source identity offerings packaged together for easier navigation and access. Keith proposed submitting a proposal to the TSC for the creation of the Identity Studio, with Pablo and Mahir's issuer and verifier libraries being included as sub-repos. The team agreed this would be a beneficial next step, as it would provide a unified approach to identity solutions and better facilitate community building around the framework. Keith plans to circulate a proposal to the community for feedback before presenting it to the TSC.

### Software Component Donation Proposal

Pablo explained that their proposal includes donating three software components: a credential SDK with issuer and verifier submodules, a CryptoSuite SDK, and a Universal Resolver driver. The team plans to upgrade the resolver to support version 2 of the derivative method once fully implemented. These components are designed to be modular and can be used individually or together as part of a larger product. Pablo emphasized the importance of the resolver for supporting the new specification, which aligns with W3C standards and incorporates the concept of a controller.

### DID Standards Integration Discussion

The team discussed the integration of different DID (Decentralized Identifier) standards and implementations, with Keith explaining that while there are multiple methods (1.0 and 2.0) that are not backwards compatible, they are working towards aligning with established standards like W3C. Pablo emphasized the importance of aligning to common standards to avoid technical messiness, citing a successful example of collaboration between different teams using the same SDK. The team also discussed the status of Identity Studio, which will initially resemble the Opal Wallet Foundation by bringing together various libraries, and confirmed that the HIP proposal for DID Method 2.0 has been moved to active status.

### Universal Resolver Driver Development Update

Keith and Pablo discussed the status and future plans for the Universal Resolver driver. Pablo explained that while the driver is Dockerized, work on it has been paused for about a year and a half. They plan to finalize the driver to support V1 of the data method in the short term, with the goal of incorporating V2 support once the DIF SDK V2 is implemented. Pablo mentioned that they are at a proof of concept stage for the SDK V2 and are preparing for its first release, which will include the DERA method V2 implementation.

### Universal Resolver Integration Strategy

The team discussed the performance and implementation of a universal resolver, with Pablo explaining that while the current solution is a proof of concept, enterprises would likely need their own caching mechanisms and infrastructure setup for optimal performance. They agreed that the Identity Studio would be the appropriate place to document these enterprise integration considerations, as it would provide flexibility for different deployment scenarios while maintaining standards compliance. Keith suggested updating the proposal to reflect that the universal resolver would be part of the Identity Studio, which both Pablo and Giuseppe supported as a natural evolution of the project.

### DSR Integration and Community Planning

The team discussed the integration work with DSR and Credo, which is nearing completion and will include video demos and public downloads once bifold support is ready. They agreed to create a Discord channel for the Hiero Identity Community and discussed the need for better infrastructure around identity calls, including a place to store meeting minutes and agendas. Keith and Diane planned to discuss offline about creating a dedicated repository for community call documentation, while Jessica offered to help set up the Discord channel and manage meeting invitations.
