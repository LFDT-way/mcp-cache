The team discussed the structure of a mentorship program where one mentor can be assigned to multiple issues, but each issue requires one mentee who must have a mentor. Hendrik explained that if multiple mentees work on the same topic, the funding would be split between them, with each receiving half of the original amount. Hendrik mentioned he had a concrete project idea and had reached out to the tech community to gather 3-4 project proposals, while also exploring the possibility of HashCraft contributing ideas to the program.

The discussion clarified that while the agenda for the upcoming TSC meeting was already set, there was flexibility to discuss mentorship topics under other business items or in next week's meeting.
