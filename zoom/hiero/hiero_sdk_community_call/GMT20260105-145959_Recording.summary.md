## Overview

The team discussed the development and testing status of the Python SDK, including the need for better communication channels and support for new contributors. They explored various solutions to improve communication between maintainers, contributors, and the community, particularly regarding issues affecting Solo and Mirror nodes. The discussion concluded with plans to address resource constraints and enhance community engagement through better support systems and leadership involvement.

## Details

### Python SDK Development Status Review

The team discussed the Python SDK development and testing status. Robert agreed to look into the state of TCK testing for the Python SDK and mentioned that Richard's son had previously worked on it. Keith suggested assigning a line chain person to support Python SDK development part-time, pending approval from Simi. Sophie inquired about community calls with Simi and suggested creating an SDK chat group for better coordination among team members.

### Community Communication Channel Improvements

Sophie and Robert discussed the need for better communication channels within the community, particularly for addressing Hiero and Solo issues. Robert suggested moving certain chats to the High Road Discord to increase public awareness, while Sophie emphasized the importance of private channels for discussions that are not yet finalized. Keith proposed creating a dedicated "Ask an Expert" channel to facilitate communication between Sophie and relevant experts, addressing specific issues like KIP1195 or Hiero network problems.

### SDK Communication Challenges Discussion

The team discussed communication challenges regarding SDK issues, particularly with the Solo network. Sophie provided an example of a stalled good first issue in the C++ SDK due to Solo network failures during the holidays, highlighting the need for better communication about such issues. Robert suggested that maintainers should monitor Discord channels and provide updates when issues arise, while Keith emphasized the importance of clear announcements about SDK-related problems. The discussion aimed to improve communication between the Solo team and third-party contributors working on SDKs.

### SDK Communication Challenges Addressed

The team discussed communication challenges within the SDKs, particularly regarding issues affecting both Mirror and Solo nodes. Sophie and Angelina highlighted the need for better communication channels to keep contributors informed about ongoing issues and their resolution timelines, especially for newcomers who may be hesitant to continue contributing without clear information. Keith emphasized the importance of having a dedicated channel for contributors to ask about test failures, without expecting them to understand complex underlying issues like Solo. The team agreed that better communication channels were a minimum need, and Sophie mentioned they would discuss further plans to address these challenges in the Python SDK.

### Supporting New Contributors with Discord

The team discussed communication channels and support for new contributors. Robert suggested using Discord channels for updates and announcements, while Sophie highlighted the existing "Good First Issue" support team's role in mentoring new contributors. Keith emphasized that first-time contributors should not be expected to understand complex underlying systems like Solo, and proposed a simple notification about SDK test failures. The team agreed that a dedicated Discord channel could help provide support and information for new contributors interested in learning more about Solo.

### Python SDK Growth and Challenges

Sophie discussed the success and challenges of the Python SDK, highlighting its growth to 100 contributors and the need for a structured onboarding process. She explained that the SDK has attracted developers from other SDKs, potentially at the expense of other projects. Sophie emphasized the importance of balancing growth with the ability to support and mentor new contributors effectively. She also mentioned the need for new triage members, committers, and maintainers to help manage the increasing workload.

### Enhancing Contributor Support System

Sophie and Angelina discussed the need to improve support for new contributors by leveling up the rest of Hiero and creating a more accessible issue labeling system. They emphasized the importance of a larger "good first issue" support team and applying the new labeling criteria across all SDKs. Sophie shared a pull request that outlines the criteria for different issue levels, revealing that many previously designated "good first issues" are now considered intermediate. Angelina proposed implementing this labeling system in the C++ SDK to guide new contributors and ease their entry into the project.

### Enhancing Developer Challenges in Hiero

The team discussed the need for more beginner and intermediate issues across Hiero repositories, particularly for SDKs, to retain and attract top developers who are currently leaving for external projects due to lack of challenging work. Sophie highlighted that advanced developers are leaving because they've solved all available issues, while Angelina noted that while the initial focus is on SDKs, the long-term goal is to implement this flow across all repositories. Keith suggested presenting this situation to Richard and Atul, emphasizing the need for additional resources to support Hiero infrastructure and developer engagement.

### Community Resource Planning Discussion

Sophie and Angelina discussed the need for additional resources to support their community and development efforts, highlighting the manual processes and high resource demands in the initial stages. They emphasized the importance of converting developers into committed maintainers and the opportunity to leverage established systems and workflows. Keith suggested involving senior leaders like Richard and Atul in a dedicated meeting to discuss the resource requirements and present the opportunity to secure more Hashgraph support.

### Hiero Repository Support Challenges

The team discussed challenges with community engagement and support for Hiero repositories. Sophie and Angelina highlighted the need for urgent help in managing developer contributions and maintaining the Hiero website, while Keith emphasized the importance of being specific about the type of support needed. They agreed to connect Sophie and Angelina to Slack channels for direct communication and to initiate conversations with Richard and Atul about providing additional support. Robert mentioned that some maintainers were still hesitant about the good first issue support group concept, which Keith and Robert agreed to discuss further offline.
