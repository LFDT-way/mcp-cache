### SDK Version Bump Decision

Simi discussed a major version bump from 2.0 to 3.0 for SDKs under the Hiero namespace, presenting two options. Option 1 involves only changing the namespace without removing deprecated APIs or introducing breaking changes, while Option 2 includes these additional changes, requiring maintenance of two separate code bases. Simi recommended Option 1 as the more manageable solution given the timeline, and Ivan agreed. Robert inquired about how this would work, but the transcript ends before the answer is provided.

### Namespace Migration Versioning Decision

The team discussed whether moving to the Hiero namespace should trigger a major version upgrade, with general agreement that it should. The debate centered on whether the version bump should communicate just the namespace change or include other potential future changes, with Simi explaining that semantic versioning guidelines suggest treating package location changes as breaking. Keith suggested that moving to Hiero, while not strictly a breaking change, represents a high-impact change that warrants a major version upgrade to ensure the community is aware of the necessary code changes.

### Major Version Bump Discussion

The team discussed options for a major version bump, focusing on whether to proceed with a new namespace or include additional breaking changes. Simi highlighted the need to define breaking changes and deprecated APIs, emphasizing the importance of giving developers sufficient time to adapt. Keith confirmed that the DevOps team requires at least 3-6 months' notice for breaking changes. The team agreed to continue the discussion in the relevant issue and bring it up in the next community call, with participants encouraged to add comments on their preferred option.
