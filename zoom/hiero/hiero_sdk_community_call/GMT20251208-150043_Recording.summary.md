### Python SDK Contributor Process Review

Sophie presented on the Python SDK's good first issue process, highlighting its success in attracting new contributors and improving issue visibility. The team discussed concerns about automatically labeling issues as good first issues, with Hendrik and others suggesting a need for maintainer approval before such labeling. Robert proposed creating a separate "needs maintainer approval" tag, while the group agreed that maintainers should review issues promptly, ideally within 1-2 days. The discussion concluded with a consensus that while good first issue templates are valuable, they should not be automatically applied without maintainer review, especially for issues that could be misinterpreted or harmful.

### Good First Issue Ranking System

Sophie discussed the ranking system for good first issues, explaining that projects need frequent updates and active maintainers to maintain a high ranking. She highlighted the effectiveness of their good first issue template in the Python SDK, which has led to faster issue resolution and improved developer engagement. Robert suggested developing a bot to notify maintainers about new good first issues, aiming to balance community involvement with maintainers' ability to review these issues promptly.

### Good First Issue Label Management

Sophie and Hendrik discussed the process of creating and managing "good first issue" labels in their project. Sophie expressed concern about the visibility of old, unaddressed issues, while Hendrik suggested using the label to quickly identify and prioritize new candidates. They agreed that maintaining a steady flow of good first issues is crucial for attracting new developers, but acknowledged challenges such as project inactivity and limited resources. Hendrik offered to share specific examples of problematic issues via Discord, and both agreed to work on improving the process to ensure only high-quality issues are labeled as "good first."

### Good First Issue Template Integration

The team discussed the implementation of the "good first issue" template and label system. Hendrik proposed merging the Python SDK's good first issue template with Hiero's template, while Andrew suggested using a GitHub action for automatic assignment of issues with the good first issue label to maintainers. Sophie highlighted a difference in opinion between community involvement and maintainer control over the good first issue label, with Hendrik emphasizing the need for maintainer oversight to protect the project's reputation.

### Enhancing Good First Issues Quality

Hendrik and Sophie discussed the challenges and potential improvements around good first issues in Hiero's SDKs. Hendrik emphasized the importance of maintaining high-quality good first issues to prevent demotivation and negative feedback from the community. Sophie highlighted the issue of good issues not being converted to good first issues, which hinders community engagement. ### Open-Source Issue Triage Challenges

The team discussed the challenges with maintaining and triaging good first issues in open-source repositories, particularly for the Python SDK. Andrew emphasized the need to provide clear training and expectations for maintainers, as many may not be aware of their responsibilities in triaging issues. The group agreed that engaging all maintainers, including those not on Discord, is crucial for effectively managing the issue backlog. They also discussed the importance of clean, up-to-date good first issues for the open-source community and the possibility of removing the good first issue tag while issues are being refined.

### Python SDK Contribution Guidelines

The team discussed the implementation of a "good first issue" label for the Python SDK, with Sophie explaining that she created a detailed template to help contributors understand requirements and provide clear examples. Andrew and Hendrik debated the timing and accessibility of these issues, with Hendrik expressing concern about a one-week timeline and the potential for low-quality submissions, while Andrew advocated for making the issues accessible to drive community engagement. The team agreed to proceed with the thorough template approach for the Python SDK, acknowledging that it might filter out some low-effort submissions but would ultimately improve the quality of contributions.

### Maintainer Roles and Documentation Discussion

Sophie and Hendrik discussed the role of maintainers in addressing and documenting issues, emphasizing the importance of maintaining clear documentation and labeling issues appropriately. Andrew highlighted the need for maintainers to be more involved and to actively manage their backlogs, while also suggesting the creation of a clear document outlining maintainer responsibilities. The group agreed on the value of such a document but acknowledged the challenge of creating one that accommodates the diverse contributions of all maintainers. They decided to continue this discussion in a future maintainers call.
