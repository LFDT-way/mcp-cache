## Overview

The marketing team discussed challenges with community engagement and website promotion for the Hiero project. They explored creating a dedicated LinkedIn page for Hiero to better promote community meetings and blog posts, though concerns were raised about maintaining the page and coordinating with the LFDT marketing team. The group also discussed updating the Hiero website's content structure, with Keith proposing to create a PR with a redesigned homepage using AI tools rather than pursuing a complex CMS implementation. Additionally, they considered adding a sponsor button on the website to enable targeted marketing donations from LFDT member companies, though this would require further discussion with the Linux Foundation.

## Details

### Website Content Restructuring Discussion

The marketing team discussed website content restructuring, with Keith suggesting that the "What is HIRO" section should be moved to the top of the landing page, followed by first issues, community calls, and how to join, while the current roadmap section should either be updated or removed. Hendrik agreed that the website needs better content about participation, particularly in the blog and news sections, and suggested that Keith create a draft proposal for restructuring the landing page content. Jessica mentioned that maintainers have permission to edit agendas and can add new calendar links, and noted that attendance for community meetings has been lower after vacation.

### Website Updates and Content Strategy

The team discussed removing the roadmap section from the website since the transition from Hashgraph to Hiero is mostly complete, and agreed to replace it with a "mission" section. They also decided to create a monthly blog post called "Hiero Rings of the Month" to share community news and updates, with the content to be shared with the LFT newsletter. The blog post will highlight interesting developments from the TSC and other community areas, with the initial format consisting of short paragraphs and one or two rings per item.

### Rings of the Month Blog Feature

Hendrik and Jessica discussed the idea of creating a "Rings of the Month" feature similar to a previous developer-driven initiative. They agreed that this should be a blog post rather than a permanent page to avoid commitment issues. Hendrik suggested publishing these posts on the website and including them in the LFFDT newsletter. Jessica expressed concern about maintaining the initiative, to which Hendrik acknowledged but emphasized the importance of having a concrete news section.

### Community Meeting Promotion Strategies

The team discussed the lack of promotion for community meetings, with Keith expressing frustration about low attendance and suggesting potential solutions like consolidating or canceling certain meetings. Hendrik acknowledged the issue and mentioned that some promotional efforts have been made, such as inviting people to meetings, but agreed that more marketing is needed. They compared their approach to other open source communities, noting that some have dedicated marketing funds and subcommittees to plan their use effectively.

### Enhancing Hiero's Community Content

The group discussed community engagement and content creation for Hiero. Keith emphasized the lack of resources and budget, highlighting the need to maximize the impact of volunteer time. Renata suggested allowing partner companies to contribute blog posts and content, which Hendrik and Keith supported. They agreed to add a blog section to the Hiero website for companies to share articles and updates.

### Blog Content Strategy Discussion

The team discussed the status and future of their blog sections, with Hendrik explaining that they already have existing blog content that could be revitalized rather than creating new sections. Jessica clarified that they have two separate blog sites - one for technical content and maintainer news, and another for major project updates. The team agreed that while they had good momentum in September and October, they need to restart regular content creation and identified the need for a dedicated channel to promote blog posts and other announcements, which would likely need to be managed by marketing or member companies.

### LinkedIn Strategy for Hiero

The team discussed creating a LinkedIn page for Hiero, with Jessica explaining that the LFDT marketing team prefers hosting it on their existing page due to established audience reach. Keith and Hendrik argued for a separate Hiero LinkedIn page to reach their specific audience, and Jessica agreed to confirm if personal LinkedIn posts about Hiero are allowed, which she will discuss in her internal meeting. The team agreed that community members like Angelina should feel free to promote Hiero activities through their personal LinkedIn accounts without needing approval.

### LinkedIn Strategy for Community Updates

The team discussed creating a LinkedIn account for Hiero, focusing on maintaining a small but engaged audience for important community updates. Jessica agreed to consult with her team about potential concerns, particularly regarding maintenance and content guidelines. Keith clarified that the goal is not to build a large following but to effectively communicate news and events to the community. The team also touched on upcoming events and Jessica mentioned she would provide an update in the next marketing meeting.

### Website Sponsorship and Redesign Plans

The team discussed the need for a sponsor button on their website to facilitate targeted donations for the Hiero project, with Hendrik suggesting a structured approach within the LFDT community for such donations. Keith proposed redesigning the homepage using AI tools, eliminating the need for a complex content management system, and planned to share a PR for review. The group also touched on the potential of GitHub sponsorship and the need for further clarification from the LFDT on its stance and usage of such funds. Jessica agreed to follow up on the LinkedIn conversation with her team and encouraged maintainers to add topics to future agendas.
