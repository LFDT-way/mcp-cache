## Details

### HOL Registry Migration to LFX

Michael welcomed participants to the HOL Registry Subcommittee session and announced that the team has finally migrated to the Linux Foundation LFX system after planning to do so for some time. He thanked Jessica for helping with the migration setup, noting that they had previously discussed making this transition at Workivo. The meeting included participants from various time zones, with Alula joining from Houston, Texas, and others including Matt, Keith, and Tina.

### HLX AI Show Relaunch Meeting

Michael welcomed Jessica from Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust who introduced herself and explained her role in supporting community projects through events and workshops. Michael outlined the agenda for the meeting, including working group updates and the relaunch of their HLX AI show in a new video format. He announced that the show would now feature video content and invited participants to book slots as guests, while also reminding them about the HL blog available to members of the partner program for sharing updates.

### GenLayer Grant Program Presentation

Ana-Maria presented on behalf of Denari Labs and Red Beard Ventures, outlining their investment approach and current focus on the coordination economy and sovereign AI. She detailed a new 8-week grant program with GenLayer focused on adjudication for smart contracts, offering up to 2 million Gen tokens to 8-10 teams starting September 8th, with applications due August 31st. Michael then discussed forming working groups, specifically the Trust Scores Working Group based on the ACS 25 draft standard, and requested participants to vote on time slots using a Rally link to reach consensus for the group's first meeting.

### Adaptive Layer Working Group Formation

Matt discussed the formation of an adaptive layer working group focused on expanding HCS25 parameters and creating an adapter registry to facilitate implementation of different trust scores. The group aims to start meeting next week on an every-other-week cadence, with Matt coordinating with Michael to determine the meeting time and share details via email. Michael provided additional context about the working group's purpose, explaining that it would complement HCS25 by addressing how to find and evaluate adapters for trust scores.

### Agent Registry Working Group Update

Hassan explained the Agent Registry for Banks Working Group, which focuses on enabling bank-issued KYC/KYB certificates for payment-capable agents interacting with bank gateways. He requested participants with different technology stacks to join the working group to ensure technology agnosticism in the standard development. Michael confirmed the process for interested parties to sign up through the rally link and mentioned the upcoming move of the cryptographic agent execution standards to the LFX system. Chris provided an update on the cryptographic agent execution standards and collaboration with DIA on decentralized AI agents, inviting participants to join the related work groups.

### AI Retail Spaces Working Group

Tina provided an update on the AI-powered physical spaces working group, explaining their goal to make physical retail spaces discoverable through Gemini Chat GPT by assigning node addresses. She mentioned a pilot with a customer who has 300 stores and plans to present at a retail conference in September. Michael noted that two e-commerce applications were recently submitted for the partner program and offered to connect Tina with these applicants for potential collaboration. New members Andrew from Vera Anchor, Joel from Certison, and Raul from Thoughtproof introduced their companies and verification platforms during the meeting.
