Alula explained that Hydrocoin is an MRV platform for water infiltration that creates tokens representing one gallon of water, and they are setting up a working group to explore potential collaborations with HOL. Alula outlined several collaboration opportunities, including integrating Hydrocoin with Hedera, developing sustainability standards, and using the X402GAL system to allow agents to offset water consumption with Hydrocoin tokens.

### AHL Partner Program Working Groups

Michael discussed the AHL Partner Program and announced upcoming working groups, including the Agent Trust Scorers Working Group kickoff on Monday the 31st at 10 a.m. Eastern. He reminded participants to join working groups through the partner program link and mentioned a recent pull request from GoDaddy regarding DNS trust adapter integration. The session concluded with an introduction to Matt Kay who would present from Intuition, and mentioned his running HCS25 adapter layers working group meetings every Wednesday at 10 a.m. Eastern.

### Grant Cohort 2 Initiative Overview

Matt presented Grant Cohort 2, an initiative to allocate $100,000 in stablecoins for funding composable protocols under the theme of information finance. The program aims to partner with other groups to pool resources and improve grant capital allocation efficiency, moving away from ineffective siloed grant programs. Matt outlined the vision for a consortium of composable protocols working together through specific RFPs and categories, with a focus on providing structured support, best practices, and networking opportunities for funded teams over an 8-12 week program.

### Information Finance Program Launch Planning

Matt explained that while internal work has been done on the program, there is no public website yet, with plans to launch in mid-Q4. He clarified that they are seeking partners rather than grant applicants, and discussed the program's focus on funding necessary public goods and sustainable ideas within information finance, particularly those producing consumable outputs. Joel offered to provide verification services for the program's cohort, and Alula expressed interest in contributing to the sustainability aspect and offered support with UI and website development.

### HLGuard 3.x Release Progress

Michael discussed progress on HLGuard's 3.x release, which includes a contribution model for extensions and a public registry for open-source contributors. He announced upcoming working groups and mentioned a September request for comments on an audible ZKP sandbox standard. The team addressed questions about plugin review timelines and monetization possibilities for extensions, with Michael explaining that while no immediate monetization plans exist, there could be opportunities similar to WordPress's ecosystem in the future.
