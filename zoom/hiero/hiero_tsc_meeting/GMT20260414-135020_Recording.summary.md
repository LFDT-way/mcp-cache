### HIP 1427 Record to Block Transition

The team discussed and approved HIP 1427, which addresses the transition from record files to block streams. Mark explained that the HIP proposes wrapping historical record files into "wrapped record blocks" to create a unified blockchain format, allowing block nodes to service the full network history. The process has been running for six months with validation checks in place, though there are concerns about potential errors in the hashing process. The team also discussed that while mirror nodes will need to be upgraded to understand the new block format, existing nodes won't need to re-process the entire historical record set. The group agreed to postpone discussion of the second HIP to next week's meeting.
