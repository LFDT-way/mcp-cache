The team discussed updates to a two-stage cutover process, with Joseph explaining that the first stage would wrap existing record files in a block structure while continuing to write to legacy buckets, allowing for a gradual transition without changing security mechanisms. Mark announced upcoming HIPs including HIP 1448 for broadcast channels in the gossip protocol and HIP 1446 for multiple parent hash graphs to improve network scalability. The TSC discussed project status reporting requirements, with Alex raising questions about maintaining oversight of Hiro projects, leading to a consensus that a structured approach should be developed to balance visibility with avoiding excessive administrative burden. The conversation ended with a decision to remove Codacy from the organization's tools, with Jessica receiving approval from the TSC to remove associated webhooks due to lack of clear ownership and maintenance.
