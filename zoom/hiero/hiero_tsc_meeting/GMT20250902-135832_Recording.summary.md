### TSS Encryption Migration Proposal

Mark presented a proposal to migrate a new encryption standard called TSS to the Hiero repository. TSS will replace the current signature scheme for nodes attesting to record files, offering improved efficiency and cost-effectiveness by using aggregated signatures and reducing the need for multiple file verifications. The migration is intended to position this critical dependency within the same organization as the consensus node, with Mark hoping to present the proposal for approval and follow up with a vote the following week, represented by Jeff Morgan.

### TSS Placement in Hiro Ecosystem

The team discussed the placement of TSS (Trustable Signature Scheme) within the Hiro ecosystem, with Mark proposing to move it there. Alex raised questions about comparing TSS with PBJ, with Richard suggesting PBJ should go to CNCF alongside gRPC, while TSS makes more sense to stay within Hiro due to its specific utility for the project. Leemon clarified that TSS is a collection of cryptography components designed for Hiro's specific needs, including aggregate signatures and address book proving, and while it could potentially be useful to other projects, it's currently best suited to remain within Hiro rather than being developed as a standalone project.
