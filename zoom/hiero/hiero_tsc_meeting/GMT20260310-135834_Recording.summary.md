### X402 Payment Protocol Introduction

Lindsay introduced the X402 payment scheme and protocol, explaining how it functions as a paymaster to reduce friction in transactions by allowing facilitators to sponsor fees for others. She described how the protocol works through partially signed transactions and smart contracts, enabling payments in different cryptocurrencies without users needing to worry about gas fees. Lindsay mentioned that their scheme has been accepted into the Coinbase spec and they are working on a reference implementation to enable anyone to create a facilitator supporting Hedera payments.

### X402 Facilitator Scheme Overview

Lindsay explained the X402 facilitator scheme, which allows micropayments using crypto transactions by adding a trust-based facilitator to prevent double-spending attacks. She noted that while this introduces a trust point, it improves Web3 user experience and is being accepted by industry leaders like Coinbase and Shopify. The scheme is currently in testnet phase with Blocky Devs operating a facilitator, and Lindsay encouraged the Hedera ecosystem to consider running their own facilitators. The discussion addressed questions about payment arbitrage, account creation handling, and the open nature of the standard, with Lindsay clarifying that facilitators handle currency conversion and merchant payments rather than creating new user accounts.

### TSS Ceremony HIP Review

The meeting focused on reviewing a HIP (Hedera Improvement Proposal) for a TSS (Threshold Signature Scheme) ceremony. Diane raised concerns about governance rules, participant awareness, and public listing of participants, which Mark addressed by clarifying that while the technical process is understood, specific details like participant selection and public visibility are still being determined. Alex questioned the network-specific language in the HIP, suggesting it should be more generic to be applicable across different networks. The group agreed that while Hedera would run the initial ceremony, other Hiero-based networks could potentially use the output, and the HIP should be more abstract to allow for different implementations. Mark acknowledged the feedback and agreed to revise the HIP, taking it back to the drawing board for further refinement.
