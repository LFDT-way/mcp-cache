The Hiro project's TSC meeting focused on its successful graduation from the Renox Foundation's Decentralized Trust, with discussions about new maintenance standards and the upcoming TSC election process. ## Details

### Hiro Project TSC Meeting Overview

Hendrik welcomed attendees to the Hiro project's TSC meeting, emphasizing that it is a public meeting accessible to everyone. He highlighted the availability of meeting invites through the Hiro org webpage, GitHub, and the public calendar, which also includes recordings and AI summaries for missed events. Hendrik encouraged attendees to subscribe to the calendar and stressed the importance of fostering a friendly and inclusive community under the Linux Foundation's code of conduct.

### Hiro Project Graduation Success

The meeting focused on the successful graduation of the Hiro project under the Renox Foundation's Decentralized Trust, marking it as the first project to transition from incubation to graduation within a year. Hendrik and Jessica highlighted the team's efforts in meeting the stricter graduation requirements and maintaining the project's status, with Jessica emphasizing the need to define and adhere to new maintenance standards. Diane praised the team's patience and detailed work in navigating the process, while also mentioning the need for a blog post reviewing the past year's releases. The discussion also touched on the OpenSSF scorecards and their impact on project evaluation, with Hendrik explaining the challenges faced and the ongoing efforts to improve the tools.

### TSC Election Schedule Announced

Hendrik announced that a new election for the TSC will take place next month, as all seats have been filled for the past two months. The election will involve the first seat, currently held by George, which needs to be re-elected due to the two-year term limit. Nominations can be submitted until September 9th, with a presentation of candidates scheduled for September 10th. The election will run for 12 days, concluding shortly before the TSC meeting on September 23rd, when the new TSC member will be announced. Jessica from LF will handle the voting process, with Turin preparing the necessary arrangements.

### New Contributor Roles and FAQ

Hendrik discussed two main topics: an FAQ document for maintainers and committers, and the addition of two new roles: Project Triage Contributor and Project Board Contributor. He explained that these new roles would allow project managers and product leads to manage Hashgraph engineers working on projects without requiring technical contributions. Hendrik also addressed a question about creating a role that can review but not merge pull requests, deciding that this functionality is already available to all contributors and may not require a special role.

### Hashgraph Improvement Proposals Process

The meeting focused on the process of handling Hips (Hashgraph Improvement Proposals) within the TSC (Technical Steering Committee). Richard explained that anyone can prepare a HIP and present it to the TSC for discussion and voting, with a minimum of two weeks allocated for review unless otherwise needed. He emphasized that HIPs can be withdrawn or deferred if circumstances change, and implementation is not mandatory but can be done by anyone, including the original proposer. Alex raised a concern about maintaining a clean HIP list, suggesting a process to review and potentially withdraw or replace outdated proposals, which the group acknowledged as a valuable consideration for future governance.

### Cross-SDK Hip Implementation Strategy

Hendrik discussed the implementation of Hips across different SDKs and programming languages, emphasizing the need for collaboration between SDK maintainers and the consensus node team. He proposed that the SDK community meetings could focus on brainstorming Hip implementations and required skills. Hendrik also mentioned a new proposal template for project proposals, which includes information such as openSSF scorecard, releases, contributors, and project scope, to help the TSC make better decisions on project incubation.

### TSC Elections Communication Improvements

The team discussed improving communication about TSC elections, with Milan suggesting the addition of clear terminology explanations for different roles like end users, contributors, and maintainers. Hendrik agreed this was a good idea and added it to his task list, while also noting that Jessica and he had completed documentation from the past two weeks. The team set a new goal to create a TSC voting menu for the following week.

### Specifications Organization and Control Discussion

Keith proposed moving the DID-Hedera specification to the Hiero repository, sparking a discussion about the organization of specifications. Richard expressed confusion about the purpose and control of these specifications, leading to a clarification by Keith about following industry standards for identity management. The group agreed that specifications could be added to Hiero through a HIP (Hedera Improvement Proposal) process, with Keith committing to create a HIP for the noncreds repo.
