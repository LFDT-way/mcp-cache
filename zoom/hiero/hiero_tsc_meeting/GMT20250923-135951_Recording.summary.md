## Details

### TSC Meeting: New Member Announcement

The Technical Steering Committee (TSC) meeting was held with a public attendance of over 20 people. Hendrik introduced the meeting, emphasizing its public nature and adherence to the Linux Foundation's Code of Conduct and antitrust policy. The minutes from the previous meeting were approved, and the HIPS tracker was reviewed, noting the anticipation of three upcoming HIPS in the near future. The conversation ended with the announcement of Georgie Razorov as the new TSC member, replacing George Spazov.

### TSC Elections and Hacktoberfest Updates

Georgi was elected to the TSC and will be onboarded this week. The team agreed to continue holding community calls for TSC elections, as it helped introduce candidates to the community. Milan and Hendrik discussed the success of the recent election process and the positive impact of the community calls. Hendrik also shared updates on preparing for Hacktoberfest, including creating a new webpage to showcase good first issues and a contributor highlighting page.

### Hacktoberfest Guidelines and Promotion

The team discussed Hacktoberfest participation and guidelines for Hiero sub-projects. Hendrik explained he is writing guidelines for Hacktoberfest contributions, including a process where issues will be reviewed before receiving the Hacktoberfest label. The group explored ways to promote Hiero's good first issues, with Michael suggesting a landing page and Brandon offering to manage an X account for Hiero. Diane advised that any social media accounts should be owned by the Linux Foundation, not individuals.

### Twitter Account Transition Discussion

The group discussed transitioning Twitter account ownership from individual members to the LFDT organization, with Brandon emphasizing the importance of elevating Hiero as a brand and suggesting weekly updates. Jessica highlighted the need to maintain brand consistency between LFDT and Hiero Twitter accounts. The team agreed to vote on the clean fees HIP proposal next week without an additional presentation, while Keith and Richard reviewed the updated proposal. Hendrik confirmed that HIP 1261 would be voted on in the following week.

### Identity Standards Consolidation Proposal

The team discussed a proposal to consolidate identity standards, including DID Hedera and Anon-CREDS, into a single Hiero repository. Keith explained that the repository would follow the HIP process for managing updates and changes. Alex raised concerns about conformance testing for these standards, suggesting the need for a test suite to ensure implementations align with base specifications. The team agreed this was an important consideration for future development, though not a blocker for the current proposal.

### Identity Standards and Tools Proposals

The meeting focused on two main proposals: creating a Hiero identity standards project and establishing a Homebrew Tools project. The first proposal was approved by vote, with the new project to house multiple DIT method specifications under one repository, following the HIP process for changes. The Homebrew Tools proposal was discussed but deferred to the next meeting due to technical questions about implementation details, with Michael requesting a visual diagram to better understand the architecture. The conversation ended with Alex raising the need to clarify expectations and value of maintainer reporting to the TSC, and Hendrik mentioned an upcoming LFDT meetup and Hackathon.
