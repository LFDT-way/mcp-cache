### HIRO Technical Steering Committee Meeting

Hendrik opened the Technical Steering Committee (TSC) call for the HIRO project, welcoming attendees and explaining that all TSC meetings are public and accessible through the project's website and calendar. The meeting focused on reviewing HIPs (High Importance Proposals), with Hendrik confirming that presentations on HIPs were given in the previous TSC call and voting on them would take place during this meeting.

### HIP 1427 Approval Discussion

The team discussed and voted on HIP 1427 regarding historical record files. All TSC members present voted in approval, and the HIP was successfully approved. Mark confirmed he or Joseph Sinclair (Hashgraph Foundry Inc. (DBA Hashgraph)) would present the second HIP for discussion, though the transcript ended before that presentation began.

### HIP 1424 Block Structure Discussion

Mark presented HIP 1424, which describes block structure and hashing logic, focusing on two main aspects: preventing pre-image attacks through additional prefix bits that identify node types (leaf, single child, or two children), and implementing subtree structures within the block tree to enable efficient verification and state proofs. Joseph Sinclair (Hashgraph Foundry Inc. (DBA Hashgraph)) clarified that including the entire state and previous blocks in the block tree allows for state proofs and verification of older block data. The team agreed to review the HIP over the next week before voting.

### HIP Voting Timeline Discussion

The team discussed voting on a HIP (Hedera Improvement Proposal) that is mathematically and structurally complex, with concerns about timing due to HederaCon next week. Hendrik proposed conducting an asynchronous vote this week to avoid conflicts with HederaCon, and the group agreed to cancel next week's TSC meeting. Mark clarified that the HIP is not currently blocking development of the block node deployment, allowing the team time to review and understand it before making any necessary changes.
