### HIP Proposal Asset Tokenization Discussion

Brandon presented a HIP proposal for a real-world asset tokenization platform that requires ongoing fees to cover storage and insurance costs. The team discussed challenges with their initial transfer-based fee structure, particularly regarding regulatory requirements and money transmitter licenses. After legal counsel feedback, they determined a wipe-based approach would be more appropriate, though this requires further investigation and potential modifications to the current HIP draft. The group agreed to make necessary adjustments to the HIP and continue exploring solutions that could benefit other asset issuers on Hedera.

### HIP Proposals and System Updates

The team discussed two HIP proposals. Brandon and Jesse presented a token maintenance fee system for HTMS tokens, explaining that fees would be collected through a "wipe" functionality rather than direct token transfers to avoid OFAC compliance issues with third-party token holders on decentralized exchanges. Richard and Jesse explored alternative smart contract implementations, discussing the trade-offs between platform-integrated features versus customizable smart contract solutions, including considerations around attack surface, complexity, and upgrade controls.Ty then presented HIP 1068 on sponsored fees, which would allow dApp users to perform transactions without pre-paying gas fees by using sponsored accounts. The team agreed to conduct an asynchronous vote on the sponsored fees HIP next week due to Hendrik's upcoming vacation. Sophie raised concerns about communication delays with maintainers in Hyro, particularly regarding PR reviews and SDK support, which Hendrik acknowledged and committed to addressing with Hashgraph leadership before his vacation.
