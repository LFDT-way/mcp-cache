Jessica discussed two main goals related to security and project roadmaps. For goal two, she explained ongoing work with the release engineering team and dev operations to improve security and transparency, including creating official documentation for minimal requirements and working to improve OpenSSF scorecards. For goal three, she outlined plans to encourage maintainers to publish project roadmaps, which Diane suggested could be helped by creating example roadmaps and holding workshops for maintainers to learn how to create them.

### Hiero 2026 Goals Review

The meeting focused on reviewing and discussing the 2026 goals for the Hiero project. Jessica presented the goals, which included increasing TSC engagement, improving roadmap communication, enhancing specification processes, and promoting training and onboarding initiatives. Sophie raised concerns about the TSC's current focus on technical issues and suggested expanding the TSC's role to include community engagement and support infrastructure. Diane explained the purpose of the TSC and emphasized the need to build the TSC's capacity to address non-technical aspects of project management. The group discussed the importance of issue progression for training new contributors and the need for more case studies and use cases to showcase the project's ecosystem adoption. They also touched on goals related to innovation, cross-project collaboration, and potential new opportunities for the project. The conversation ended with an invitation for maintainers to provide feedback on the goals and share their thoughts on the proposed direction.
