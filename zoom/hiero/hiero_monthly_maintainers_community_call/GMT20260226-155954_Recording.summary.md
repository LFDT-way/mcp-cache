## Details

### 2026 Project Goals Discussion

The meeting focused on maintaining communication with project maintainers and discussing 2026 project goals. Jessica introduced the agenda and mentioned a presentation about Code Rabbit that might be moved to the next community call. The group discussed the 2026 project goals, which were previously presented at a TSC meeting. Jessica encouraged attendees to review the goals and provide feedback, noting that some of these initiatives are already in progress, particularly in areas of project participation, engagement, and security.

### Linux Foundation Project Goals Update

The team discussed project goals and their progress, with Jessica mentioning that initiatives are being created by different teams to achieve these goals and that progress will be reflected in reports to the Technical Advisory Council of the Linux Foundation. Sophie raised the topic of adding analytics to the Hiero repo, and Hendrik suggested creating a separate repo for scripts and uploading results to the Hiero repository. The group also talked about OpenSSF scorecards, with Jessica explaining how to find and interpret them, and emphasizing the importance of addressing any vulnerabilities flagged by the scorecards.

### OpenSSF Scorecard Implementation Strategy

The team discussed the OpenSSF scorecard and its importance for project health assessment. Jessica explained that while some improvements are challenging, such as implementing branch protection rules and signed releases, the team can focus on easier tasks like cryptographic signing of releases. Sophie suggested creating a support system for maintainers to engage more effectively with these tasks. The group agreed to bring this topic into a deeper discussion during the next community call, with Hendrik and Jessica planning to prepare monthly snapshots of scorecard values for maintainers to track progress.

### Community Engagement Strategies for Hiero

The meeting focused on strategies to increase community engagement and contributions to the Hiero project. Jessica discussed plans to integrate metrics into Maintainers calls and emphasized the importance of inviting the open community to participate in core component module calls. Sophie presented a detailed approach for onboarding new contributors, including the use of Good First Issues and a junior committer role to build skills and trust. Hendrik highlighted the success of this strategy in the Python SDK and its potential application across other SDKs. The team also discussed the use of CodeWebit's AI tools for issue description and pull request reviews, with Hendrik raising concerns about future costs and suggesting that the Genox Foundation could provide free access for Linux Foundation projects.
