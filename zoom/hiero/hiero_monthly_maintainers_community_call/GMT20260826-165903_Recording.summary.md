## Details

### TSE Nominations and Maintainer Cleanup

The team discussed two main topics: TSE nominations and inactive committers/maintainers cleanup. Jessica announced that nominations for TSE positions are open until September 8th, with a two-step voting process involving maintainers first selecting two candidates, followed by TSEs choosing one additional candidate for the third position. The team also reviewed PRs created by Sophie to remove inactive committers and maintainers who haven't been active for six months, with current maintainers being asked to review these PRs and provide feedback in the comments.

### Repository Cleanup Progress Update

The team discussed repository cleanup efforts led by Sophie, who was revealed to be Explorer 3. Sophie reported that 2-3 PRs had been merged, with some rejected as not applicable and others requesting specific changes. The group addressed challenges with reaching maintainers through GitHub notifications and agreed to continue promoting the cleanup efforts in TSC meetings, with Jessica offering to email maintainers as well. The discussion concluded with plans to address inactive committers and updates to responsibilities and maintainership documents.

### Commit Guidelines and Contributor Process

Hendrik presented updated guidelines with added responsibilities for committers and maintainers, requesting feedback on the new paragraphs. Jessica confirmed the PR is open for discussion and encouraged participants to review and comment on the proposed changes. The meeting also covered an overview of Hiero Hackers, an organization aimed at providing a sandbox environment for contributors to test new ideas and features that could potentially integrate with Hiero Ledger, with a simple process involving issue creation in the governance repo for repository requests.

### Hiero Hackers Analytics Repository Review

Jessica presented the Hiero Hackers analytics repository, which shows contributor activity and metrics including 44 outside contributors. The group discussed that anyone can review or comment on the repositories without special configuration, but becoming a committer or maintainer requires manual addition by Jessica or Sophie. Sophie explained that the approval processes vary by repository, with analytics requiring more formal approval while other repos like SDK Automations have lighter requirements.

### OpenSSF Scorecard Performance Discussion

The team discussed OpenSSF scorecard performance across Hiero repositories, with many scores currently below 7, the minimum target. Daniel proposed assigning dedicated maintainers to monitor and improve scorecard metrics, while Jessica explained that organization owners can modify branch protection rules to help boost scores. The group agreed to aim for a minimum score of 7 and ideally 8, with specific improvements like signed releases and fuzzing being discussed as relatively easy implementations. The meeting also touched on AI usage policies, with Hendrik noting ongoing discussions at the Linux Foundation level about establishing guidelines for AI-assisted contributions, and the team agreed to schedule further discussion about AI policies for the next community call.
