## Details

Hendrik announced the Hyro Community Day in Berlin scheduled for July 7th, which will be held at the Treehouse venue as a free event in conjunction with the We Are Developers World Congress. The event will feature presentations and networking opportunities, with Hendrik planning to conduct a workshop on AI and related topics. Jessica expressed interest in attending and helping with presentations, particularly given her upcoming talk on open governance for open source projects at Rare Evo. ### Issue Moderation Workflow Updates

Angie presented new issue moderation workflows implemented across the website, Enterprise Java, and Python repositories that automatically close issues for community member review and require maintainers to approve them before they can be interacted with again. The system also automatically closes pull requests without linked issues to help maintainers understand the context of the work. Jessica noted that more maintainers should have attended the presentation and suggested either creating documentation for them to review or presenting the information again in the upcoming TSC or community call on Thursday.

### Maintainer Guideline and Automation Discussion

The team discussed presenting a maintainer guideline document to the TSC, with Hendrik suggesting they should first get the maintainer guideline merged and approved before presenting additional automation features. Angie shared a public document containing ideas for automating PR management and image generation for blog posts, noting that some content was adapted from Sophie's work and would need her involvement to avoid conflicts. The team reviewed a Save the Date announcement for an upcoming event created using Claude, and discussed potentially creating a temporary webpage for the event. Jessica presented data on community growth and issue progression, highlighting the benefits of the triage role and comparing contributor statistics with LFX Insights data.
