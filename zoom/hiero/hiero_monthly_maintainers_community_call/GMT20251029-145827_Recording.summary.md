## Details

### Maintainers Meeting: Issues and Agendas

Jessica led the first maintainers monthly meeting, discussing two main topics: good first issues and org-level discussions. She emphasized the importance of maintaining agendas for future meetings and sought maintainers' help in addressing technical discussions that were difficult for her to handle alone. The team agreed to review good first issues and provide feedback on the process and guidelines, with Jessica planning to complete the agenda documentation this week.

### Repository Maintenance and Discussion Management

Jessica discussed the need for maintainers to address discussions and issues in the organization's repositories, emphasizing the importance of timely responses and moving relevant conversations to the appropriate repos. She highlighted that while individual repo maintainers are responsible for their respective discussions, she would appreciate more support from all maintainers to manage discussions at the organizational level. Nadine acknowledged her unawareness of the discussions and expressed willingness to help. Hendrik agreed with Jessica's concerns about unaddressed discussions but noted the challenge of determining when a discussion is resolved, suggesting a need for a clear process to mark discussions as answered.

### Enhancing Maintainer Project Engagement

Hendrik and Jessica discussed the need to increase developer and maintainer participation in project discussions, particularly for topics like server-side components. They agreed to invite maintainers to the monthly meeting through calendar invites, which maintainers could easily decline if they preferred not to attend. Jessica emphasized the importance of active maintainer involvement and noted that internal conversations need to be more open and centralized on Discord. Hendrik offered to help obtain email addresses for maintainers from Hashgraph and other teams.

### Discord Integration for SDK Maintainers

Jessica and Hendrik discussed promoting conversations to Discord, with Hendrik providing an update on his conversation with Simi, the manager of SDKs at Hashgraph. They agreed to record the meeting and make it available for maintainers who couldn't attend, with Hendrik suggesting it be shared in the maintainer channel. Sophie raised concerns about the difficulty of joining Discord channels, particularly for the Python SDK, and suggested adding a Discord MD and using long number strings for links.

### Discord Channel Management Improvements

The team discussed issues with Discord invitations and channel organization. Sophie and Nadine highlighted problems with finding specific channels and being notified about irrelevant mentions, while Jessica suggested adding a dedicated Discord section alongside meeting recordings. The group debated whether to rename the Hyperledger Discord to something more specific, like "LFDT Discord," but no final decision was made. Roger informed Sophie that she could fix her mention notifications by adjusting her Discord settings.

### Enhancing Discord for Contributor Engagement

The team discussed improving communication and contributor engagement for the Hiero projects. They decided to create a Discord channel for Python contributors and use threads for easier organization. Sophie raised concerns about the effectiveness of Discord reactions and the need for better engagement. The group agreed to focus on improving the Discord channel rather than creating new GitHub teams. They also discussed the potential use of "easy," "medium," and "hard" labels for good first issues. Hendrik shared plans to involve Hashgraph maintainers in the Discord channel, with Simi helping to coordinate efforts. The team concluded that good first issues should be well-documented and up-to-date, but different repositories can maintain their own templates as long as they align with the overall goals.
