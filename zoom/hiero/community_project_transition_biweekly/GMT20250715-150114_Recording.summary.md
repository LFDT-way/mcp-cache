Discussion, questions and concerns on community driven project's transition into hiero-ledger

## Overview

The meeting covered various topics including travel experiences, new features for meeting recordings, and the status of several projects and repositories. Discussions focused on transferring repositories to the Hiero organization, implementing best practices, and improving project health monitoring. The team also addressed organizational structure concerns and the need for promoting upcoming meetings to key parties.

## Details

### Travel Experiences and Home Misses

Hendrik shared that he was currently in Bergen, Germany, and would be returning home in a few days after a week of travel. He expressed a preference for traveling occasionally rather than frequently, as he had done for 5 weeks straight. Ry recounted his experience of spending over half the year outside the US for work, which led to a humorous realization of how much he had come to miss home comforts.

### YouTube Meeting Recording Beta Test

The team discussed a new feature where meetings are automatically uploaded to YouTube as a beta test, which Ry demonstrated. Jessica expressed concern about being recorded, while Hendrik noted that he would not have noticed the recording notification. Ry mentioned that this was a test based on previous successful 2-minute meeting recordings, and Hendrik agreed that YouTube uploads make it easier to comment and find content.

### Hiero HAC SDK Project Review

Jessica raised concerns about the status of Michael's Hiero HAC SDK JS project, noting that while Andrew felt it could be moved to the Hiero Ledger organization, he was uncertain about the repository's security meeting organizational requirements. Andrew mentioned that Roger might have more information and would follow up with him, while Jessica noted that Richard had previously approved the TSC but had not yet reviewed the project.

### Repository Transfer and SDK Updates

The team discussed the transfer of several repositories to the Hiero organization. Jessica and Andrew agreed to be added to the mailing thread for the Hierode SDK project, which is currently in a mailing sweat. Hendrik mentioned that he would schedule a meeting to bring the repository over to Hiero, with security settings being a priority. He also discussed a new project, Hedera Cai, which is a command line interface for interacting with the Hedera network, and plans to meet with the main developer to explore its potential for use with any Hiero network.

### OpenSSF Scorecard Implementation Discussion

Jessica and Ry discussed the need to implement best practices and ensure project health monitoring, with Ry mentioning tools developed with Tracy for increased visibility. They also talked about adding projects to the OpenSSF scorecard, with Jessica planning to discuss a workflow with Nathan. Hendrik inquired about the current process for the OpenSSF scorecard, and Ry explained the benefits of using GitHub Actions for more frequent and granular checks. The group also briefly discussed the lack of chat message availability on YouTube for live streams.

### Repository Scores and Security Discussion

Jessica shared a list of repositories that need to improve their scores, noting that 2 are nearly there and 7 more need work. She mentioned that these scores are already public in readmis and won't affect graduation, but it would be nice to have them reviewed. Andrew brought up a topic about creating a separate organization to publish repositories that may not meet security standards, which needs to go through the TSC. Jessica mentioned reviving a discussion about this in the chat, referencing a conversation with Diane, Michael, Keith, and Sophie.

### Project Organization Structure Debate

The group discussed the organization structure for their projects, particularly whether to maintain a split between a main organization and an ecosystem organization. Hendrik expressed concern about differing opinions on the future structure, while Ry suggested a more flexible approach where projects could move between organizations as needed. The discussion highlighted a need for clarity on whether projects should be excluded from the main organization due to security requirements or if they should be included but meet specific standards. Jessica reminded the group about an upcoming task force meeting that was not promoted during the DSC meeting.

### Promoting Weekly Community Meeting

Jessica and Hendrik discussed the need to promote a meeting scheduled for Thursdays after the community call weekly. They agreed to use the hybrid channel in the Hashcraft Slack and the Discord general announcement channel to reach out to key parties. Roger mentioned that he had not heard any updates on the Hiero HCS SDK JS from internal discussions or from Richard, and they decided to wait for further information. The conversation ended with no additional topics for discussion, as Andrew and Roger were busy with other internal matters.
