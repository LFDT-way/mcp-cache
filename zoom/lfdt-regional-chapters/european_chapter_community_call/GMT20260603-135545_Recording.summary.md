### Blockchain Network Deployment Tool

David presented Chainlunch, a tool he developed for deploying and managing blockchain networks including Hyperledger Fabric, Besu, and Fabric X. He demonstrated how Chainlunch can deploy networks with multiple organizations and nodes on a single VPS without requiring Kubernetes, emphasizing its benefits for speed, cost efficiency, and ease of use compared to Kubernetes-based solutions. David also explained the tool's governance features, showing how network policies can be updated through a proposal system that requires signatures from multiple organizations to implement changes.

### Blockchain Network Deployment Solutions

David explained the differences and challenges between spinning off a network based on Fabric versus a monolithic approach, highlighting increased complexity and resource requirements due to the separation of components like orderer groups and peers. He demonstrated how Chainlaunch simplifies blockchain network deployment and management through its unified control plane, reducing setup time from weeks to minutes and enabling features like certificate authority management, network synchronization, and disaster recovery. The conversation ended with Maciej announcing the next European Chapter meeting in September and inviting attendees to reach out to David for further questions or demonstrations.
