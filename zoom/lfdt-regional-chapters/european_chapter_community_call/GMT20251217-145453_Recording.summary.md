## Details

### LF Decentralized Trust European Chapter

Maciej introduced the LF Decentralized Trust European Chapter, an initiative that began earlier in the year to foster blockchain innovation, collaboration, and knowledge sharing across Europe. The chapter aims to build a vibrant, inclusive community contributing to LF Decentralized Trust projects and serving as a regional hub for distributed ledger and decentralized technologies. Maciej welcomed two distinguished guests, Dr. Besfort Shala and Dr. Errol Kazam, both working at the Deutsche Bundesbank, who will present on advancing DLT in central banking and the Trigger solution.

### CBDC and DLT Interoperability Solutions

Besfort and Errol presented on the Deutsche Bundesbank's work in digital currencies and distributed ledger technology (DLT). They explained the Bundesbank's wholesale CBDC project, which aims to enable the settlement of DLT-based financial transactions using central bank money. The presentation covered the Eurosystem's exploration of interoperability solutions, including the Bundesbank's "trigger solution" that acts as a bridge between market DLT platforms and traditional payment systems. They also discussed the Eurosystem's upcoming projects Pontus and Appia, with Pontus aiming to provide a production-ready solution by the end of next year.

### Hyperledger Fabric Trigger Solution Discussion

The meeting focused on discussing the functionality and feasibility of a trigger solution using Hyperledger Fabric 2.5 for facilitating transactions between companies. Dominik inquired about the platform's throughput, but Besfort explained that the exploratory phase did not prioritize realistic transaction speeds, emphasizing instead the solution's functionality. Besfort also detailed the solution's architecture, highlighting the integration of APIs, the possibility for market participants to operate their nodes, and the use of HTLC as an interoperability protocol. The discussion concluded with an overview of the system's components, including the internal integration server, network service providers, and the connection to a traditional payment system.

### Trigger Solution Discussion Overview

The team discussed the trigger solution, which serves as a messaging system for creating payment instructions with unique IDs and managing them in a queue. Agnieszka raised a question about transaction finality and double-spending prevention, to which Besfort explained that the current implementation does not face this issue due to the way payment instructions are processed. Imre inquired about the necessity of using a blockchain between the market DLT and the RTGS, to which Besfort clarified that the trigger solution was developed to provide market participants with an alternative to use their blockchain for various use cases, including programmability that RTGS lacks.

### Smart Contract Isolation Strategy

The discussion focused on the implementation of smart contracts on a trigger solution, which covers wholesale CBDC and DLT exploration. Besfort explained that while smart contracts were initially explored as a key feature, the Bundesbank adopted a strategy to isolate external chain codes by not installing them on their own peers, thus maintaining security while allowing market participants to deploy their own nodes and contracts. Imre raised concerns about the potential risks of external smart contracts affecting the Hyperledger Fabric's performance, to which Besfort clarified that the Bundesbank's peers do not participate in endorsement or validation processes for transactions involving external chain codes, ensuring isolation and maintaining atomicity of money transfers.

### DLT Solutions for Wholesale CBDC

The meeting focused on exploring Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) solutions, particularly in the context of wholesale CBDC and payment systems. Besfort and Erol from the Bundesbank presented their institution's approach to DLT, emphasizing an agnostic and exploratory stance. They described their modular architecture using EVM and ZKStack, with plans to expand to other virtual machines in the future. The team is currently focusing on capital market services but is open to supporting other business departments. Questions were raised about interoperability, identity management, and the use of zero-knowledge proofs for compliance verification. The conversation ended with an invitation to connect at upcoming events in January.
