### AML and SSI: Regulatory Evolution

Stoyan explained the growing importance of AML (Anti-Money Laundering) regulations and their connection to SSI (Self-Sovereign Identity) solutions, particularly in financial services and other industries. He detailed how AML requirements have evolved from simple identity checks to complex compliance needs, including travel rule responsibilities for crypto asset service providers. Stoyan predicted that as AML regulations continue to expand to new sectors like commodity trading and luxury goods, the need for SSI solutions will increase, especially for privately hosted identities.
