### Blockchain Adoption in Financial Institutions

Szymon presented findings from a blockchain survey conducted among financial institutions, revealing that 9 out of 10 respondents had blockchain-based projects in their organizations, contrary to the initial hypothesis that blockchain was overhyped. The survey identified several emerging trends including tokenized commodities, treasury solutions, embedded finance, and crypto lending, with tokenized commodities and treasuries showing particular promise. Szymon noted that organizations implementing these solutions were often seeking competitive advantages and building internal expertise, while treasury projects were sometimes used as tools to reach new demographics.

### Blockchain Survey Findings Presentation

Szymon presented findings from a blockchain survey, discussing various project types including tokenized treasuries, embedded finance, and crypto lending. He highlighted key concerns among respondents, such as blockchain volatility, public network governance, and cybersecurity issues, while noting that different project types had varying levels of concern about these factors. Szymon emphasized the importance of clearly defining goals and understanding stakeholders' concerns when working with blockchain solutions, particularly for teams with limited blockchain experience.

### Blockchain Survey Insights Presentation

Szymon presented insights from a blockchain survey, noting significant changes in the approach toward blockchain in businesses compared to the previous year. He highlighted that while regulatory concerns were previously a major issue, respondents now pointed to specific regulations like MICA and BNB in Brazil as key factors. Szymon also discussed the growing importance of stablecoins, particularly USDC and USDT, which are becoming increasingly dominant in the market. When asked about regional differences, Szymon indicated that while stablecoins were more significant in Europe and the United States, no clear regional variations emerged in this year's survey.
