Yurii clarified that this specification originated from Aries RFCs and is used in various SSI systems, offering standardization for credential display across devices. Roberto expressed interest in researching how to display credentials as a product, with Sandra's help to narrow down the options, while Yurii noted the complexity of the specification due to its multiple categories for different aspects like multi-language support and format.

### Multi-Format Credentials Discussion

The team discussed the concept of multi-format credentials, with Gonçalo explaining his initial idea of a credential wrapper containing multiple credential types. Fábio raised concerns about interoperability and the potential creation of new standards, while Gonçalo acknowledged the complexity of the issue and suggested exploring W3C specifications. The group considered the possibility of implementing multi-format credentials in the Cloud Agent to automatically provide credentials in different formats when requested by wallets.

### Multiple Credential Formats Discussion

The team discussed the possibility of supporting multiple credential formats within a single JSON structure, with Roberto explaining that this could be achieved by attaching credentials inside other credentials. While Yurii and Fabio expressed concerns about creating a new credential format, they agreed that this could be addressed by executing multiple flows for different credential types. Gonçalo suggested creating a GitHub discussion and sending an email to W3C to check their thoughts on this approach. The team concluded that while the increased file size might not be a significant issue, they should focus on other topics for now and revisit this discussion in the future.
