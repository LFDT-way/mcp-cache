### CSER Technical Specification Approval Update

Drummond explained that CSER is under the Carrie Stack Working Group and has been approved as the first technical specification from Trust over IP. He noted that the process for approving deliverables is now established, and a blog post about the approval is planned to be released before upcoming conferences in Utah. Drummond also discussed the status of DID-SKID, which is waiting to go through the DIF methods working group's recommendation process, and mentioned two existing implementations. Scott raised the importance of incorporating work on a DID-X509 spec into the High Assurance Verifiable Identifiers (HAVID) task force to enhance cross-interoperability between different identifier systems.

### ToIP Working Group Updates

The meeting focused on updates to the Trust over IP (ToIP) working group and its task forces. Drummond reported that Alex Tweedale, who had previously led the cross-correlation task force, has returned and released a new version of the spec on GitHub. The group discussed bringing in Cary to help with infrastructure components and agreed to resurrect the task force. Reza proposed creating a new task force focused on homomorphic encryption, which Drummond guided him through the process of proposing. The group also discussed upcoming presentations at the Internet Identity Workshop (IIW), including plans for COG, C2PA, decentralized trust graph, and ZKPs. Reza mentioned plans to demo a new QR code-based credential system at IIW, and the group noted that the Harvard Applied Social Media Lab team would also be demonstrating their ASML wallet at a separate event.
