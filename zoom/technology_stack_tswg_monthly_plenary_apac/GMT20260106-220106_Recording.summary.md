### Trust Protocol and DID Implementations

Drummond discussed the current implementations of the trust planning protocol, including the Web of Trust and the TSP project at the Open Wallet Foundation. He mentioned that the specifications for Trust OP and the DIDSGID need to be updated with current documents and references. Drummond also explained the process for DIF recommended DID methods and noted that all current methods are self-certifying identifiers. He highlighted the importance of DID SKID as a meta method for standardizing self-certifying identifiers. Finally, Drummond shared that Glenn Gore, working on a Linux kernel maintainer verification project, successfully implemented DID SKID for peer-to-peer use of WebVH.

### DIDSCID Integration and Trust Registry

Drummond explained that implementing DID methods to be DIDSCID compatible is straightforward, involving only parsing and using existing code for reformable history processing. John mentioned that Alex Tweedale, who was previously involved in high assurance VIDs work, has been uncontactable since December and is currently in Africa, though he was collaborating on UN projects. Scott Perry expressed interest in using high assurance VIDs for trust registry work, and John noted that Jo and he have been encouraging Steve Capell, who leads UNTP work, to adopt DIDSCID as a more robust generalization of DID Web methods. John also discussed a challenge from his Spanish registrar co-lead regarding the integration of GTR Grid with the EU's WeBuild consortium and VLI/Glyph, highlighting the importance of cryptography in these systems.
