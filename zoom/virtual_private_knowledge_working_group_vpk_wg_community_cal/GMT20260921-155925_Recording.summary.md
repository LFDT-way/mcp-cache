### Confidential Vector Search Protocol

Reza led a discussion on Virtual Private Knowledge (VPK), focusing on enabling confidential vector search by scrambling data before transmission to untrusted service providers. He outlined three working groups: one to specify and standardize the VPK protocol, another to research custom vector embeddings, and a third to develop solutions for confidential vector search. The team discussed the use of partially homomorphic encryption for dot product calculations and emphasized the need for a solution that balances security with conversational speed, potentially allowing for sharding and record reordering to enhance security.

### Vector Database Storage Options

Reza explained that Bob has the option to keep a local copy of the vector database or store it remotely, with updates being possible through insert record commands. Andrea, a guest with experience in digital preservation and AI-assisted archival materials, highlighted the importance of maintaining unaltered original assets and the challenges encryption poses in traditional archiving contexts. The discussion explored how AI and RAG systems handle knowledge bases and vector databases, with Reza noting differences between traditional archival challenges and the current context of uploading documents to third-party AI services.

### Encrypted Data Access Solutions

The group discussed the challenges of balancing data preservation and access, particularly in archival and encrypted contexts. They explored the use of homomorphic encryption and vector databases for searching encrypted data, with a focus on the VPK (vector proxy key) approach that allows for secure querying without exposing the original data. The discussion highlighted the distinction between disaster recovery archives like Iron Mountain and interactive, conversational-speed query capabilities. At the end of the meeting, Reza sought volunteers to lead the VPK protocol specification task force, with the first two initiatives ready to begin immediately while the custom vector embedding task is pending preliminary specification.

### VPK Working Group Progress Update

Reza led a discussion about the VPK (Verifiable Private Knowledge) working group, highlighting the progress of the Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group and encouraging participants to join the Linux Foundation as individual members. The group discussed potential task forces, with Mitchell expressing interest in working on custom vector embeddings and suggesting it might be premature to split into separate task forces. The team set a goal to have something substantial to report at the upcoming SoCal Linux Expo in April and discussed intellectual property rights for the project's work.
