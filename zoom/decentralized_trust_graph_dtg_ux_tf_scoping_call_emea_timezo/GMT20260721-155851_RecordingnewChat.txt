00:49:13	Carly Huitema (University of Guelph):	What happens at a key-signing party? A key-signing party is essentially an identity verification event. Alice meets Bob in person. Alice checks Bob's government-issued ID. Bob proves he controls his PGP key (usually by providing its fingerprint). Alice signs Bob's public key with her own private key. Bob may do the same for Alice. By signing Bob's key, Alice is saying: "I have personally verified that this public key belongs to Bob." She is not endorsing Bob's code quality—only the key-to-identity binding.
