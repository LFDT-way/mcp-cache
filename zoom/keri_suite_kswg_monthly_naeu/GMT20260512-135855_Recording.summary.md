### DID Web S Specification Issues

The team discussed feedback from the recent KERI conference and identified issues with the DID Web S specification that need addressing. Jonathan noted several problematic areas in the spec that require revision, suggesting they should take a month to reassess it before moving forward. Samuel acknowledged not having reviewed the spec yet and proposed discussing the issues in their upcoming meeting next week, with Jonathan and Kent planning to meet beforehand to catalog the specific problems. The discussion also touched on interoperability concerns regarding DID Web S and W3C edge credentials.

### DIDWeb Methods Discussion

The group discussed the use of DIDWeb methods, particularly focusing on DIDWebS and DIDWebH. Samuel explained that while DIDWebH doesn't currently support anchoring credential issuances, it does allow explicit authorization of host and path portions of identifiers, which is not the case with DIDWebH. Jonathan noted that DIDWebS has two main shortcomings compared to other methods: inability to anchor credential issuances and the explicit authorization feature. The discussion also touched on the security implications of malleability in DID methods, with Samuel emphasizing that while extensibility at the application layer is beneficial, extensibility at security layers creates problems. Samuel concluded by highlighting that most current DID methods, including DIDWeb methods other than DIDWebS, do not satisfy NIST's best practices for crypto period management in key management.

### KERI Implementation and Security Concerns

The group discussed concerns about SwissID's implementation of KERI, with Samuel highlighting potential security issues and noting that at least one community member acknowledged they were doing it incorrectly. Kent proposed splitting off KERIA Signify meetings from the current Tuesday slot, and after discussion with Samuel and Philip, agreed to schedule separate meetings during the two-week gaps between regular calls, or to continue discussions during implementer's calls when needed. Venu raised a topic about applying KERI and ACDC concepts to assets and blockchains, suggesting the use of DAGs and microledgers for decentralized account representation and transactions.

### KERI Asset Management Proposals

The group discussed approaches for using KERI in asset management, with three main proposals outlined by Samuel. The first proposal involves using a key event log to control an identifier, while the second proposal suggests a transaction event log transfer registry using ACDC. The third proposal, which Samuel recently introduced, involves adding a transfer event to the blinded state registry for delegator authorized transfers. Venu expressed interest in the second approach and agreed to explore developing a proposal further. Carly raised a question about the calculation method for URN Z, seeking community input to finalize a recommendation for determining the Z value.

### URN Processing Technical Alternatives Discussion

The team discussed two technical alternatives for handling URN colon set prefixes in document processing. Kent and Samuel explained the differences between a pre-processing step (option A) and a post-processing step (option B), with Samuel raising concerns about Base64 serialization complications with option A. After considering these technical implications, the group aligned on selecting the post-processing approach (option B), though they agreed to potentially revisit the decision in more detail at a later time.
