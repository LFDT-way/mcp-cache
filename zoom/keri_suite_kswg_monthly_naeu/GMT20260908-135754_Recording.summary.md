## Overview

This was the TrustRover IP KERI Stack Working Group meeting where the team reviewed action items, discussed upcoming conferences, and addressed technical issues. Samuel announced three major developments: Utah will formally adopt KERI and ACDC with KERI sessions planned at an upcoming summit, a mini-conference will be held before IAW with a call for speakers now open on KERIconf.com, and ISO announced they will remove paywalls from their specifications. The group also discussed reports that the city of Lugano may adopt SETI (which implies KERI adoption), with Christopher Allen reportedly serving as consultant for this initiative. Henk noted strong interest in KERI among European attendees at GDC, with multiple people advocating for KERI's role in various sessions.

### PIDFest Presentation and Specification Updates

Carly announced her upcoming presentation at PIDFest in the Netherlands on October 27-29, where she will discuss SET and its value in research identifiers, and invited team members to potentially meet up for dinner in Leiden. The team discussed renaming the "U field" from "UUID" to "unique entropy" in the ACDC specification, with Daniel proposing the change to avoid confusion since the field functions as a salty nonce rather than a true identifier. Samuel agreed with the terminology change and suggested implementing it in version 2.1 to avoid breaking existing code while deprecating the UUID parameter.

The team discussed issues with pull request reviews and merging, particularly regarding changes to specifications and SpecUpT-related code. They agreed to implement required reviews for specs and identified the need to revert a post-quantum codes merge until proper review could be completed. Henk presented a comprehensive project on curating and segmenting KERI conference footage, creating a value system graph and labeled segments that can be used for training and answering questions about the KERI suite. The team expressed appreciation for this work and discussed how the curated content could help promote KERI as an identifier system.
