### Abstract Selected for PidFest Announcement

Carly announced that her abstract was selected for PidFest in Leiden in October. The meeting began with participants joining, and Samuel suggested adding the announcement to the agenda.

### Trust Over IP Policy Update

The team discussed updating the Trust Over IP policy notice following the organization's transition from a foundation to a Linux Foundation project. Carly agreed to check if the current antitrust policy remains valid under the new project structure, as the membership relationship with the Linux Foundation has changed. The discussion clarified that while Trust Over IP is now under the Linux Foundation's decentralized trust (LFDT), it operates as a separate project with different membership rules and financial capabilities compared to member organizations.

### Persistent Identifiers Terminology Discussion

Carly announced she submitted an abstract to PIDFest about persistent identifiers (PIDs) and will run a workshop on their usage, including discussion of the EUDI namespace. The team identified a terminology conflict where PID means "Persistent Identifier" in research but refers to "Person Identification Data" (verifiable credentials/PII) in EUDI nomenclature. Samuel clarified that EUDI PIDs are not cryptographically derived identifiers but rather verifiable credentials, and the discussion touched on different approaches to PID management including central authority and decentralized cryptographic methods.

### UBI Registration Specification Updates

The meeting covered several key updates and discussions. Kevin reported on a specification request for UBI registration under the well-known RFC, which requires expert review and submission via GitHub. Phil noted that current WUBI processing in Cary P needs updating to reflect the new spec requirements. Samuel provided an update on Cary Py, indicating ongoing work to eliminate technical debt and migrate code to be spec-compliant with published versions of the specs, particularly moving from version 1 to version 2 of Cary, ACDC, and Caesar. The conversation ended with Venu preparing to present a discussion topic from the previous meeting.

### URN SED and Digital Asset Management

The team discussed two main topics: URN SED generation and Venu's presentation on digital asset management. Regarding URN SED, Samuel explained that the size of the document being hashed must be included in the document itself, requiring modifications to the Cary Pi certify algorithm to ensure proper size computation and prevent cryptographic malleability issues. Carly confirmed that Kent's team is working on implementing these changes. Venu then presented his work on extending Cary into digital asset management, proposing a system using micro-ledgers and UTXO models to handle ownership transfers and obligations, with Nicholas from Keystate Capital expressing interest in potential collaboration on cross-ledger asset transfer protocols.
