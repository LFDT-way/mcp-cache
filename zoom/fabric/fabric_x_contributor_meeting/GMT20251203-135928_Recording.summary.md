## Details

### Fabric X Community Call Launch

The meeting began with Marcus introducing the first Fabric X community call, emphasizing the importance of respectful communication. He outlined the agenda, which included a development update from the AutoX team, a presentation on "Fabric Mix meets EVM" by Arle and Anna, and time for community questions and discussions. Marcus noted that the meeting would be recorded and shared, with the recording automatically uploaded to YouTube for those unable to attend.

### Fabric-X Integration and Endorsement Updates

The team discussed progress on two main epics: reconfiguration and hardening/productizing the order. IL-0HF-720 reported on work with Fabric-X, including endorsement policy updates, moving utility and protocol to FabricX Common, and enabling TLS communication between sidecar and daughter. Marcus shared updates on the endorse report, focusing on upgrading to Committer X version 017 and integrating the notification service feature into the fabric smart client. The conversation ended with an invitation for others to share updates on open source components.

### EVM on Fabric Network Integration

Alessandro presented on a joint project to run EVM smart contracts on Fabric and Fabric-X networks without modification, enabling Ethereum clients to interact with these platforms through a compatible API layer. The architecture includes an adapter that bridges Ethereum and Fabric, using a state database wrapper to manage persistent data and a Fabric connector for endorsement, ordering, and commitment processes. The project aims to provide a migration path between L1s, improve interoperability, and offer high throughput and permissioned system capabilities to Ethereum developers.

### Ethereum-Fabric Transaction Integration Demo

Arne demonstrated a working prototype that integrates Ethereum transactions with Fabric, showcasing the ability to execute transactions, read from the world state, and transfer tokens using a Tether USDT smart contract. He explained the process of deploying smart contracts, executing transactions, and handling multiple transactions simultaneously, highlighting the advantages of Fabric's execute-order-validate platform. The team discussed considerations for gas usage, nonces, identities, and Ethereum logs, with Ali noting that the separation of Fabric and Ethereum identities could be beneficial. They also acknowledged the need to improve concurrency for high-demand accounts and decided to further explore ways to enhance the platform's capabilities.

### EVM Support on Fabric Implementation

The meeting focused on a presentation and discussion about implementing EVM support on Fabric, with a live demo showcasing a prototype. The team discussed handling MVCC conflicts, with IL-0HF-720 suggesting a simpler retry approach instead of complex dependency graphs. Marcus inquired about endorsement policies and state models, with Alessandro explaining that endorsement policies would be set at the namespace level rather than per-function. The team emphasized that while some Ethereum smart contracts might not be compatible with Fabric, their goal is to make it easy for developers to migrate from Ethereum to Fabric. Arne and Alessandro outlined next steps, including an upcoming Fabric-X RFC and a repository for the project, encouraging community participation.
