### Credential Graph Edge Creation Discussion

Drummond asked about why authorization credentials don't create edges in the graph, while delegation credentials do. Geoff explained that delegation requires a two-way handshake similar to power of attorney, while authorization is already implied and doesn't need acceptance. The group discussed whether this distinction should be noted editorially in the specification, with Glenn suggesting implementers would likely ignore non-standard credential types in practice. The conversation concluded with Brendan noting two PRs that are nearly mergeable and asking Drummond about approval or comments.
