## Overview

The meeting covered updates on the Fusaka hard fork and ongoing bug bounty program, along with discussions about deprecating various components including Qlik and proof of work algorithms. The team addressed plans for removing support for pre-London fee markets from Besu and discussed migrating the Besu wiki to GitHub, while also reviewing progress on block-level access lists and project charters. Upcoming events were announced including Linux Foundation elections and a maintainer event in New York, along with updates on the Bonsai Archive with State Proofs PR and new collaboration with Kaleido.

## Details

### Fusaka Hard Fork and Updates

The meeting began with introductions and a reminder about the antitrust notice under the Linux Foundation. Justin announced that Fusaka had been hard forked a few hours ago, and while participation was steady, there was no significant news. He also mentioned an ongoing bug bounty for Fusaka, with reports being managed through the security reporting process. Participants were reminded to direct any bug reports to maintainers for proper triage and escalation.

### Qlik Deprecation and Algorithm Updates

The team discussed the deprecation and removal of Qlik and proof of work algorithms. Fabio and Matthew agreed to remove Qlik, with Matthew suggesting a 6-month notice period and updating documentation. They decided to prepare messaging for the deprecation announcement. Justin agreed to continue work on proof of work, with Matthew offering to reopen the related PR. Fabio introduced the idea of potentially deprecating another algorithm, but the discussion was cut off.

### Pre-London Fee Market Phase-out

The team discussed removing support for pre-London fee markets from the transaction pool and block production in Besu. Matthew noted that while some users still run pre-London chains due to long-running historic chains, removing this support would impact some users and require careful consideration and clear communication of timelines. The team agreed that removing support from the transaction pool might be more palatable than removing it from block production, and Fabio proposed marking it as deprecated while maintaining support for historic transactions.

### Besu Wiki Migration Discussion

The team discussed migrating the Besu wiki from Confluence to GitHub, with Ry presenting an initial export and Justin emphasizing the importance of preserving links and searchability. They debated whether to keep the wiki in a separate repository or integrate it with the main Besu repository, with Luis suggesting a hybrid approach for different types of documentation. Justin announced progress on block-level access lists and encouraged maintainers to approve the project charter, which is still pending approval from some Australian contributors.

### Linux Foundation Event Updates

David announced upcoming events: nominations for the Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust Technical Advisory Council elections opening on October 13th, and an in-person maintainer event in New York on January 26-27. He explained that while the maintainer event would primarily focus on technical collaboration between maintainers, it would also include a Member Summit with potential opportunities for Besu users to meet and provide feedback. Justin expressed interest in gathering user data and feedback about Besu usage, and David confirmed this could be arranged either during the maintainer event or the Member Summit.

### Bonsai Archive Merge Discussion

Matthew discussed the status of the Bonsai Archive with State Proofs PR, aiming to merge it as an experimental option soon. He mentioned testing it against the mainnet for over 10 million blocks and sought help from Gary to move the process along. Matthew also introduced Nick Miano from Kaleido, who will be more involved in Besu and its PRs. Gary agreed to review the archive work and potentially discuss it further on Zoom.
