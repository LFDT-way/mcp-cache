## Details

### Rusaka Mainnet Deployment and Sunset Plan

The LFDT Besu Contributors call discussed the upcoming deployment of Rusaka to mainnet in about 8 days, with all tests passing and everything in good shape. The team reviewed progress on the sunset plan, which includes removing Ethereum Classic support by the end of Q1 2026 and eventually removing the CLIC protocol, with only one known user currently depending on it. The team confirmed that these changes have been communicated and there has been no pushback, allowing them to proceed with the planned deprecations.

### Consensus Mechanism Deprecation Plans

The team discussed plans to deprecate proof-of-work and proof-of-authority consensus mechanisms, with Justin agreeing to add these changes to the next release notes and transition work agenda. Matthew explained that while progress on proof-of-work removal had been slow due to team churn, Sally had taken ownership of the PR, and the team remained committed to the change. The group also explored the possibility of replacing Forest with Bonsai as the default database configuration for mainnet archive nodes, though concerns were raised about Bonsai's suitability for larger archive nodes.

### Forest Mode Deprecation Discussion

The team discussed the potential removal of Forest mode, with Matthew and Gary highlighting that it would be challenging without archived state proofs, as it would leave users without an archive offering proofs. Matthew shared that the state-proof archive mode for the first 10 million Ethereum blocks requires approximately 300 gigabytes of disk space, with the space doubling for 20 million blocks. Gary emphasized the need to compare Forest's performance with Bonsai Archive and state proofs before deprecating it, as Forest supports critical use cases like the Block Explorer. Justin was advised to consult Linea regarding Block Explorer concerns. The conversation ended with a reminder to update the wiki, marking outdated pages for deletion or archiving, and Rai was invited to discuss the export process.

### Confluence Migration and Glamsterdam Planning

Ry discussed the migration of Confluence pages to GitHub, explaining that meeting notes and important pages would be prioritized for export to Markdown format. Justin agreed to review the output and provide feedback, with the goal of making the Confluence wiki read-only by the end of Q1. The team also discussed the Glamsterdam planning, noting that headliners were selected and a stack ranking of other EIPs was created. Justin emphasized the need for timely feedback on the charter, which is still pending approval.

### Besu Repository Migration Discussion

The team discussed the progress of LF Legal reviewing previous changes and the upcoming LFDT Maintainers Days event in New York City on January 26-27, where members were encouraged to suggest Besu presentations. Ry explained the mechanics of migrating to the Besu ETH.org repository, highlighting that it would maintain code, issues, and pull request history while improving role management and cost allocation. Justin expressed interest in initiating the migration at the start of the new year, and Matthew confirmed that Kaleido had no strong opinions on the matter, noting they would monitor for any issues with Maven coordinates.

### New LFDT Member Introduces Collaboration

Jessica introduced herself as a new LFDT member working with the Hyperledger community and proposed connecting with Besu maintainers for potential cross-collaboration opportunities. Justin mentioned that December's meeting might be canceled due to the holidays, and the group exchanged holiday wishes before concluding the call.
