## Details

### Base2 Project Contributor Call

Justin welcomed participants to the June 9th contributor call for the Base2 project and outlined meeting protocols including antitrust policy, recording, and muting requirements. The meeting had four agenda items planned, and Justin specifically welcomed Vivek, who is part of the mentorship program, to the call.

### Besu Migration to GitHub Wiki

Justin announced a migration from Confluence to GitHub-hosted wiki for the Besu project, explaining that while all Confluence content remains available in archive mode, new contributions should be made through pull requests to the GitHub wiki repository rather than directly editing the generated wiki. He clarified that the GitHub wiki uses a flat structure with specific curation requirements that differ from Confluence's approach. Ameziane inquired about the migration status and assistance with moving important content, to which Justin offered to help through the issue tracking system if team members need assistance with the migration process.

### Confluence Wiki and Contribution Guidelines

The team discussed the status of the old Confluence wiki, with David clarifying that it will remain available and links will continue to work, so no action is needed from contributors like Ameziane. Justin raised concerns about updating the contribution guidelines to address the increased volume of pull requests and discussed potential improvements including setting higher quality standards and possibly implementing automated testing. The group also debated the use of co-authored-by tags for AI-assisted contributions, with Matthew and Gary discussing different perspectives on transparency and the need for more nuanced categorization of AI involvement in code submissions.

### Agentic Contributions Tracking System

Justin and Gary discussed the need for identifying agentic contributions in their codebase, focusing on creating a platform-agnostic approach that encourages agents to self-report their contributions. They proposed developing an "agents.markdown" directive to guide agents in providing accurate information about their contributions. The discussion also touched on the potential for future conversations about what directions to enshrine in their codebase, particularly regarding the use of tools like Claude.

### Contributor Guidelines and Branding Updates

The team discussed contributor guidelines, with Gary suggesting a low-friction approach to compliance until a formal policy is established. Justin announced that documentation is now available at docs.baseoeth.org as part of the de-branding initiative, with the old URL basie.hyperledger.org still functioning but requiring future deprecation planning. David committed to following up with Rye about updating Twitter branding and mentioned he would flag the documentation update during their upcoming one-on-one meeting.

### Mempool Encryption Team Initiative

Justin announced a team initiative to encrypt the mempool, which he noted was a pet project of his and would include Basu's involvement. He mentioned that a social announcement about the project would be made on the 11th and invited team members to reach out to him privately if they had concerns. The conversation ended with Justin welcoming Vivek to the team and indicating the next meeting would be in a month.
