## Overview

The meeting focused on updates regarding blockchain implementations in telecom projects, particularly the Hyperledger Fabric integration in the Linux Foundation 5G core project and plans for deployments and research papers. David and Vipin discussed strategies to attract more contributors and promote the work through various channels, including blog posts, virtual meetups, and conference attendance. They explored plans for an upcoming event at MWC Barcelona, emphasized the growing importance of blockchain technology in 6G standards, and agreed to collaborate on publishing articles and promoting the work through various platforms.

## Details

### Hyperledger Fabric 5G Implementation

Vipin shared updates on recent work, including the publication of a block and plans for deployments and research papers. He highlighted an implementation of Hyperledger Fabric in the Linux Foundation 5G core project and discussed the importance of having reference implementations for telecom standards, particularly in 5G. Vipin mentioned that they are in the process of submitting their work to Hyperledger labs to attract more contributors and advance the project.

### LFDT Site Engagement Strategies

David offered support to attract more people to the LFDT site by suggesting blog posts and virtual meetups. Vipin discussed a mentee's question about attending the MWC in Barcelona and sought David's opinion on the possibility of attending, emphasizing its importance as a telecom event to showcase their blockchain deployment.

### Mentorship Program and Travel Support

David and Vipin discussed the mentorship program, noting that Min manages it and approves travel requests. They mentioned that stipends will be offered to mentees this year instead of travel support, which was found less effective. Vipin expressed support for mentees attending conferences to learn and contribute to the community with new ideas. They also discussed pushing code to the lab to enhance the project, which is important for testbeds and benchmarking results.

### Open Source Lab Contribution Strategy

Vipin discussed the process of pushing open-source code to a lab, which David explained involves following the same steps as before. David suggested introducing the new lab to the community through virtual meetups and promoting it on YouTube. Vipin mentioned they are upgrading the code and plan to contribute it to 6G and other efforts. David encouraged Vipin to propose the lab whenever they are ready.

### MWC Event Launch Planning

David and Vipin discussed plans for an upcoming event, potentially launching it at MWC with proper codes and white papers targeting telco players. Vipin mentioned that Canonical has agreed to sponsor tickets, and he is exploring travel support from the Linux Foundation. David offered to help with the meetup and labs process, and Vipin expressed the need for promotion from LFDT to reach the right telcos for the MWC launch.

### 6G Blockchain Integration Progress

Vipin explained that blockchain technology is now integrated into 6G standards, which was not the case in 5G, and highlighted the importance of promoting this development. David suggested reaching out to Daniela, the executive director, to discuss the potential for increased engagement from telecom providers, as blockchain is now more relevant to their needs. Vipin agreed to create and share an article about 6G blockchain standards with David next week for publication.

### Blockchain in 6G and 5G Advanced

David and Vipin discussed the importance of blockchain in 6G and 5G Advanced, highlighting its inclusion in standards like Release 18 and 19. Vipin planned to update their website and connect with standard bodies, while David offered support for hosting labs, meetups, and travel. They agreed to publish an article on the LFDT blog, with Vipin or their mentee drafting it for Emily's review. Vipin also committed to sharing relevant standards in PDF format without requiring a login.

### Blockchain and 5G Collaboration Discussion

David expressed his willingness to support and engage with the team going forward, emphasizing his availability through email or Discord. Vipin highlighted the importance of taking action and planning effectively, mentioning the growing influence of blockchain technology in the telecommunications industry. Both agreed on the progress made in standardizing blockchain technology, which Vipin noted was a significant development in 5G and across telcos. They concluded by agreeing to share an article and maintaining communication for further collaboration.
