### GitMesh Data Optimization Challenges

Ryan and Parv discussed challenges with GitMesh, a project that collects signals from various sources like Reddit, X, and Slack. They identified that the current data collection is too random and noisy, making it difficult to manage. Ryan shared that he had previously implemented algorithms to classify and structure the data, but these solutions were too resource-intensive for most users. They agreed that the project needs optimization to make it more accessible and valuable to contributors, with a focus on integrating team-relevant tools like email, HubSpot, and Jira.

They agreed to focus on creating a well-structured project by the deadline, as the coding itself can be completed relatively quickly once the concept is established.
