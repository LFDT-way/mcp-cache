## Details

### Planning and Feedback Review Session

Art and Katariina discussed the timing of their meeting, noting the challenges for participants in Asia due to the early time. They planned to review feedback and have a working session together, regardless of whether others joined the call. The meeting was being recorded for those who could not attend.

### PDP.P Community Governance Changes

The first fortnightly PDP.P community call discussed governance changes, with public comments on the standard closing October 1st. Art explained that governance was split into two phases: operating rules currently under review and a proposed future governance structure. The discussion covered conformance measurement through self-checks and verified status, with plans for both interim and long-term technical committees to handle discretion requirements.

### Legal Structure for Program Implementation

Art explained that the legal structure for the program has been a topic of discussion, with options including immediate definition of the legal entity or allowing the elected committee to determine it later. He recommended maintaining the current lab structure within PDP-Connect at the Linux Foundation during interim phases to enable conformance certification, with discussions about incorporation potentially happening in Q1 next year. This approach allows the program to move forward with national implementations of PDPβ while the long-term legal structure is being determined.

### PDPifornia Governance Structure Discussion

Art and Katariina discussed the governance structure for PDPifornia, focusing on membership, legal entities, and the involvement of governments and regulators. Art proposed keeping the program free in its first iteration, redirecting any financial commitments to LFDT, and deferring legal obligations until the steering committee is seated. They agreed that governments and regulators could indicate support but should not have voting rights in the initial phase, with more detailed rules to be determined later.

### PDP Supporter Program Overview

Art explained that the supporter program was designed to quickly build community support for PDP principles around data portability, optimizing for simplicity and speed rather than technical compliance. He clarified that while some might argue the requirements are too easy, the low barrier allows everyday people to indicate support for user-owned internet and user-controlled data, while encouraging companies to participate without requiring technical implementation. Art proposed that a public statement of intent should be sufficient for companies to register as supporters without giving them voting rights in governance decisions.

### PDPβ Strategy and Implementation Plan

Art explained that while smaller to middle-tier companies would likely support PDPβ, large tech platforms are more cautious about data sharing as they view it as part of their competitive advantage. Art proposed focusing initial efforts on securing support from individuals and smaller companies to demonstrate groundswell support before approaching larger platforms. He suggested creating momentum by bringing smaller companies into the initiative first, which could then be used to persuade larger companies to align with the PDPβ position.

### Five-Seat Steering Committee Proposal

Art explained the reasoning behind proposing a five-seat steering committee with one vote per organization, aiming to ensure proportional representation and amplify smaller voices in the standard development process. He noted that the voting mechanics, such as preferential voting, still need to be designed once there is a formal membership structure in place.

### Committee Separation and Term Limits

Art proposed a solution to address concerns about power concentration by implementing a separation of powers between the steering committee and technical committee, preventing members from serving on both. He suggested adding a removal function where a simple majority of partners could remove any committee member or chair, including implementing a 1-year term limit. Art noted that while this addresses potential issues, the specific implementation would need to be included in the bylaws.

### Product Certification Governance Structure

Art explained the governance structure for product certification, proposing that the technical committee would independently assess conformance and make recommendations to the steering committee, which could overrule with a supermajority vote. He outlined a compliance monitoring system involving annual reviews for most members and quarterly reviews for operators, along with a requirement for operators to make contractual commitments to maintain conformance and notify PDP-Connect of any compliance issues. Art also established that any detected issues would result in immediate removal from the conformant register with a cure period for re-registration.

### Data Portability Standard Review

Art explained that the rapid pace of the data portability standard development is driven by regulatory pressures from countries like the Dominican Republic and Korea, which have legal requirements for data portability implementation. He noted that Varna has been successfully implementing a PDP-conformant framework since 2024 and emphasized the importance of meeting the demand for the standard. Art requested community members to review the standard at pdpp.dev, provide comments by October 1st, and consider joining the core team or attending future planning meetings.
