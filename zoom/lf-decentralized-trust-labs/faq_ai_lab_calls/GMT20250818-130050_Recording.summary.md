### AI FAQ Caching System Implementation

Ryan reports that the multi-agent system with fast vector storage and basic retrieval for AI FAQ is working properly. He proposes implementing a caching system to store previously answered questions that users have validated as correct, which would reduce token usage and costs while preventing hallucinations. Peter questions how the system would determine if questions are the same, to which Ryan explains they can use cosine similarity of vector embeddings. Ryan also mentions he has renamed "beetle" to "gitmesh" as requested by Rye Jones and is currently working on integrating AI FAQ into it.

### Gitmesh Website Development and Branding

Ryan explains that the name change from Beetle to Gitmesh was suggested by Rye Jones because "beetle" sounds like a product or company name, which the Linux Foundation cannot promote, while "git" is an open-source term and "mesh" refers to a network. Bobbi shares her struggles with the website, having moved from WordPress to Weebly through Pair Hosting after consulting with Gianluca. She has published a basic version of the site and is seeking help with graphics and branding, planning to work with Daria on this.
