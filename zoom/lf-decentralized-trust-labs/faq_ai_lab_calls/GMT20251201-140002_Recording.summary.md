This is a regular call for the AIFAQ lab and anyone who is interested is welcome to join.

The scope of the lab is to use Large Language Models to support the LF Decentralized Trust users (users, developer, etc.) with their work, avoiding to wade through oceans of documents to find information they are looking for.
