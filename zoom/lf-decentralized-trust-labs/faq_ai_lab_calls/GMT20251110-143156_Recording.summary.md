### AI Training Costs and Privacy

Gianluca and Bobbi discussed the challenges and costs associated with training AI models, particularly for sensitive data like medical information. Gianluca explained that to ensure privacy, data should be processed locally on a machine or server with a GPU, which is more expensive than using a CPU. He emphasized that companies in critical areas like medicine or insurance can afford these costs, while smaller businesses might not have the budget. They also touched on the use of APIs for data processing, noting that while they are currently popular, they may become expensive in the future.
