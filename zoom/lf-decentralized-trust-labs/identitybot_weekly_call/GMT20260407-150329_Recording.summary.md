## Details

### IdentityBot Project Architecture Update

Robert provided an update on the IdentityBot project, focusing on architectural decisions made over the past two weeks. He explained the need for a port manager to handle instances of Identity Agents, including managing ports and killing previous application versions to ensure proper functionality. Robert also mentioned the addition of sub-ports for protocols like the carry protocol and authentication manager.

### Blind Watcher Service Concept

Robert discussed a concept for a "blind watcher" service that would function as a hardware-backed, black box monitor within a secure environment. The service would observe and validate root AIDs associated with relationship AIDs, aiming to enhance privacy by issuing new AIDs for each contact and interaction rather than using a single root AID that could lead to de-anonymization.

### Blind Watcher System Implementation Update

Robert discussed the implementation of a blind watcher system to validate credentials between root AIDs and newly spawned relationship AIDs without revealing the root AID. He explained that while the system appears to work well in theory, it still needs to be implemented in practice through a server. Robert also provided an update on the project's progress, noting that they have completed 9 out of 39 milestones and he is currently working on architecting the remaining 30 milestones.
