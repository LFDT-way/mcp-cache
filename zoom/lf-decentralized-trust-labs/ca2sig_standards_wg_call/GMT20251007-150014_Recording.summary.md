## Agenda

The biweekly Standards Working Group has been defining a set of ontologies that enables the description of anthropogenic impact following W3C semantic web guidelines. 
The public release of four interrelated ontologies in OWL, JSON-LD and TTL format is planned for the third quarter of 2025. 
We foresee the use of the ontologies in data models, MRV systems, and AI-assisted applications.
Anyone is welcome to join.
The call will take place every other Tuesday at 8 AM Pacific Time.
More about the Standards group is at: https://wiki.hyperledger.org/display/CASIG/Standards+WG
