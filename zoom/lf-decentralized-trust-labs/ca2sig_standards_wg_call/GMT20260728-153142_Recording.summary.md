## Agenda

The biweekly Standards Working Group has been defining a set of ontologies that enables the description of anthropogenic impact following W3C semantic web guidelines. 
The public release of four interrelated ontologies in OWL, JSON-LD and TTL format is planned for the third quarter of 2025. 
We foresee the use of the ontologies in data models, MRV systems, and AI-assisted applications.
Anyone is welcome to join.
The call will take place every other Tuesday at 8 AM Pacific Time.
More about the Standards group is at: https://wiki.hyperledger.org/display/CASIG/Standards+WG

The team discussed feedback about logo usage for their lab, with Alex explaining that Mint prefers labs not to have their own logos, except when they were previously projects that migrated back to lab status. ### LFX Crowdfunding Discussion

Christiaan and Alfonso discussed LFX crowdfunding, where Alfonso explained that it's a curated collection of projects managed by LFX, with donations flowing through LFX and distributed based on performance metrics. They reviewed the current funding landscape, noting significant contributions from organizations like Google and Cloud Native. The discussion also covered the use of GitHub epics for project management and budget planning, with Alfonso confirming they had worked on setting up epics and budget over the past two weeks. ### Antitrust and Open Source Discussion

The team discussed concerns about antitrust issues related to a crowdfunding project within LFX and the distinction between open source projects and commercial products. Alfonso clarified that Linux Foundation policies allow for separate project and product structures, with the company incorporation being for commercial use while maintaining open source community contributions. The group agreed to separate the business planning discussions from the community call and schedule a separate meeting to discuss commercial details, with Christiaan taking the lead on drafting a blog post about becoming an LFDT lab focusing on how impact accounting commons contributes to decentralized trust.
