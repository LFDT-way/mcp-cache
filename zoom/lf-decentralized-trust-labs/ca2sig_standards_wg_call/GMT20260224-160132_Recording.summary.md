## Agenda

The biweekly Standards Working Group has been defining a set of ontologies that enables the description of anthropogenic impact following W3C semantic web guidelines. 
The public release of four interrelated ontologies in OWL, JSON-LD and TTL format is planned for the third quarter of 2025. 
We foresee the use of the ontologies in data models, MRV systems, and AI-assisted applications.
Anyone is welcome to join.
The call will take place every other Tuesday at 8 AM Pacific Time.
More about the Standards group is at: https://wiki.hyperledger.org/display/CASIG/Standards+WG

Alex updated the spreadsheet by removing two existing columns and adding two new ones: one for ontology versions and another for the LLM used in annotation. Christiaan confirmed that much of the theoretical exposition in the wiki was written by Alex, and Alfonso expressed frustration with Confluence's system, which he feels confuses authorship with ownership and allows for unclear responsibility in edits.

### Wiki Content Restructuring and Updates

The team reviewed and restructured their wiki content, consolidating duplicate entries and organizing sections into courses, news, events, presentations, and summaries. They noted that a recent presentation had 72 views on YouTube and discussed adding a press release about the Linux Foundation's blog post from last year regarding the Ontology Suite. ### AI Annotates Impact Assessment Data

The team discussed using AI tools, specifically ChatGPT, to automatically annotate and structure impact assessment data using the AYA ontology. Alex demonstrated a successful implementation where ChatGPT created a complete JSON-LD representation of a solar energy project in Turkey's national grid, including project activities, states, agents, and claims. The team agreed to focus on getting the labs implementation going first, with Christiaan committing to review and approve pull requests in the morning. Alfonso suggested developing a learning use case to showcase this AI application for AYA, potentially for presentation at LF AI and Data.
